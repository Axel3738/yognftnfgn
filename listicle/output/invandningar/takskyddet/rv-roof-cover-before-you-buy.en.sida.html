4. Doesn't a cover trap moisture? It can. Here is what to do about it.

A few of you asked whether a cover on a closed-up roof means condensation and mold, and you're right to ask. The fabric is waterproof, and a waterproof sheet lying flat on a roof doesn't move much air underneath. We're not going to tell you it breathes. What we're doing about it: a spacer that lifts the cover off the roof and lets air move under it is in the works. There's no date and no price yet, so it isn't on this page as a promise. Until it exists, lay something under the cover that keeps a gap, a couple of pool noodles or a strip of foam along the middle of the roof, so air can move. And give the roof a dry day before the cover goes on, not a wet one.
