## Rules
- Price exactly 799 kr (compare-at 1 039 kr). Never invented urgency or "thousands of customers".
- No store name, no URL, no logo. No free shipping, no delivery time.
- Never show or claim reaching the roof of an SUV, van or motorhome — the pole is 100 cm. Never "halkfri", "reptålig" or "skonar lacken".
- Text set directly on the 4:5 canvas, not as a caption overlay added after export.
- Export: JPG or PNG, 4:5 (1080×1350) and 1:1 (1080×1080).
