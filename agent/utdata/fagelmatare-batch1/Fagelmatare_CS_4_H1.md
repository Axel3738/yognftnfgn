# Fagelmatare_CS_4_H1
**Make:** Clean product beauty shots — exterior, camera opening in the house, solar panel on the roof — while the price and the exact saving appear as bold on-screen numbers. End on the phone with the app open.
**Format:** Video 9:16 + 4:5, 20–25 s
**Why:** The price-anchor angle (list price vs. sale price) is the strongest-performing angle across the whole ad account and should be tested early on every new product.

## Rules
- Price exactly 1 659 kr (compare-at 2 159 kr). Never invented urgency or "thousands of customers".
- No store name, no URL, no logo. No free shipping, Klarna, returns or delivery time.
- Never claim AI species recognition, two-way audio, IP66, cloud storage, memory card or battery life. Say "the app", never an app name.
- Product in frame before second 4. Swedish captions word for word.
- Export: MP4, 9:16 (1080×1920) and 4:5 (1080×1350), captions burned in.
