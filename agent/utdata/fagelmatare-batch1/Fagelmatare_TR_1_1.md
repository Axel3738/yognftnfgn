## Rules
- Price exactly 1 659 kr (compare-at 2 159 kr). Never invented urgency or "thousands of customers".
- No store name, no URL, no logo. No free shipping, Klarna, returns or delivery time.
- Never claim AI species recognition, two-way audio, IP66, cloud storage, memory card or battery life. Say "the app", never an app name. This ad's whole point is stating that limitation honestly — do not soften it.
- Text set directly on the 4:5 canvas, not as a caption overlay added after export.
- Export: JPG or PNG, 4:5 (1080×1350) and 1:1 (1080×1080).
