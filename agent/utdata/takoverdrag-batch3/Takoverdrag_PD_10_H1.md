Keep pacing and camera style identical to the original; this is a variable swap, not a new concept.
**Format:** Video 9:16 + 4:5, 20–25 s
**Why:** The most common silent objection is "it probably doesn't fit my caravan" — that objection can be closed in the hook itself, before the viewer scrolls past.

## Rules
- Price always written "från 1 129 kr" — never a single price alone, since the 13.5 m size costs 2 239 kr. Nine sizes exactly, never ten or thirteen.
- No store name, no URL, no logo. No free shipping, Klarna, returns or delivery time — store policy differs between the two stores.
- Product in frame before second 4. Swedish captions word for word.
- Export: MP4, 9:16 and 4:5, H.264.
