# Takoverdrag_TR_3_H1
**Make:** Straight-to-camera, deadpan delivery — the caravan owner (or a plain narrator) states flatly that this cover only protects the roof, not the whole caravan, before explaining why that's actually the point: one person can fit it alone, nothing rubs against the paint all winter. No hype tone, no ad-voice — it should sound like someone being honest about a limitation.
**Format:** Video 9:16 + 4:5, 20–25 s
**Why:** An ad that opens with its own limitation reads as honest and can't be signed by a full-cover competitor — that same claim would undercut their own product.
