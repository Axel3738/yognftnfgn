# Takoverdrag_GT_10_H1
**Make:** Pan across the gifted caravan's gear that the owner already has — awning, leveling blocks, gas heater, whatever reads well on camera — building a portrait of "he's already got everything for the caravan" before landing on the bare, unprotected roof as the one gap. Then show the cover being placed as the gift. Warm, observational tone, like someone who knows this person well — not a generic "gift idea" ad voice.
**Format:** Video 9:16 + 4:5, 20–25 s
**Why:** The gift buyer recognizes the recipient faster through his garage and gear than through his personality — same mechanism that made GT_2_H1 the account's fourth-best profit contributor, applied to a concrete inventory instead of a character description.
