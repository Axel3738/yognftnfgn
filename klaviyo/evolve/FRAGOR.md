# Frågorna till Evolve-boten

Axel klistrar in en fråga i taget i Evolve-botens chatt och klistrar tillbaka svaret
till Claude. Svaren sparas i `SVAR.md`. Frågorna är på engelska eftersom kursen är
det, och vår situation står i varje fråga så att boten inte svarar generellt.

---

**Fråga 1: vilka flöden först**

```
I'm a total beginner at email. My store: Swedish general store selling niche products (boat covers, caravan roof covers, surveillance cameras, fishing rod holders, tools). ~75 orders/day, AOV ~$70, 8,400 customers, 6,200 subscribed to email. Only 3% of buyers ever buy again — people come from a Meta ad for ONE niche product. Klaviyo account is empty: no flows, no signup forms, never sent a campaign.

Which flows should I build first, in what order, and for each flow: how many emails, what timing, and what should each email do? What does the course/community say the best-performing version of each flow looks like?
```

**Fråga 2: övergiven kassa och webbhistorik**

```
For abandoned checkout and browse abandonment flows: what timing and number of emails does the community use? Discount in them or no discount? Plain-text style vs designed email? Any examples of what the best ones say?
```

**Fråga 3: köpare som bara köper en gång**

```
My buyers are one-product buyers from ads (a boat motor cover, a caravan roof cover). Repeat rate is 3%. What post-purchase and winback flows actually get a second purchase in a store like that? Cross-sell by category? What timing? We have an existing offer: buy again over ~$28 and get a free gift (spin-the-wheel).
```

**Fråga 4: kampanjer och uppvärmning**

```
How often should I send campaigns with a 6,200 subscriber list that has never received a campaign, on a brand new sending domain? How do I warm it up without landing in spam? Which segment first?
```

**Fråga 5: hur ett bra mejl ser ut**

```
What does a high-converting campaign email look like according to the course/community: length, design vs plain text, one product vs many, how to write the subject line and preview text? How do I turn my winning Meta ads (proven hooks and angles) into emails?
```

**Fråga 6: creative strategy för mejl**

```
How do I run the Evolve creative strategy loop (hypothesis per ad, learnings per ad, iteration/imitation/ideation) on email campaigns? What should I test first, how long do I wait before judging, and what numbers do I judge on with click-only attribution?
```

**Fråga 7: popup och välkomsterbjudande**

```
My site has no popup. In Sweden, what popup setup and welcome offer does the community recommend (discount %, free gift, no offer)? AliaLearn vs Optimonk for a store doing ~$150k/month?
```

**Fråga 8: vem som gör jobbet**

```
Billy says to build email in-house with a designer + retention strategist. At my size (~$150k/month), should I first build the routine with AI (Claude) and then hand it to a VA from the Philippines? What should that person's job be, and what training does the community recommend?
```

## Omgång 2, 2026-09-26: återkommande kunder i en general store

Axels fråga: "hur vi kan optimera flowsen mer eftersom vi har en general store. Jag vill ha
återkommande kunder". Siffrorna i frågorna kommer ur `ATERKOP-ANALYS.md`. En fråga per
meddelande, klistra in svaren i `SVAR.md`.

**Fråga 9: general store och andra köpet**

```
I run a Swedish general store (~40 unrelated products: boat covers, caravan roof covers, garden tools, fishing gear). Only 3.3% of buyers ever order twice, and 2.4% buy again within 30 days. What do Evolve members with general stores (not single-niche brands) do to get the second order? Flows, new products to the same buyers, or something else? Any real examples with numbers?
```

**Fråga 10: korsförsälja efter köparens typ**

```
In a general store, buyers are "owner types": boat owners, caravan owners, garden owners. Is it better to cross-sell other products for the same owner type (boat cover buyer → boat products), or send everyone the store bestseller? How do members group products for this, and how many emails after the first purchase?
```

**Fråga 11: timing från leverans**

```
Our packages take 7-14 days to arrive (shipped from China). Do members trigger post-purchase and cross-sell flows on "delivered" or on "order placed"? How many days after delivery does the first cross-sell email work best?
```

**Fråga 12: erbjudandet för köp nummer två**

```
AOV is about 500 SEK (~$50). For the second purchase, what converts best: store credit, a free gift with the next order, or a % code? Grayson mentioned store credit gets used ~30% of the time. How was it set up, and in which email did it go out?
```

**Fråga 13: vinback utan naturlig återköpscykel**

```
Our products are not consumables, so there is no natural repurchase cycle. When should a win-back flow start for a general store, and what should the win-back emails offer or say?
```

**Fråga 14: kampanjer till köparlistan**

```
My subscribed list is ~6,000, mostly past buyers. For repeat purchases, how many campaigns per week do general stores send, and what works: "new product this week" drops, seasonal bundles, or bestseller emails? What repeat rate after 90 days would members call good for a general store?
```
