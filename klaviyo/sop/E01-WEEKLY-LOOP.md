# E01 — The weekly loop

Every campaign is a test with one idea. We read the result, write what we learned,
and build the next emails on it. Without the "learned" step we are just sending
emails, and the list gets tired for nothing.

**The rule that sets the pace:** no more new campaign ideas in a week than
lessons written the week before (at most 5). Week 1 gets 3.

---

## The week

| # | When | Step | Who now | Who later |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monday morning | **Read results** of every email that is now 7 days or older (E04). Claude runs the report | Claude | You read the numbers and flag alarms; Claude writes the lesson |
| 2 | Monday | **Write one lesson per email** in the campaign log (`klaviyo/logg/baverbutiken/kampanjlogg.md`) | Claude | Claude |
| 3 | Monday | **Pick 2–3 ideas** from the brief queue, each with a source (a winning ad, a customer objection, an earlier email's lesson) | Claude | Claude |
| 4 | Monday–Tuesday | **Write the copy** (three subject lines, preview text, blocks) | Claude | Claude |
| 5 | Tuesday | **Build** and **upload as drafts** to Klaviyo. Claude posts the gallery link | Claude | Claude |
| 6 | Tuesday | **QA** every draft (E03) | Claude + owner | **You** |
| 7 | Tuesday | **Approval**: the owner says OK per campaign | Owner | Owner, always |
| 8 | Tuesday–Wednesday | **Set up A/B and schedule** on the planned time (E02) | Owner | **You**, after the OK |
| 9 | Every day, 5 minutes | **Flows and list health** (E05, E06) | Claude (in the report) | **You** |

## Your steps, in order, once you have them

1. Monday: open the report link Claude posts. Read E04. Write in the escalation
   channel: which emails are too early, and any alarm.
2. Tuesday: open each draft Claude uploaded. Run E03 top to bottom.
3. Post "QA green" or the problems you found, one line per campaign.
4. Wait for the owner's written OK **for that campaign by name**.
5. Set up and schedule it (E02). Post "Scheduled: <campaign name>, <day> <time>".
6. Every day: E05 and E06, five minutes.

## How a step moves from Claude to you

A step moves only when Claude has done it right **three weeks in a row**: QA
green, no alarm, on time, and no correction from the owner. Then you do it for two
weeks while Claude checks your work. Then it is yours. The owner keeps the table of
which steps are yours.

## Definition of done (each week)

- [ ] Every email 7+ days old has a lesson in the campaign log (or is marked too early)
- [ ] New ideas this week ≤ lessons written, and ≤ 5
- [ ] Every new campaign has a memo and a source
- [ ] Every draft passed E03
- [ ] Every scheduled campaign has the owner's written OK by name
- [ ] Flows and list health checked every day, alarms escalated
