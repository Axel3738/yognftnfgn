# Matstrumpor: the delivery delay email, and what you do with the replies

*Written 2026-10-03. NOT sent yet: this applies from the day the owner turns the email on.*

## What the customers get

An apology email from Matstrumpor, in the customer's own language:

- **Who:** everyone who bought from 23 September, plus every new order for six days after the
  email goes out (a flow sends it one hour after the order).
- **What it says:** delivery can take **5-10 business days longer than usual** because of a
  disruption in the supply chain. Nothing more is said about the cause, so do not add details
  in your replies either.
- **The offer:** if the parcel **has not been shipped yet**, the customer can get a **full
  refund** by replying to the email with their order number. Customers who want to keep the
  order do not need to do anything.

Replies land in kundsupport@matstrumpor.se like every other email. The auto-reply bot flags
refund requests to you (it never refunds anything itself).

## What you do with a refund request

1. Find the order in Shopify (order number, or the customer's email address).
2. **Not fulfilled yet** (no tracking number, status Unfulfilled): cancel the order with a
   **full refund**, including shipping, and tell the customer it is done. No questions, no
   counter-offer, no partial refund.
3. **Already fulfilled** (it has a tracking number): the full-refund offer does not apply. Reply
   kindly that the parcel is already on its way, give them the tracking page link and their
   parcel number (starts with **MS-**), and say it can take 5-10 business days longer than
   usual. If they still want to return it when it arrives, the normal return policy applies.
4. No order number and you cannot find the order from the email address: ask for the order
   number once.

Never promise a delivery date, and never promise a refund date.

## Other replies

- "Where is my parcel?" Same as always: the tracking page. Add that it can take 5-10 business
  days longer than usual right now.
- Angry or a chargeback threat: handle as usual, and mention the full-refund offer if the parcel
  has not shipped.
