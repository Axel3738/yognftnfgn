# How to use the Growth Guide

For Bruce. Matstrumpor. Our mirror of Evolve's Growth Guide 3.0: https://www.notion.so/3ed270ab908c80a3beb7e0090eebce5d

## The tabs

- **Overview**: the hit rate, written by the system every round. Raising it is the goal.
- **Ad Roadmap**: one row per concept (batch), added before it is briefed.
- **Log**: one row per day the system did something. You write **NOTES**.
- **Desires**, **Sub Avatars**, **Avatars**, **Awareness**, **Creators**: planning, seeded from research, then yours.
- **Ad Results**: one row per ad. Numbers only, system only.

## Ad Roadmap: who writes what

- **The system**, every round: BATCH #, DATE ADDED, AUTHOR, FILE TYPE, AD TYPE, links, RESULTS, numbers. It seeds DESIRE, SUB AVATAR, ANGLE(S), BREAKTHROUGH MEMO, AWARENESS and LEARNINGS once, where empty.
- **You**: STATUS, UPVOTE (the rows you believe in most) and LEARNINGS.
- **STATUS**: Working (planned or being cut) → Learning (live, has RESULTS) → Done (LEARNINGS written, or "Too little data"). Filming = waiting for raw clips.
- Seeded text ends with "(end of seed)": write UNDER it. The system never overwrites a person. Never edit the "System data" toggle or rename a column.

## Your week

- **Monday 15:00 Manila**: the one-page Monday check Axel sent you. Then add ONE new row for next week's test, before it is briefed.
- **Tuesday 09:00 Manila**: feedback as a Log row like "2026-W41 Weekly review", a comment tagging you, ONE action item. Reply in NOTES.
- Axel is only tagged if the Monday check is skipped two weeks in a row, or a winner waits two weeks without a live iteration.
