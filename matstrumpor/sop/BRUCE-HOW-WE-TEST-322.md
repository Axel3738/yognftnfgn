# How we test ads: 3:2:2

## The setup and the test

- One campaign. **Champions** holds proven winners, moved in by Axel; new ads never go there. Beside it at most 4 test ad sets. All full? A new concept waits.
- **One concept = one test ad set = three ads**: one body, hooks h1, h2, h3, each with 2 headlines and 2 primary texts. Never image and video together.
- Normally 7 days (3 to 14), judged per **ad set**, never per ad. Winner: most spend at KPI (break-even), or a better campaign return; its best ad goes to Champions at a fifth of the spend. Ads that work stay on.
- Not your clicks: the uploader builds ad sets; winners and budgets are Axel's.

## Labels per ad after 7 days, and what to make next

- 🏆 **Breakthrough**: big share at KPI, campaign grew. Iterate: longer problem part, awareness up or down, new hooks, new formats. Never a plain copy.
- 💸 **Spend winner**: big share, but under KPI or no growth. Fix what loses the sale: hook to hold, script, what is missing. Do not rebuild.
- 🎯 **KPI winner** (beat the campaign's return, little spend) and ❌ **Loser**: find the execution error. Iterate only research ideas, never a lost imitation.
- ⚪ **No delivery**: barely shown, not judged. Labels only go UP in weeks 2 and 3. Under 300 kr or 3 purchases: "Too little data". Hit rate = 🏆 + 💸 ÷ labelled ads.
- Three tries with a result, none beats the parent: drop the concept.

## Your brief, per concept

- ONE brief: hook table H1 to H3 (Swedish line, English meaning) and a COPY CARD (Primary text 1 and 2, Headline 1 and 2).
- Three rows in **Matstrumpor creative hub**, one per hook: Draft, then To be Reviewed with the file. New filming: no row before the raw clips are in Drive.
- No store name in ads. Only sushi. Numbers exactly as their source writes them.
