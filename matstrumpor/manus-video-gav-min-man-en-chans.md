## Editor brief (English)

9:16 UGC talking-head, warm and self-aware, not over-smiley — proud but a
little sheepish. Beat 1 (0–3s): she's in her kitchen or living room, straight
to camera, mid-thought energy. Beat 2 (3–10s): cut to her holding the package
her husband brought home — no shopping bag, just the package on the table.
Beat 3 (10–18s, THE HEART OF THE AD): slow, deliberate unwrap, piece by
piece — the tray reads as a sushi platter at first glance, then resolves into
socks. Give this beat the most screen time and a clean macro shot of the
double-take. Beat 4 (18–25s): reaction shot around a table, then a
socks-on-feet shot proving real use. Beat 5 (25–30s): package under the
Christmas tree, deadline text overlay. Music: warm light acoustic, gentle lift
at the reveal, no dramatic stings. Cuts snappy, not frantic. No price on
screen, ever.
