# Meddelandet till leverantören: lägre pris per låda (2026-09-30)

Axels beställning samma dag. Talen i texten är mätta i Shopify 2026-09-30:
141 ordrar 28–30/9 × 2,12 lådor ≈ 100 lådor/dag; december 2025 1 613 ordrar.
Shopifys Cost per item: 5-pack 80,23 kr. Svaret skrivs in i Shopify (Cost per item)
och `matstrumpor/cogs.json`, och räknaren `node matstrumpor/erbjudanden/ekonomi.mjs`
körs om.

---

Hi [Name],

Sales of the sushi socks are growing fast. We are now shipping around 100 boxes a day,
and last December we had over 1,600 orders. We expect Q4 this year to be bigger, and we
want to plan the volume with you now.

To grow with you, we need a better price. Could you:

1. **Lower the price for the 5-pair sushi box to 65 SEK per box** (about 5.70 USD),
   delivered to Sweden, instead of today's 80 SEK?
2. **Quote a price for 2, 4 and 6 boxes in the same parcel.** More customers will buy
   4 boxes, so a good multi-box price helps us send you bigger orders.
3. **Tell us about customs.** Is the customs fee (about 3 EUR) charged per parcel or per
   box? Are the wooden chopsticks declared as a separate item?
4. **Confirm the timing for December.** How many boxes can you ship per day in
   November and December, and when do we need to order stock so we do not sell out like
   last November?

If 65 SEK is not possible, please send your best price and what volume it needs.

Thank you, looking forward to a big Q4 together.

Best regards,
Axel
STONEBITE ECOM AB
