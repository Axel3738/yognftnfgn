--- slide 6: HOW TO HANDLE DEFECT ISSUES WITH THE MANUFACTURER? // Be upfront about defect expectations. Ask the manufacturer for data regarding their current returns. Create a report from your CS team that can clearly track  defects, share this with the manufacturer on at least a monthly basis. Hone in on the specific issue you’d like resolved. If the issues are large enough, use your order as leverage until they fix the problem. Negotiate an overshipping plan where the factory will overstock your next order by the % of defects on your last order to keep you ahead of the game.
