--- slide 3: FACTORIES // Raw Material Factories:  Produce raw materials for assembly  Assembly Factories:  Responsibile for final assembly and quality control Completion of final packaging
--- slide 4: ADVANTAGES // DISADVANTAGES // Quality control can be difficult to manage on your own Specialize in a few products vs Offering a wide range  Better to work with a group of factories vs just one factory Low volume ordering makes you less of a priority Communication challenges, especially with small and medium size factories. Less supply chain transparency They only offer new products that they plan to produce, it may not be relevant to the brand needs Example: IPL Hair Removal Factory also produces Hair Dryers. Now their sending new hair dryers they’re designing to an IPL hair removal brand. // Direct source Lower price higher MOQs Collaborate on product creation or product customization Different size factories have different advantages: Small Eager for business  Medium Better pricing and resources Large Extensive resources High capcity // FACTORIES
--- slide 5: TRADE COMPANIES // An office setup that essentially acts as factory sales arm. They don’t manufacture anything. They will often partner with factories to help develop sales channels like Ali Express or working with dropshipping agents. Commonly found on Alibaba
--- slide 6: ADVANTAGES // DISADVANTAGES // TRADE COMPANIES // Offers more product variety for specific categories Has better quality inspection services than factories in some cases Less communication issues than working with factories Some factories prefer to work exclusively with trade companies Easier to work with if you can only buy low volume. Many Ali Express vendors are trade companies. // Price is more expensive than Factories. Incentivized to keep price high. Less transparency with profit margins. They are more of a reseller. On many occasions, they pose as factories on Alibaba, which can be misleading Usually work in small circles of suppliers to control  pricing For product customization, they can take longer due to an extra layer of communication. QC issues can take longer to resolve Many dropshipping Agents source products from trade companies. They will only suggest new products within their network of manufacturers and their own vertical.
--- slide 7: DROPSHIPPING AGENTS // A warehouse setup—sometimes with an office— fulfilling and shipping a wide variety of products. They sometimes help source products or have products available on demand They can partner with trading companies and factories to help with fulfillment and sales of their products.
--- slide 8: DROPSHIPPING AGENTS // ADVANTAGES // DISADVANTAGES // They can both source and fulfill products for ecommerce businesses under one roof They can source products at lower MOQs They can source in many product categories . Product sourcing lead times can be faster when they source existing inventory from trade companies  They can get you pricing for products quickly  Quick online research   Pull prices from suppliers’ online stores (kind of a disadvantage) They can provide more product pricing options than a trade company // Ultimate goal is to source products quickly so they can ship, leading to a sacrifice in quality They may not be sourcing directly from factories and sourcing from trade companies instead There is zero transparency on pricing. They will pad margins into the product price and the shipping price They stay away from doing customization for brands They don’t always work with compliant factories (FDA certificates, UL or CE certificates.) Trademarks or Patents can be messy due to a lack of due diligence in their sourcing process Not product professionals. They are shipping professionals.  Limited purchase planning knowledge. Limited knowledge in optimizing the defect rates. They are not going to pass negotiated prices down.  Typically like sourcing for companies so that they can have full control over your supply chain.
--- slide 9: SOURCING COMPANIES // Operate with a research team in a broad network of factories. Helps develop products and services with your company. Set up manufacturing and shipping solutions.
--- slide 10: ADVANTAGES // DISADVANTAGES // Over-relying on your Sourcing agent because they control communication with the supplier. It's a big change from the dropshipping model Working with a bad sourcing company can be the same as working with a trade company. Reselling Rates vs having transparent service pricing.  They may have a poorer understanding of your brand and types of products you’re trying to offer. Cost involved in using a sourcing company (time saving and product price/quality can offset the cost) *Many sourcing companies have “hidden” prices. They offer 5%-10% purchasing fees, but they are making an additional 10-20+% commission from the factory (look at turn over & number of clients). // SOURCING COMPANIES // Strong Due Diligence Capabilities; Product Manufacturing Research that specializes in a variety of industries.  Interests are aligned - functions as a representative of the brand Saves brand owners’ time. Can problem-solve on behalf of the brand. Having the capability to negotiate pricing and payment terms on behalf of the brand.  Better Quality Control - Visits the factory on behalf of the client to make sure they are not cutting corners.  Sourcing Companies may be able to offer better payment terms than the factory. Has the capability to help brands find new products that solve defects or return problems from older products they sell.  Can help brands develop a product portfolio.
