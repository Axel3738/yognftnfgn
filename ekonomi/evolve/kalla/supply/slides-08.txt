--- slide 4: TACTILE SURFACE FINISHES YOU CAN USE TO ELEVATE TO YOUR PRODUCTS // There is a wide variety of finishing techniques that can be used on any product: Electroplating - adds a metallic shine and feel to plastic (real metal coating) Paint finishes - can add a similar look but not the same feel as electroplating, however colors are more open here Sandblasting or polishing - whether you want a gritty grippy feel or a smooth to the tough surface, these are all customizable options  Logo treatments - branding is everything. How will you apply your logos to the product? Laser engraving, silk screen or 3D printing, foil hits and UV are all options
--- slide 5: COMPONENT UPGRADES TO AIDE IN MARKETING A PRODUCT AS SUPERIOR TO THE COMPETITORS’ // Identify your budget, then work backwards Understand the room you have to upgrade components while staying in cost per unit budget  Things that can be upgraded: Batteries, capacity or power output Active ingredients for increased affect or earlier results  Key issues that competitors might have This can really help with marketing if you are solving a problem that other brands are experiencing Charging speed Scents and textures for skin care or supplements
--- slide 6: ADDING A TRAVEL-READY ELEMENT TO YOUR PRODUCT FOR GREATER PERCIEVED VALUE // Consider packaging your product in a reusable travel friendly package Small travel case Spaces for all things needed such as chargers or extra spaces for parts to complete a skin care routine  Something branded  Something the end user does not want to throw away Create a travel bundle Bundle certain items from your site into a travel package Run a limited time free travel case promotion to move the items  The idea is to add value to the packaging itself. If the consumer can get value out of the packaging, that is a huge selling point compared to competitors that don’t add that value
