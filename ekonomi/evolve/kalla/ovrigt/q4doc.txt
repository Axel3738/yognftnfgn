Ad Creative Management During Sale Phases:


         * The rule: you never have to turn off ads with a WORSE offer than what is live on the site. You DO have to turn off ads with a BETTER offer than what is live on the site.

         * Example: your pre-Black Friday creatives say 20% off and they are still performing. The offer moves to 25%. Leave them on. You still launch the 25% creatives alongside them. Nobody gets mad about clicking a 20% ad and landing on a site that says 25% or 30%.

         * The failure: ads running at 30% while the site says 20%. That is a congruence break. It tanks conversion rate, creates CS tickets, and burns trust with the exact people who were about to buy.

         * When to audit: the offer only steps DOWN once all Q4, from 30% (BFCM) to 25% (Holiday Sale) on Tuesday, December 1st. 

That is the day every 30% creative gets paused before the Holiday Sale goes live. 
Every other transition steps UP, so old creatives can stay live as long as they perform.


            * The same logic applies to landing pages, emails, and pop-ups. Whatever the ad promises, the site must meet or beat it.

Evolve Backend


Your backend is going to be the single most important thing and the biggest failure point during Q4


Every year we see the same pattern of chad scalers going hard scaling, then finding out they don’t have enough stock.

Stock does not magically stock infinitely. If you don’t know your own stock capacity it can go very wrong, leading to massive chargebacks that causing your store that had huge potential to shut down.


Here’s supply chain tips for dummies


IF dropshipping:


            1. You’d need to get you agent to check:
            1. Whether their factories can handle the extra Q4 volume even with other customer orders factored in
            2. Current capacity % of the factory
            3. Current agent warehouse capacity %
            2. Even if you’re working with a dropshipping agent you need to know these, and it’s highly recommended to pay a visit to them this month so you don’t get screwed by the factory or 3PL (agent). Smaller agents have higher risk of their own fulfillment centers not being able to keep up with fulfillment volume.
            3. You may need to buy some stock just in case to secure allocation. This is advisable for our December push anyways (covered below)


IF direct factory:


            1. What do your forecasts look like in comparison to the stock you have? If you don’t have a solid forecast with Q4 uplift in mind, you can get very screwed.
            2. To build a solid supply chain internally you need to do 2 things:
            1. Get better credit terms with your factory. You can utilise chinese gov backed loans like Sinosure. Here’s the credit negotiation guide: https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/992407ee?md=f1919fad5e2649a2b0ad3d3d89db5aed
            2. And to reduce lead time. This can be done multiple ways once you understand what is required to produce your product
            1. For supplements creating the label and bottles takes more time (~30 day lead time) than filling the supplement (~14 day lead time). So prepurchasing a lot more bottles than needed drops your lead time in half
            2. Buy the raw material upfront with the factory at 25% cost, and have that sitting. This will cut production time in half, as they don't need to wait for raw materials to be made. You pay the remaining when they finalize the order. 
            3. So the ideal scenario for your backend at any point is to look like this: Instead of regular NET30 terms, you secure NET90 with the factory due to negotiation/sinosure, whatever. Instead of only buying 2-3 months worth of stock, you can buy 5-6 months of stock for the same upfront capital, and due to larger order you may be able to get lower prices. This way if you sell too many too fast you got extra stock to over
            4. And because your lead times are reduced, if your stock is moving faster than forecasted you can get new ones produced way quicker to cover
