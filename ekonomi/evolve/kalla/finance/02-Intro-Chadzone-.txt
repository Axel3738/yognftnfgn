Quick Intro 

What’s up guys — I’m pumped you’re here.

This module is going to cover the stuff that actually matters if you want to scale without blowing yourself up: LTV, contribution margin, and the cash conversion cycle (aka when money leaves your bank account vs. when it comes back).

If we haven’t met yet, my name is Grayson. I’ve been in e-commerce for the last 10 years, generated over $2B in e-commerce revenue, and I’m currently managing over $100M/month in subscription LTV revenue.

And here’s the punchline:

Most e-commerce people tell you, “You’ve gotta lose money in month one, month two, and you’ll make it back later.”

Cool story.

I’m going to show you how to run your ad account at a 0.5 ROAS and still make more profit than you’re making right now — without playing the “we’ll be profitable later” game.

Even better: I’ll show you how to set this up so you can scale while keeping control of cashflow, so money doesn’t have to leave your bank account in the way people think it does.

This training is broken into a few short videos. The first one is simple and foundational, and then each video stacks on the last and gets more advanced. If you follow it in order, it’ll click.
