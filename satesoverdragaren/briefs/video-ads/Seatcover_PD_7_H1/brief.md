# Seatcover_PD_7_H1 — Mechanism-led: "Därför spricker ditt säte"

**Type:** New concept (FAS 8) — mechanism-led persuasion (why the problem happens + why this fixes it)
**Format:** 30s explainer-demo · 9:16 + 4:5 · burned-in Swedish captions
**Production level:** Medium (tabletop material demo + supplier b-roll; no creator required)
**Primary KPI:** CPA (< 314 kr) · diagnosis: hold p50/3s ≥ 25% (mechanism content must retain, or it's just a lecture)

## Hypothesis
All spent ads argue outcome (dry, comfy, cheap). None argue **mechanism**. Educating "sun UV + rain is what kills the foam cushion — a 600D barrier stops both" gives problem-aware owners a reason to act *before* the seat cracks, expanding beyond the "already ruined seat" audience. Variable vs winner: **persuasion mechanism**; format discipline (length, captions) copies winning DNA.

## Exact hook (0–3s)
Extreme close-up of cracked seat foam crumbling under a thumb + caption line 1. (Source: frame from PD_2 before/after master, or shoot a junk seat cushion.)

## Creator direction
Hands-only shoot. Clean outdoor light. The water-pour demo (shot 5) is the hero — rehearse so beading is clearly visible; wipe cover dry between takes.

## Editing direction
- Captions word-for-word, winner style. No shot > 4s.
- Optional calm VO reading the captions; video must work muted.
- Sound: subtle water foley on the pour demo.
- "649 kr" plain text; no strikethrough graphics.
