HOW TO BUILD IT

  • Look: stop-motion claymation — visibly hand-made clay, fingerprints, slightly stepped motion (12 fps feel), warm light plus the tree lights. Think Aardman, not Pixar. The joke is in the faces, and the faces are the whole ad since there is no voice.
  • Cast: four figures, same design in every shot. Dad (jacket, the bag, the "open" signal), mum (doorway, then beside dad), two teens with different hair and colours. Make a character sheet first — front, side, four expressions each: hungry, "what is this?", "huh?", laughing — and reference it in every beat, or the faces drift.
  • Props: white paper takeaway bag with wooden chopsticks poking out; TWO sushi boxes exactly like the real product — reference the photos on matstrumpor.se/products/sushi-strumpor. The rolls must read as maki until one is unrolled. Never one box, never three.
  • Workflow: image first, motion second — stills per beat from the character sheet, then image-to-video in 3–4 s clips. Never let the model render text; all lines and the CTA box are burned in afterwards. Keep text inside the middle 80 % of the frame.
  • The three faces that carry the ad: the "what is this?" look between the teens (hold a full second), the "huh?" that breaks into laughter, and mum and dad's eyebrows-up smile. Spend the generation budget there, not on the door or the bag.
  • Files: MATSTRUMP_sushi_gift_anim_026h1_v1 · _026h2_v1 · _026h3_v1. AI-generated → the Meta ad must be created with the AI disclosure switched ON; it cannot be added afterwards.

RULES (NOT SUGGESTIONS)

  • Swedish on screen exactly as written, including "dom". English is never on screen. No voice-over, no dialogue.
  • The text says "såg ut som" (looked like) — never that it IS sushi, never that anyone was fooled. The kids' expectation is the picture's job.
  • Reactions are actions in picture. The text never says laughed, got happy, smiled.
  • Teenagers, never small children: the socks are one size 36–44 and a small foot in a sock is a false size claim. Size, material, stretch, durability and price never appear in text or picture.
  • The parents are the heroes: they bought the boxes, they get the last shot.
  • Christmas lives in the picture only (the tree). No Christmas Eve, no wrapped presents, no Santa, no set table. The text never says jul, julklapp, december. Run window: mid-November to 20 December.
  • The offer is written exactly "Köp 1 Få 1", only in the CTA box. No percent, no price, no "rea".
  • No brand words in the lines ("matstrumpor", "sushistrumpor") — the brand lives in the CTA box.
  • Do not add lines to the two text-free beats. If a line falls apart in the edit, say so — it gets rewritten, never improvised.
