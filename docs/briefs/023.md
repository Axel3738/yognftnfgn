## How to use this brief

- Pick **one body per creator**. A creator records one body with all three of its hooks (the hook is the first 3 seconds; the rest is identical). Never mix hooks across bodies.
- File naming follows the account convention: `MATSTRUMP_sushi_gift_ugc_023ah1_v1` (body A, hook 1) … `023ch3_v1`.
- Beats run in this order and are not reordered: 1 hook (0–3 s) → 2 the conversation (3–8 s) → 3 the turn: what I could have bought instead (8–14 s) → 4 the box that looks like sushi, the moment they realise you remembered, then socks (14–22 s) → 5 proof / identity (22–28 s) → 6 CTA (28–35 s).

## Creator instructions (read before filming)

- **The remark must actually have been said.** Swap the remark, the place and the relation to your real one (the car, the sushi place, the lunch are examples) but keep the structure: said in passing, everyone moved on, I remembered. Never invent the remark. Name the recipient by relation in beat 2 (my brother, my son, my colleague — or your own), never "the recipient".
- **The alternative gift** (headphones, sneakers, the bouquet) is swapped to what you actually considered, or what the office actually bought. The word "expensive" is never said — the object carries the comparison. No amounts.
- **Beat 4 is filmed as it happened.** The lines ("Sushi?" / "Did I say that?" / "The car, I said") are examples of how it went, not lines to act. If something else happened, say that. No staged silence, no waiting for a look, no counted pause.
- Body B, beat 4: "Sa jag det?" means he did not remember the remark himself. It must land as *you remembered*, never as *I never said that*.
- **The validation must have happened** (mum wanted a pair · the box on his story · the boss asked where I found them). If it didn't: use the one that did, or cut it and keep only the socks being worn afterwards. Never an invented quote, never a third person talking like a review.
- "Så ja" is only in body C, beat 5. A and B have no written transition — say your own or go straight to the next sentence.
- Say "looks like sushi" / "a sushi box" — never that the socks *are* sushi, never that anyone was fooled.
- Christmas is mentioned only in body A, beat 4 ("på julafton"). If the gift is given on another occasion, change the word and the version becomes season-neutral. Body A's hooks carry no time markers.
- The offer is called **Köp 1 Få 1**, said exactly so and only in beat 6. Nothing else about the offer — no numbers, no other words, in speech or on screen.
- The socks are never described: not how they're made, not what feet they fit. The chopsticks are visible in the box but never described in speech.
- The self-irony must be true for you (forgot his birthday · "I'll take it" · wrote it down · I sit closest · I don't listen to everything). If it isn't, replace it with your own.
- Any AI-generated material must be reported to Axel.

# D. Sanningsvillkor

- Repliken ska ha sagts. Kreatören byter replik, plats och relation till sitt verkliga (bilen, sushistället, lunchen är exempel) men behåller strukturen: sagt i förbigående, alla gick vidare, jag kom ihåg. Repliken hittas aldrig på. Mottagaren heter alltid sin relation i beat 2 (brorsan, sonen, kollegan — eller kreatörens egen), aldrig "mottagaren".
- Det andra alternativet (hörlurar, sneakers, bukett) byts mot det kreatören faktiskt övervägde eller kontoret faktiskt köpte. Ordet "dyr" sägs aldrig — objektet bär jämförelsen. Inga belopp nämns. Vet kreatören inte vad kontoret gick ihop om byts buketten i C-hook 2 och beat 3 i samma svep, annars är jämförelsen påhittad.
- Reaktionen vid öppningen filmas som den blev. "Sushi?" / "Sa jag det?", "Bilen, sa jag", pekandet, skrattet och rullen är givarens egen berättelse, inte ett förskrivet manus — hände något annat, säg det. Noten under varje body gäller före tabellens rad 4. Ingen tillverkad tystnad: ingen blick som väntas in, ingen paus som räknas.
- B beat 4: "Sa jag det?" betyder att han inte mindes repliken själv. Det ska landa som "du kom ihåg", aldrig som "jag sa aldrig det". Minns han den själv, säg det i stället.
- Valideringen ska ha hänt: mamma som ville ha ett par, sushilådan på hans story, chefen som frågade. Hände det inte: byt till den som hände, eller stryk och behåll bara strumporna på fötterna efteråt. Till Body B: har kreatören en tredje person som frågade (kompis, syskon, den andra föräldern) är den starkare än storyn — ta den.
