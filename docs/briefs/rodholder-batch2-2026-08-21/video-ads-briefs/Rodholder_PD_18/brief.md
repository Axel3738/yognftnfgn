## Source & hypothesis

**Real VoC** (owner's research among fishing friends, 2026-08-21): anglers use hair ties (and rubber bands) as the hack to keep collapsed rods together — and they describe it as constant hassle. This is the account's first genuine customer-language insight for the product. Hypothesis: naming and showing the exact hack people already do ("slipp hårsnoddarna") creates recognition the generic tangle-pain can't — the viewer sees THEIR OWN workaround on screen. Sibling of `Rodholder_PD_8` (Trasselbollen): same structure, different pain-object. Isolated variable: **the enemy** (hair tie hack vs tangle ball).
