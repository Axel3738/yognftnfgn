# Rodholder_PD_5_H1 — "När det hugger" situation demo

**Type:** Video · **Production level:** medium (staged boat scene or assembled from supplier + stock-free b-roll of own filming) · **Tier 1**

## Hypothesis

The winning copy sells storage-tangle pain. The sister-product page uses a sharper moment: *the strike is exactly when tangle hurts most*. Hypothesis: an in-the-moment situation hook (fish strikes, rods tangled) out-hooks the storage angle. Isolated variable: **the hook situation** (strike vs storage) — demo mechanics and price framing unchanged.

## Format & structure

9:16 + 4:5, 15–22 s, works muted, captions max 2 lines.

| Beat | Time | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 0–3 s | Rod tip jerks (strike!) — hand reaches for second rod, it is tangled into the third. REAL footage, hands/rods only |
| PROBLEM | 3–7 s | Close-up: lines wrapped, lure hooked into another line |
| DEMO | 7–14 s | Clamp on: each rod closed and parked; hand grabs ONE rod cleanly |
| PROOF | 14–18 s | The 4-pack laid out on boat bench |
| CTA | 18–22 s | End frame, price 289 kr |
