# Rodholder_PD_9_H1 — "Garageväggen" (before/after wall storage)

**Type:** Video · **Production level:** medium (one location: garage/shed wall) · **Tier 2**

## Hypothesis

Use-case 2 (screw to wall, one rod per clamp) has never had budget: the old CS static showed the wall but its copy sold a discount. A competitor (wall-mounted rod rack) runs this angle in SE — the demand exists and nobody owns it. Hypothesis: before/after transformation of a messy rod corner opens the storage segment. Isolated variable: **use case** (wall storage vs boat tangle).

## Format & structure

9:16 + 4:5, 15–20 s, works muted.

| Beat | Time | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK (before) | 0–3 s | Rods leaning in a corner, slid into each other, one falls |
| PROBLEM | 3–6 s | Hand tries to pull ONE rod out — two follow, lines snag |
| DEMO | 6–13 s | Four clamps screwed in a row (timelapse ok), rods clicked in one by one |
| AFTER | 13–17 s | Locked shot: four rods vertical, evenly spaced, floor clear |
| CTA | 17–20 s | End frame 289 kr |
