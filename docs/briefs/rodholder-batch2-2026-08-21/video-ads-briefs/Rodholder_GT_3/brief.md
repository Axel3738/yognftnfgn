# Rodholder_GT_3_H1 — Gift for the angler

**Type:** Video · **Production level:** low-medium · **Tier 3 — do not launch before Tier 1–2 are live; ideally hold for gifting season (Father's Day / Christmas)**

## Hypothesis

⚠️ **Marked as a GUESS in the test plan** — the GT concept got 27 kr of CBO spend in batch #1 (no data), and no competitor runs a gift angle for this category (could mean gap, could mean no demand). Hypothesis: the buyer-for-someone-else segment (partner/family of an angler) responds to a low-risk 289 kr gift with obvious daily use. Isolated variable: **audience/frame** (gift-giver vs self-buyer).

## Format & structure

9:16 + 4:5, 15–20 s, works muted.

| Beat | Time | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 0–3 s | The 4-pack being wrapped / ribbon tied around it — real hands, real product |
| WHY IT LANDS | 3–8 s | Cut to the angler's world: rods in boat, clamp clicked on |
| PROOF | 8–13 s | Wall row with 4 rods — "he uses it every trip" visual |
| TRUST | 13–16 s | Guarantee caption |
| CTA | 16–20 s | 289 kr end frame |

## Direction

Creator: real product, real wrapping, hands only — **no AI people** (the old GT static's AI product renders are the cautionary example, see image-README reference list). Editing: warm but quick; the product must appear unwrapped by second 3. CTA: SHOP NOW.

## KPI & learning

Primary KPI: profit contribution; diagnosis: CTR (does the gift frame stop a different scroller?) + CVR (do gift-buyers actually check out?). **We learn regardless:** whether a gifting segment exists for this product before we spend season budget on it.
