# Rodholder_PD_8_H1 + Rodholder_PD_8_H2 — "Trasselbollen" (the tangle ball)

**Type:** Video (two ads: same film, two hooks) · **Production level:** medium (one macro prop shot + demo footage) · **Tier 2**

## Hypothesis

Our account's hook rule: the first second must show one real, physical, slightly disgusting object — never a person. The tangle ball (a fist-sized knot of lines, lures and swivels pulled out of a boat locker) is that object for this product. Hypothesis: a pain-object hook stops the scroll harder than product-in-use. Isolated variable: **hook object** (tangle ball vs clamp demo). H1 vs H2 = different first line over the same shot.
