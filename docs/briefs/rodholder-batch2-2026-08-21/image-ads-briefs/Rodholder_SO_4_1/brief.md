# Rodholder_SO_4_1 — Listicle static ("4 hållare, 4 platser")

**Type:** Static · **Production level:** medium (4-cell collage) · **Tier 2**

## Hypothesis

A competitor's modular-rack framing signals that buyers think in "how many rods / where". The pack quantity (4) maps perfectly onto four usage places. Hypothesis: a 4-cell listicle showing boat / car / storage / travel widens perceived value beyond one use case. Isolated variable: **value framing** (multi-place utility vs single demo).

## Design

- Headline top. 2×2 grid of REAL photos of clamped-together rod packages: (1) in the boat, (2) in the car trunk, (3) in storage/shed, (4) carried in one hand for travel. NO mounting anywhere. Each cell gets its short label (table below) as a chip.
- Footer strip: price line. Product visible in every cell. No AI environments — request footage if a cell is missing; do not fake it.
