# Rodholder_PD_10_1 — Risk / cost-of-inaction static

**Type:** Static · **Production level:** low-medium · **Tier 2**

## Hypothesis

"Protect the investment" is a proven angle family in our playbook (the account's grill pivot). Anglers own expensive rods; tangle wear (line friction, snagged lures, rods slammed together) is the slow damage. Hypothesis: pointing at the reader's own most expensive rod reframes 289 kr as protection, not storage. Isolated variable: **persuasion mechanism** (loss aversion vs convenience).

## Design

- Scene: ONE premium-looking rod (real) lying across a boat bench, a clamp closed on it, macro on the contact point — the clamp visually "guarding" the rod. Muted background, headline in the empty zone.
- Headline = the question (biggest). Subline points to the clamp. Footer: price.
- Do NOT state any rod price, brand, or damage statistic — the reader supplies their own number. **No AI gear.**
