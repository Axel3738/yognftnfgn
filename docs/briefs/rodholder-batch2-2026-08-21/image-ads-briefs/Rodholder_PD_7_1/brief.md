**Type:** Static · **Production level:** low-medium (two-panel photo) · **Tier 1**

## Hypothesis

Before/after is the simplest conflict and a proven pattern in our playbook. Hypothesis: the tangle (before) next to the neatly clamped-together rods (after) tells the whole story at scroll speed. Isolated variable: **visual persuasion mechanism** (contrast vs single demo scene).

## Design

- Split layout, vertical divider. LEFT "UTAN": real tangle — rods crossed, lines wrapped, a lure snagged (make it genuinely messy, use real gear). RIGHT "MED": same rods, each closed with a clamp, laid parallel as neat two-piece packages.
- Label chips top of each panel (text from table below). Headline across the top, footer strip bottom with price.
- Same lighting both panels — the ONLY difference is order vs chaos. **No AI-generated gear.**
