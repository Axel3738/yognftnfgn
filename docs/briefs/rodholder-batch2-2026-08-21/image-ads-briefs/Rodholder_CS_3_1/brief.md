# Rodholder_CS_3_1 — True offer static (replaces the false "40 % RABATT")

**Type:** Static · **Production level:** low · **Tier 1**

## Hypothesis

The retired discount ad converted at demo-level CPA (143.80 kr) but its 40 %-claim was false and is now banned. Hypothesis: the offer-responsive audience converts on the TRUE price mechanics — 289 kr for 4, ~72 kr per clamp, 30-day guarantee — without any fake urgency. Isolated variable: **honest offer vs (retired) fake discount vs no offer**.

## Design

- Hero: the 4-pack laid out flat, all four clamps visible and countable (use `../reference-assets/PRODUCT_*` for product truth; re-shoot style of `DO_NOT_REUSE_SO_2_1_*` layout is fine — the LAYOUT was good, the burned-in old price was the problem).
- Headline = the price mechanic (biggest). Subline under. Footer: guarantee + Klarna.
- **Absolutely no**: struck-through prices, percentages, countdowns, "IDAG ENDAST".
