## Why this ad exists

**Sources:**
- **VoC category 1** (~35 voices, the largest cluster): the trunk is the root
  problem. Rods bend and bounce among cooler boxes and gear; the fear that
  "something's going to break one day" is in their own words.
- **`Rodholder_PD_6_1`**: statics convert well on this product (7.27 %
  purchase-per-click, 13.2 % ATC/LPV).
- Before/after is the simplest form of conflict per `docs/copy-regler.md`
  ("Konflikt driver allt") and needs no explanation to read.

## Design

- Split canvas: **left = before, right = after** (or top/bottom for 1080×1350).
- **Before:** two rod halves lying loose in a real trunk, crossing each other,
  among actual gear — cooler box, bag, tackle. It should look like a real car,
  not a set.
- **After:** the same rod, same trunk, same framing — now clamped into one tidy
  package lying flat.
- **Identical camera position and lighting in both halves.** If the framing moves,
  the comparison stops being evidence and becomes decoration.
- Real photographs. No CGI trunk, no AI-generated interior. If the footage does
  not exist, request it — do not fake it.
- No wall, no mounting anywhere in either half.
- Export 1:1 and 1080×1350.
