## Why this ad exists

**Source:** VoC category 2 + 5. Two findings meet here:
- Hooks and lures **damaging car seats** is named explicitly; people detach hooks
  and fasten the leader against the rod as a workaround (category 2).
- People keep a rod **permanently rigged and ready in the car** so they can fish
  after work (category 5, ~8 voices). A cheap four-piece travel rod is described
  as standing "permanently rigged as first choice".

The insight this brief is built on: **we don't manage the hook — we remove the
reason it swings.** Two loose halves flap; a clamped package doesn't. That is an
honest, demonstrable claim, and it opens the spontaneous-fishing angle that has
been sitting in the backlog since 2026-08-24 without ever getting budget.

## Shooting rules

- A genuinely **rigged** rod — line threaded, lure or hook attached.
- A real car interior. The "before" shot must show the hook actually swinging and
  contacting the seat. That shot is the ad — do not fake it or cut it short.
- Do **not** claim the clamp secures the hook or protects the seat directly. The
  claim is: the rod stops moving, so the hook stops moving. Show it, don't overstate it.
- Outdoors / in-car natural light. 12–18 s. 9:16 primary, 4:5 export.
