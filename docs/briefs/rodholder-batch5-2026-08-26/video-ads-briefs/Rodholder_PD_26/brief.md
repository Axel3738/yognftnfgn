## Why this ad exists

This is the **control replication of the account's best-converting creative**.
`CS_1_H1` and `CS_1_H3` ran identical copy, headline, CTA and offer. The only
difference was the video: H1 indoors against a grey sofa, H3 outdoors in a real
fishing setting. Result: **4.49 % vs 10.39 % of clicks became purchases**
(CPA 126 vs 48 SEK). See `products/fiskespohallaren/dna.md`, law 2.

`CS_1_H3` carries claims we cannot stand behind ("40 % RABATT", "IDAG ENDAST")
and is being retired. This ad keeps **the thing that worked — the location —
and replaces the copy with claims that are true.**

**Isolated variable:** honest copy on the proven outdoor format. If this holds
CPA anywhere near 48–126 SEK, the location was the driver and the discount claim
was never needed.

## Shooting rules (non-negotiable)

- Shot **outdoors, at the water** — lake, jetty, riverbank or boat. Real daylight.
- Real two-piece rod, real hands. **No studio, no sofa, no neutral backdrop.**
- Phone camera is fine and preferred. Wind noise, a visible microphone, slightly
  imperfect framing are all **good** — they read as real. Do not clean this up.
- No AI-generated people. Hands only, or a real angler filmed from behind.
- Product visible and in use within the first second.
- 12–18 s. 9:16 (1080×1920) primary, 4:5 export.
