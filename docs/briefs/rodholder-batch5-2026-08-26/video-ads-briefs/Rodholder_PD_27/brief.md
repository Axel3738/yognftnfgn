## Why this ad exists

**Source:** VoC category 1 — the largest cluster in the research (~35 voices,
`docs/voc-reddit-fiskespohallaren-2026-08-24.md`). Transport space is the root
problem: people **choose rods based on their car**. Their own words describe rods
bending and bouncing against cooler boxes, and the fear that "something's going
to break one day".

Combined with law 2 (location beats production value), this puts the winning
format in the exact place the customer feels the problem.

**Isolated variable vs PD_26:** the location (car/trunk) rather than the water.
Same format, same honest copy — so the two together tell us which context sells.

## Shooting rules

- Shot **outdoors at a real car**, tailgate open. Real daylight, real gear in the
  trunk — cooler box, bag, whatever is actually there. Mess is good.
- Real two-piece rod, real hands. **No studio, no sofa, no CGI car.**
- Phone camera. Imperfection reads as real — keep it.
- Product in use within the first second. 12–18 s. 9:16 primary, 4:5 export.
