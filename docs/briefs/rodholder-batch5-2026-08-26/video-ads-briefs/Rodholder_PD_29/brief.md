## Why this ad exists

**Source:** VoC category 2 — fear of gear damage in transit (~25 voices).
Their own problem picture: rods bending against the load, micro-cracks that grow
into breaks, the rod tip named as the most fragile part, reels scraping each
other. People transport expensive rods with extra care and deliberately pick
cheap rods for risky trips.

This is the objection that stops a buyer with a rod they care about. It is also
the one place where our differentiator is a **verified physical fact**: the
high-elastic foam lining. VoC category 3 confirms the contrast — the fixes people
use today (rubber bands, velcro) "slid around and lacked the grip".

⚠️ **Warning against law 1:** macro material shots are exactly the kind of
footage that is satisfying to watch and does not sell (`PD_11_H2`, the account's
only loss-making ad, was a click macro). This ad only earns its place because the
macro **answers a question** rather than showing craftsmanship. Every shot must
serve the answer "no, it does not scratch" — cut anything that is merely pretty.

## Shooting rules

- A visibly **glossy** rod section — the finish must read on screen.
- Show the foam lining clearly before the clamp closes.
- The payoff shot is mandatory: **clamp opened afterwards, same section of rod,
  no mark.** Without it this ad is just a nice macro and will lose money.
- Natural light. No studio sterility. 10–16 s. 9:16 primary, 4:5 export.
