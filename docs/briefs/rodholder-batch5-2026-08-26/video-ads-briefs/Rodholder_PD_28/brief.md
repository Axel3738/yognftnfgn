## Why this ad exists

**Source:** `Rodholder_PD_16_H1` — the same objection, and the best-converting
video in batch #3: **6.33 % of clicks became purchases** against the 2.5–3.7 %
demo baseline, CPA 120 SEK on 5 purchases (preliminary, `dna.md` law 3).

PD_16 was shot as a neutral macro demo. This version keeps the winning idea and
adds law 2: **shoot it outdoors, in a real setting.** That is the isolated
variable — same objection, same comparison, new location.

It also picks up the VoC finding that the customer's current fix fails exactly
here: rubber bands and velcro "slid around and lacked the grip" when rod
thickness changed (category 3, ~28 voices).

## Shooting rules

- Two genuinely different two-piece rods: a thin spinning rod and a visibly
  thicker sea/boat rod. **The size contrast is the entire ad** — if it isn't
  obvious on a phone screen, the shot has failed.
- Same framing for both so the difference reads instantly.
- Outdoors, real light, real hands. No studio.
- 12–18 s. 9:16 primary, 4:5 export.
