# Batch #5 — "QUALIFY, DON'T FASCINATE" (2026-08-26)

**Product:** Fiskespöhållare 4-Pack (fishing rod clamps), 289 SEK, Bäverbutiken.
**14 creatives:** 10 video (5 concepts × 2 hooks) + 4 statics.

---

## What the product actually does — read this first

A two-piece fishing rod is taken apart for transport. The two halves then rattle
around, knock against each other and tangle with lines and hooks in the car, the
boat and the shed. **The clamp squeezes the two halves together into ONE package.**
Click on, carry it like a single rod. Click off at the water.

⛔ The clamps are **NOT mounted** on walls, boats or anything else. The words
*mount / screw / wall* and every mounting claim are forbidden. They fasten rod
parts **to each other**.

## Why this batch exists — the numbers behind it

Read `products/fiskespohallaren/batch-log.md` (Avläsning #4) for the full analysis.
Three findings drive every brief here:

**1. High hold does not sell.** Ads that keep viewers watching longest convert
worst. `PD_15_H1` (the shake test) holds 25 % of viewers — 4× account average —
and has the lowest purchase rate of any big ad (2.60 % of clicks). `PD_11_H2`
(the click macro) holds 17.6 % and is the account's only loss-making ad.
→ **No brief in this batch has "keep the viewer watching" as a goal.**

**2. Location beats production value.** `CS_1_H1` and `CS_1_H3` run **identical
copy, headline, CTA and offer**. The only difference is the video. H1 was shot
indoors against a grey sofa: 4.49 % of clicks buy. H3 was shot outdoors in a real
fishing setting: **10.39 % of clicks buy, CPA 48 SEK** — the best in the account.
→ **Every video in this batch is shot where fishing actually happens.**
Studio, sofa and neutral backdrops are forbidden. A phone camera outdoors beats
a clean studio shot. Visible microphones, wind, imperfect framing are all fine —
they are proof the footage is real.

**3. Objection-crushers convert; product demos don't.** The three highest
purchase rates in the account all answer a question the buyer actually has.
Pure demo clips sit at 2.5–3.7 %.
→ **Every brief below names the objection it kills, at the top. If a brief has
no objection, it does not get produced.**

## Hard rules for every creative in this batch

**Forbidden — these cost money or trust:**
- Any discount or percentage claim. The numbers **149**, **148.75** and **"40 %"**.
  The price is 289 SEK, flat, no compare-at price, no sale.
- Any scarcity claim we cannot verify: *sold out, few left in stock, we're clearing
  the warehouse, while stocks last, today only, price goes up tomorrow.*
  (The current live `CS_1` ads and the `REA_V01–V10` statics contain these.
  They are being replaced — do not copy their language.)
- **"Fri frakt" / free shipping.** The threshold is 300 SEK and the product is
  289 SEK, so a single pack does not qualify. Saying it implies a benefit the
  buyer does not get.
- *"Passar alla spön"* / "fits all rods" → say **delbara spön** (two-piece rods).
- Invented reviews, quotes or numbers. AI-generated people.
- The words mount / screw / wall (see above).

**Verified facts — only these may be claimed:**
- 4 clamps per pack (enough for two rods)
- Locks with one click, opens and closes one-handed
- High-elastic foam inside — grips without scratching the rod
- Built for two-piece (splittable) rods
- 289 SEK, flat price
- 30-day satisfaction guarantee

**Format:** 9:16 (1080×1920) primary + 4:5 export. Video 8–18 s. Captions max 2
lines, must work muted. Statics 1:1 + 1080×1350.

**Copy:** Swedish lines are verbatim — use the ⭐ line, do not translate the
English column into the ad. All copy in this batch was written to
`docs/copy-regler.md` and passed the three-question test (see each brief).
