## GLOBAL RULES — for the editors (English)

These apply to every file in this batch. The brief's own §9 adds to them, never
replaces them.

1. **Price exactly 999 kr** (ord. 1 600 kr as a drawn line, never the word). No
   percentage, no countdown, no second price. Numbers other than the price are
   written in words in Swedish.
2. **The ad never names the store.** Not "Grillkliniken", not the domain, not the
   wordmark. The product is "Mastern".
3. **Never say his grill is dirty or that he is lazy.** The giver praises him; the
   fault is the wire brush that cannot reach the gaps.
4. **First three seconds: ONE real physical object in macro.** Never a person, never
   AI, never the word "present" as the first word.
5. **Risk reversal as the store writes it:** "30 dagars öppet köp", "livstidsgaranti —
   går den sönder får han en ny", "fri frakt inom Sverige". Never "14 dagar", never
   a delivery date (only 317 carries one).
6. **Facts allowed** are listed in every brief's §9. Not allowed anywhere: "34 %",
   "Journal of Food Science", "40 000 grillare", "fem tusen i Norden", any invented
   quote or star rating. Recalls only as "i USA".
7. **Reviews verbatim with the name exactly as on the product page**, or not at all.
8. **Swedish lines word for word.** Captions max 8 words each, inside the middle 80 %
   of the frame. Å, Ä, Ö must render. Do not "fix" the Swedish — comment on the row.
9. **Season:** it is November. Jackets, dusk, terrace lamps, leaves on the cover.
   Summer only as a memory.
10. **Sources:** every direction row names a Drive folder id, a CDN image or "NEW
    FOOTAGE" with who shoots it. Cannot find the source? Comment on the row and set
    it back to Draft — never replace the product shot with a generic one.
11. **Export:** video 9:16 (1080×1920) + 4:5 (1080×1350); statics 4:5 + 1:1, JPG/PNG,
    sRGB, under 30 MB. File names exactly as in the brief's "Deliver as".
12. **Spelling traps:** Mastern · fars dag · stålborste · borststrån · springorna ·
    grovhuvud · polerhuvud · livstidsgaranti · öppet köp · USB-kabel.
