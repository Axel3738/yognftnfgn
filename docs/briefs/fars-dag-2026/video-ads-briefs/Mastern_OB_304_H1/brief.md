## 1. Why this ad exists
Objection ads carry the code OB (naming convention, Axel 2026-09-21). The objection here is the giver's: "he already has a brush, is this thing real?". The emotional act is the doubt itself (no one wants to be the one who gave the gimmick). The functional act is proof you can see: warm grate, wet head, one pass, black flakes, the head dipped in a bowl — the water goes black. Then the build that makes it not a gimmick: the coarse head locks the metal, the polish head has no metal, 2 600 mAh, four to six cleanings per charge. Marcus A. and Lars-Erik H. close it with their own words.

## 2. Hypothesis
A demo that starts from the giver's doubt (OB) converts the doubters that a proud demo never reaches — and the 30-day open purchase is the line that lets a sceptic buy.
**Hook (H1):** Mastern låg oöppnad en vecka. Jag var rädd den skulle hamna i skåpet.
