**Type:** Video, new persuasion angle. **Marked as educated guess** — not
tested for this product yet.
**Hypothesis:** The CS (clearance/discount) ads claim a 23 % discount that
does not exist in Shopify (single 309 kr price, no compare-at, no discount
code — see `dna.md`, flagged as a BLOCKER). Those ads created urgency through
a false discount. This ad rebuilds the same urgency mechanism honestly:
loss-aversion on the CONSEQUENCE (a burst pipe, water damage) instead of a
fake price deadline. **No invented kr-figure is used anywhere in this
script** — we do not have a verified Swedish plumber-callout or repair cost,
and CLAUDE.md rule 3 forbids inventing one. If Axel has a real number, it
strengthens version 2 (see `backlog.md`).
**Isolated variable:** persuasion mechanism — cost-of-inaction/loss-aversion,
vs. the account's existing angles (demo, testimonial, discount, gift).
**Kept:** the real product facts (420D Oxford, insulating lining, 10-second
install, 309 kr, 30-day guarantee). **Changed:** the entire hook and framing.

**Format:** Video, 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** Simple — recut of
existing broll (frozen pipe / tap footage) + new VO.

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-29 teardown)
CS_1_H1/H2/H3 and CS_2_1 are a compliance risk, not just an underperformer:
they advertise "23 % RABATT" and "vinterrean" that do not exist on the
landing page (ARBETSREGLER #7 — offer-integrity BLOCKER). Rather than leave a
gap where the "urgency" angle used to be, this ad tests whether the same
urgency function can be served honestly — timing urgency (the cold snap is
coming) and consequence urgency (what happens if you wait), with zero
discount language.
