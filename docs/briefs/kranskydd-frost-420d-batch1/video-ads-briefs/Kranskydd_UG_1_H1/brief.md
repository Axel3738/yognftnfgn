## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-29 teardown)
The account has never tested a person speaking to camera. Every PD ad uses
the identical spec-sheet script with no human anchor, and every PD ad with
real spend is currently underwater (ROAS <1). This is the cheapest possible
way to isolate whether the missing ingredient is a human voice, not the words.

**Shot list:**
- 0–3 s: creator on camera, unprotected tap visible over their shoulder
  (product-adjacent framing before second 4; the product itself appears at
  line 4/second ~9 — flag this to the editor as the one deviation from the
  "product before second 4" rule, and prioritize getting the product package
  into frame earlier if the shoot allows it, e.g. holding it from second 1).
- 3–6 s (line 3): creator gestures at the bare tap, concerned tone — no
  overacting.
- 6–9 s (line 4): pulls product out of packaging or pocket.
- 9–17 s (lines 5–7): real-time installation, hands in frame, natural pace.
- 17–20 s (line 7 cont.): quick cutaway to the insulating lining (open the
  product briefly to show the inside fabric).
- 20–25 s (line 8): creator back on camera, product now installed visible
  behind them, price stated out loud.
- 25–28 s: end card, price + CTA.

**Creator direction:** real house exterior, daylight, no styling, casual
clothing; genuine unscripted energy — light retakes only for audio clarity,
not for polish. Any gender/age reads fine; a homeowner-coded look (not a
young influencer aesthetic) matches SP_1_H3's "villaägare" positioning.
**Editing direction:** minimal cuts, captions word-for-word, no music under
the spoken lines, keep the installation in one continuous take if possible
(a genuine 10-second install is itself the proof — cutting it away invites
doubt).
**Primary KPI:** hook rate (first 3s) and hold (p50) vs. PD_1_H2/H3's
baseline; secondary CPA vs. 322 kr break-even after ≥300 kr/3 purchases.
**What we learn regardless:** whether a human-delivered demo closes PD's
conversion gap, independent of the words used (which stay almost identical
to the already-tested PD script).
