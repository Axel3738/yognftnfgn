## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-29 teardown)
PD is the account's second-most-spent angle and currently underwater on
video. Before iterating the video again, this tests the cheapest possible
structural change — split composition — on a static, where production cost
is low and the result reads fast (copy-regler.md: "en Mississippi-testet").

- Price + CTA in a clean band at the bottom, no discount badge.
