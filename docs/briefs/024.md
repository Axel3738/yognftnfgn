## Editor instructions

- Use the **original master** — no burned-in caption, no "Tidig Jul Rea!" badge. Strip the old text layers before adding new ones.
- **Badge**, top-left, same place/shape/colour as last year's, on screen 0–26 s. Exact text: **Köp 1 Få 1**. Never "rea", never a percentage, never a price.
- Graphics like the winner: one line on screen at a time, white text with black outline, lower third. CTA line bigger than the rest. The text zone never enters the badge zone.
- Timing is read from a 360×450 copy, one frame per 2 s — time against the master (±0.5 s) with three absolute rules: line 2 cuts in on the **first unroll frame** (~4 s); no line containing "strumpa/strumpor" before a sock is visible; CTA holds at least 4 s.
- Change **only the words** — not the cuts, rhythm, reveals or badge position.
- Deliver **track 1 × 3 hooks** as three files (`024_t1_h1/h2/h3`). Track 2 is the alternative spine — only if Axel picks it (`024_t2_h1/h2/h3`). Never mix hooks between tracks.

## Truth conditions (must hold before launch)

- **Köp 1 Få 1 must be live** on matstrumpor.se for the whole run. Written exactly so in badge and CTA — never a percentage, price, "rea" or "spara".
- Dottern, brorsan, svärmor and "jag" are the ad's narrator persona (same convention as 017 body 2), not customer quotes: no quotation marks, no name, age or town, nobody on screen presented as the customer. If Axel wants it true for himself, swap the relations to his real ones — "Barnbarnet får hela lådan." (4 words) in track 1 line 3; line 4 "Hon" assumes a girl, otherwise "Han nämnde sushi en gång i somras." (7 words).
- "I somras" holds while the ad runs in autumn 2026; after New Year it becomes "i höstas".
- Before the reveal the hooks call the box what it looks like ("sushi", "sushilådan") — the giver's moment, not a product claim; line 2 corrects it on the first unroll frame. No line says anyone was fooled.
- "Tio" is the winner's own line (VO 0:09), the persona's claim — not a customer number. "Tio till på listan" is the Christmas list, not the phone note from line 5. Reserve if Axel wants the spine cleaner: "Hon får sushi. Tio till får sitt." — not swapped in without him asking.
- The wooden chopsticks are in the box (017 product facts) and visible 14–16 s — the only product fact in text. No size, material, price or pair count anywhere.
- Christmas references per combination: track 1 — hook 1 + CTA = 2, hook 2 + CTA = 1, hook 3 + CTA = 2; track 2 — hook 1 + CTA = 1, hook 2 + CTA = 1, hook 3 + CTA = 2. Add none.
- "Tidig julklapp" in the CTA is the only place (22–26 s) where the ad speaks instead of the giver — deliberate. If Axel wants it gone: "matstrumpor.se · Köp 1 Få 1".
- Every line ≤ 7 words; five sit at the cap. No word may be added at timing or line-breaking — break between words only.
- Two-sentence hooks sit on one frame. "Släkten jagar julklappstips." never stands alone (alone it reads as a self-purchase).
- Track 2 hook 1 punctures the illusion before the reveal — a deliberate A/B against track 1 hook 1. Never combined with track 1.
