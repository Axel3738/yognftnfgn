## How to use this brief

- One body, three hooks = three files: `MATSTRUMP_sushi_gift_anim_026h1_v1` … `026h3_v1`. Only the first 3 seconds differ.
- Line 1 (0–3 s) is the hook in H1. In H2 the doorway line replaces it; in H3 the cold open is added before it. Everything from beat 2 onward is identical in all three.
- **Beats 3–6 s and 12–15 s have no text.** The faces are the ad. Do not add a line.
- **Last line rule:** the moving text is "Nu hade dom fler par strumpor." — "matstrumpor.se · Köp 1 Få 1" is a fixed graphic in the CTA box that lights up 0.5 s after the sentence. Never merge them, never split the line.
- Two boxes out of the bag, one per teen. Never one, never three — the CTA explains two.
- AI-generated video → create the ad with Meta's AI disclosure ON. It can't be switched on afterwards.
- Only ONE version of the body is delivered (no two alternative versions as the copy rules normally require) — Axel's call 2026-09-17 to keep the cost down.

## D. Sanningsvillkor

- The boxes are the real product: sushi takeaway boxes with wooden chopsticks on top; the rolls are socks rolled like maki. The text says "såg ut som" — never that it IS sushi, never that anyone was fooled. The kids' expectation is the picture's job.
- TWO boxes come out of the bag, one per teen. That is what Köp 1 Få 1 gives — the offer in the CTA box explains the picture. Never show more boxes than two.
- The kids are teenagers (13–17). The socks are onesize 36–44, so a small child's foot in a sock is a visual claim about a size that doesn't exist. Size, stretch, material, durability and price are never in text or picture. The size range is a casting instruction and never goes on screen.
- The avatar is the giver: the parents bought the boxes, the parents get the last beat (eyebrows, the glance, the smile). Never "a fun thing for myself". The kids are the receivers of the reaction, not the buyers.
- Reactions are actions in picture ("what is this?" look, the laugh, the poses, the parents' eyebrows) — the text never says "skrattade", "blev glada" or "ler". Beats 3–6 s and 12–15 s have no text on purpose; don't add one.
- Christmas lives in the picture only: a lit tree in the background, nothing else festive, and it is a weeknight before Christmas — no Christmas Eve, no wrapped presents, no Santa, no set table. The text never says jul, julklapp, december. Run window: mid-November to 20 December.
- The offer is written exactly "Köp 1 Få 1", only in the CTA box, never in a line. No percent, no price, no "rea". It must be live on matstrumpor.se the day the ad goes live.
- No voice-over, no dialogue, no brand words in the lines ("matstrumpor", "sushistrumpor"); the brand lives in the CTA box only. Not used, because other ads own them: "de har redan allt" (022), "presenten bevisar att jag lyssnade" (023), "hjärnan är tom / ingen aning" (025), "tvättmedel / skämtpryl" (primary text under the video), the headline "Rolig i kväll. På fötterna i morgon." (headline under the video), "på köpet".
- Max 7 words per moving line, one line at a time, at most four lines in the whole film. The last line is the moving text; "matstrumpor.se · Köp 1 Få 1" is a fixed graphic in the CTA box that lights up 0.5 s after the sentence — never merged into one text card, never split into two.
- AI disclosure: this is AI-generated video. The ad creative must be created with Meta's AI disclosure ON (self_ai_disclosure OPT_IN). It cannot be added afterwards — a creative made without it has to be rebuilt.

## E. Förkastade rader (så det syns vad som valdes bort)

- "Vad hade han i påsen?" (H2) — funkar muntligt men är en fråga, inte ett påstående; skrevs om till "Pappa hade nåt i påsen." som säger samma sak deklarativt.
- "Magarna skrek efter middag." (H1) — hyperbol ("skrek") och pekar inte på soffan som syns; "kurrade i soffan" är bokstavligt sant och peka-baserat.
- "Nu är dom bara strumpor." (bild 6, skribentens förslag) — tanken var att nyheten blir något vanligt man använder; huvudsessionen valde bort den eftersom "bara" läses som ett antiklimax över föräldrarnas leende i en tyst feed, och Axels egen formulering ("nu har dom fler par strumpor") landar payoffen utan tvetydighet.
- "Detta hände sen." (H3) — för abstrakt, utan ankare i det som visas; "Så här gick kvällen till slut" pekar på slutbilden i stället för att bara flagga ett tidshopp.
