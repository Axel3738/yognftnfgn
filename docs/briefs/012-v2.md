## Why v2 (context for the editor)

V1 was warm but flat: no twist, no reaction, no laugh — the same calm zone our
biggest competitor already owns. V2 keeps the joy and the family idea, but the
first two seconds now do what this brand does best: look like food, turn out to
be socks.

## Rules

- Slideshow of still images. ~2 s per slide, end card ~2.5 s. Hard cuts. Total 12–15 s.
- **Music on the whole video — required.** Calm, no lyrics, license-safe for Meta ads. You pick the track.
- **You generate the images yourselves (AI).** Every image must be **hyperrealistic** — it has to read as a real phone photo. No cartoon, no gloss, no obvious AI look.
