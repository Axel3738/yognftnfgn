## Rules

- **Format: 9:16 only** (1080×1920).
- Slideshow of still images. ~2 s per slide, end card ~2.5 s. Hard cuts. Total 12–15 s.
- **Music on the whole video — required.** Calm, no lyrics, license-safe for Meta ads. You pick the track.
- **You generate the images yourselves (AI).** Every image must be **hyperrealistic** — it has to read as a real phone photo. No cartoon, no gloss, no obvious AI look.
- The image column below is **inspiration, not instruction** — if you have a better idea for a slide, use it, as long as the Swedish line still matches what's on screen.
- **Product accuracy is the one hard rule:** use the real product photos on matstrumpor.se as reference so the boxes and socks look like the actual product (sushi box with sock "maki" rolls, pizza box, burger box, pink donut box). The ad must show what the customer gets.
- Text: simple white text, subtle shadow/gradient if needed. Keep inside middle 80 % of frame height. The Swedish lines are FINAL COPY — place them exactly, never translate.
- Musts: tick the **AI-content disclosure in Ads Manager** (required, not optional). Never caption anything as customer photos. No Christmas scenes. No claims beyond what's written here.
