## Concept & hypothesis

Second re-cut of `Tofflor_SP_1`'s footage (same source as `Ergoslippers_SP_6_H1`).
Variable changed vs SP_6_H1: the ANGLE — steady steps / anti-slip on hard floors,

comfort — worth its own track if CPA is competitive.
