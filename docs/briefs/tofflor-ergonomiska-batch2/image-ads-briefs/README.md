## Global rules (apply to every static)

1. **Price is 309 kr.** Compare-at 400 kr struck-through, "spara 91 kr". Nothing else.
   No percentages, no discount codes.
2. **The product (or the orange insole) is the largest visual element.** The orange wavy
   insole is the product's scroll-stopper — batch #1 data shows the highest-CTR ads all
   show it clearly.
3. **Swedish text word-for-word from the brief's caption table.** Never translate or
   rewrite. Headline = biggest text. Keep all text inside safe margins, readable on a
   phone at feed size.
4. **Export 1:1 (1080×1080) and 4:5 (1080×1350).**
5. **No fabricated reviews, star ratings or "verified customer" framing** — the product
   has no verified reviews yet. Proof lines are the guarantee ("30 dagars öppet köp") or
   the true stock fact (more pairs ordered than we had in stock in week one). See
   DO_NOT_REUSE in reference-assets.
6. **No medical claims.** Ceiling = product-page language ("avlastar leder och rygg").
7. **Product print warning:** angle/crop shoes so the garbled English print on the strap
   is not readable. Retouching the print away is allowed.
8. **Spelling traps:** "tofflor", "stötdämpande", "halkfria", "öppet köp", "jämförpris",
   "hälar och knän".
