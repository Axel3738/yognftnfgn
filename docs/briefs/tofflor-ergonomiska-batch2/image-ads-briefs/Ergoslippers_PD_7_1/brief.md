compare-at 400 kr (save 91 kr), 30-day money-back, Klarna.**

## Concept & hypothesis

DEMO static: the orange wave insole STRAIGHT to camera — the sole IS the image.
Source pattern: proven on the sibling product (strandtofflorna DNA pattern #8:
"sole-to-camera works and lifts CTR" — PD_13_6, 10 purchases). Also the format-transfer
slot for `Tofflor_SP_1` (this product's #2 ad), whose footage is all about the sole.
**Hypothesis:** the insole alone stops the scroll better than the whole shoe.
