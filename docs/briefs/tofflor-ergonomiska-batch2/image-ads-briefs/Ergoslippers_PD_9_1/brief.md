## Concept & hypothesis

LISTICLE: 4 concrete points around the product. Source: the account's best-performing
image format historically is the listicle (`b020`-format, ROAS 2.53, per
`pipeline/b020-format.mjs` notes). **Hypothesis:** the proven listicle skeleton works
for this product when every point is concrete and checkable.
