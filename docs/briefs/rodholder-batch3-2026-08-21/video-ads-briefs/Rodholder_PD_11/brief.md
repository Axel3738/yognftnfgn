## Hypothesis

The winning clips all contain one moment that sells: the clamp CLICKING shut around a rod. This ad is nothing but that moment, repeated from 2–3 angles. Isolated variable: pure click-demo vs the winners' longer supplier demos.

## Shot list

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | MACRO: clamp open in hand, rod section enters, CLICK shut. Fill the frame. |
| 3–7 s | Same click, second angle (from above). |
| 7–11 s | Rod lifted by the clamped section — nothing moves, line pinned flat. |
| 11–14 s | All 4 clamps on the table, one already on the rod. End on product. |

**H1 opens on:** the click itself (0–3 s as above). **H2 opens on:** the rod being lifted (shot 3 first, then the clicks).

## Rules

NO text, NO voiceover, NO price. Natural sound — the physical *click* should be audible. Real hands, mobile 4K, within 15 cm, locked camera. 8–15 s. Export 9:16 + 4:5.
