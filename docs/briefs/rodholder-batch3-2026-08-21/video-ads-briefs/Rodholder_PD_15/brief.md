# Rodholder_PD_15_H1 + Rodholder_PD_15_H2 — "Skaka-testet" (the shake test)

**Type:** Video ×2 · **Production:** minimal · **Tier 2**

## Hypothesis

A proof-demo: the clamped rod is held upside down and shaken — nothing opens, nothing slides, the line stays pinned. Objection killed without a word ("does it actually hold?"). Isolated variable: stress-test proof vs neutral demo.

## Shot list

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Clamped rod held straight up, then flipped upside down. Hold. |
| 3–7 s | Shake it — deliberately, hard enough to be convincing. Clamp stays shut. |
| 7–11 s | MACRO: the clamp mid-shake — foam gripping, line trapped flat, zero movement. |
| 11–14 s | Rod flipped back, set down clamped. End on product. |

**H1 opens on:** the upside-down flip. **H2 opens on:** the macro of the clamp under shake (shot 3 first).

## Rules

NO text, NO voiceover, NO price. The shake must be REAL — do not fake stability with cuts; one continuous shake take minimum 3 seconds. Real gear. 8–15 s. Export 9:16 + 4:5.
