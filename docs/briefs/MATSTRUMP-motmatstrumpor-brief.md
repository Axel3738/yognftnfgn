# Meet Matstrumpor — silent brand video

**Meta ad name (use when launching):** `MATSTRUMP_alla_benefit_lifestyle_motmatstrumpor_v1`

**Concept in one line:** A calm, almost silent "meet the brand" video. Short text
lines pop on screen over everyday product shots. The product does the talking:
socks that look like food, in a box that looks like a sushi tray.

**Delivery file:** `motmatstrumpor_9x16.mp4`

We are copying the structure of a top-performing US ad (Roy's Boys). Reference
screenshots of the original are in the folder shared with this brief — match the
**feel** (casual, homey, playful), never their product or logo.

## The 3 rules that make this ad work

1. **NO voiceover.** Silence or very low, calm music (no lyrics, license-safe
   for Meta). The original's best ads are almost silent — that IS the concept.
2. **NO call-to-action, NO prices.** The video ends on the logo only.
3. **Normal, everyday video.** No effects, no zoom-punches, no flashy hooks.
   Phone-camera feeling, daylight, real Swedish-home vibe.

## Format

- **9:16 only** (1080×1920), total length **~14 seconds**.
- 8 quick shots (~1–1.5 s each) → 5 s of plain product b-roll → logo end card.
- Hard cuts, no transitions.
- Text: simple white text with subtle shadow. Keep inside the middle 80 % of
  frame height. Text position moves around (bottom → top → middle) so the eye
  stays awake — follow the table.
- Shot 1 text uses a **typewriter effect** (~1 s). All other lines **pop on
  instantly** — no fades, no sliding.
- Footage: film the real products, or generate hyperrealistic AI video/stills
  with subtle motion. Either way it must read as a real phone video.
  **Product accuracy is the one hard rule** — use the real product photos on
  matstrumpor.se as reference (sushi box with sock "maki" rolls, pizza, burger,
  donut socks). The ad must show what the customer gets.
- Musts: tick the **AI-content disclosure in Ads Manager** if any AI footage is
  used. Never caption anything as customer footage. No claims beyond this brief.
