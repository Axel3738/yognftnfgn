Context: We run a few Shopify stores in the Nordics (Sweden is the main market). One is a general store where we test products; the others are one-product brands built from the winners. Products are mostly one-time purchases (vehicle and boat covers, garden and outdoor gear, a novelty gift sock box that sells mainly before Christmas). Repeat purchase rate is about 0-2%. Meta spend per store is roughly 100 to 2,500 USD/day. Every active campaign is a CBO on highest volume (lowest cost, no cap). We ship new creatives every week and judge ads on day 7 by contribution margin vs break-even CPA (break-even ROAS around 1.5-1.7).

1. Cost caps. He runs cost caps with an inflated budget (e.g. 3x the normal daily budget, tight cap) so only better ads get spend, and says Meta recommends it after Andromeda. 2. Customer survey. He says the easiest way to understand the customer is to survey buyers (age, what drew them in, what to improve). Which questions have you found actually produce new angles and hooks? Send it after purchase or after delivery? Is an incentive (store credit, giveaway) worth it?

3. Organic TikTok/Reels. He posts behind-the-scenes, non-selling content and says blended ROAS went up. For a small team with one-time-purchase products and one Q4 gift product, is organic worth the time, or should all effort stay on paid creative volume?

Bonus: with about 1% repeat rate, is a 10% store-credit cashback program worth it, or wasted margin?
