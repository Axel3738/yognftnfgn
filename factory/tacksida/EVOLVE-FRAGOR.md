# Frågorna till Evolve-boten om tacksides-upsellen

Axel klistrar in en fråga i taget i Evolve-botens chatt (Discord) och klistrar
tillbaka svaret till Claude. Svaren sparas i `EVOLVE-SVAR.md`. Samma arbetssätt
som `klaviyo/evolve/FRAGOR.md`.

⚠️ **Inget i frågorna avslöjar brandet** (Axels krav 2026-09-26: "jag vill
inte likna mitt brand i den servern"): inga butiksnamn, inga domäner, ingen
produkttitel ordagrant, inga exakta ordertal. Produkten beskrivs som "a caravan
roof cover", tilläggsprodukterna som "window insulation mats" och "a themed
advent calendar". Talen är avrundade. Klistra in frågorna som de står.

Bakgrunden som frågorna bygger på (mätt 2026-09-26 i butiken, senaste 14
dygnen): ~27 ordrar/dygn, 93 % av ordrarna är en enda artikel, Klarna 45 % av
alla betalningar och **74 % i Sverige**, Shop Pay 30 %, PayPal 7 %. Shopifys
one-click-sida efter betalning visas inte för Klarna-köp — därför handlar
flera frågor om vad man gör när kunden redan betalat med Klarna.

---

**Fråga 1: strukturen — one-click-sida eller tacksida**

```
I run a Swedish Shopify store (Grow plan, not Plus) selling caravan and motorhome accessories. One hero product (a caravan roof cover, ~100 EUR), ~30 orders a day, 93 % of orders are a single item. I want to add a post-purchase upsell with two cheap complementary products (window insulation mats ~50 EUR, and a themed advent calendar ~35 EUR for Q4).

Two placements are possible: (a) Shopify's one-click post-purchase page between payment and the thank-you page, and (b) an offer on the thank-you / order-status page that opens a new pre-filled checkout with the discount applied. What does the community actually run, and what take rates do members see for each placement in a hobby/niche store with this AOV? Is there any data on whether running both (one-click first, thank-you page as fallback) cannibalises or adds?
```

**Fråga 2: rabattdjupet**

```
Same store as above. Post-purchase, the acquisition cost is already paid, so I can discount the add-on hard. What discount depth does the community recommend for a post-purchase offer on a 35–50 EUR complementary product: 20 %, 30 %, 40 %, 50 %? Is there data on how take rate moves with depth? Fixed price ("249 kr instead of 539 kr") vs percentage — which converts better? Should the add-on be priced below its own product page price only here, or should the product not exist in the store at all outside the offer?
```

**Fråga 3: ett eller två erbjudanden, och vilket först**

```
Same store. Should the post-purchase page show ONE offer or two (an offer and a downsell if declined)? If two: which goes first — the closest complement (window insulation mats for the same caravan) or the seasonal gift item (advent calendar in October–November)? Does the community swap the post-purchase offer for Q4, and does a gift-type item convert post-purchase at all when the customer just bought a practical item for themselves?
```

**Fråga 4: copy och inramning**

```
Same store. What headline and button patterns does the community use on post-purchase offers that actually convert — "Add to your order, ships together" vs "One-time offer" vs "Complete your setup"? Countdown timer: help or hurt? Struck-through list price: yes or no? What wording causes support tickets or refunds (for example customers thinking the first order failed)? If members have real examples of a high-converting post-purchase page (screenshots or copy), I'd like to see them.
```

**Fråga 5: Klarna-problemet**

```
In my store 3 out of 4 Swedish customers pay with Klarna, and Shopify's one-click post-purchase page is not shown for Klarna (and some other) payments. For those customers the only options are the thank-you page (a new checkout, pre-filled, discount auto-applied) and email/SMS shortly after the order. What do Nordic or European members do here? Does a thank-you page offer with a one-tap re-checkout (Shop Pay / Klarna) get a meaningful take rate, and what timing works for a post-purchase email offer (10 minutes, 1 hour, next day)?
```

**Fråga 6: mätning**

```
Same store. Which numbers do you judge a post-purchase offer on (take rate, incremental AOV, refund rate on the add-on, effect on the main product's conversion), how many orders do you wait for before deciding, and what counts as a "good" take rate for a one-click offer vs a thank-you page offer? How do members keep post-purchase revenue from inflating Meta ROAS on the ad that brought the customer, or do they just accept it?
```

**Fråga 7: appar eller egen bygge**

```
Same store, Shopify Grow plan, five languages (sv/nb/en/fi/da) and five currencies. Which post-purchase / thank-you page apps do members use in 2026 (ReConvert, AfterSell, Zipify OCU, Kaching, others), which handle multi-language and multi-currency without hacks, and has anyone built their own checkout extension instead? What would you avoid?
```

**Fråga 8: logistiken bakom**

```
Same store, dropshipped from a supplier that fulfils each order separately. If the add-on is bought as a second order from the thank-you page, it ships as a second parcel (free shipping for the customer, extra cost for me). Does the community merge such orders before fulfilment, price the offer to cover the second parcel, or only run one-click (same order) offers for that reason?
```
