# Anställningsavtalet som faktiskt används — mall för OPS-rekryteringen

**Källa:** Axel visade det signerade avtalet 2026-09-06 (skärmbild, signerat
2026-09-04, arbetsgivare **Stonebite Ecom AB**). Detta är slutresultatet av
annonsen i `factory/rekrytering/jobbannons-video-editor.md` — annonsens
bonuspaket ($60/$50/$15) kokades i avtalet ner till lönetrappan plus
**0,4 % commission på adspend** för de ads redigeraren skapar.

Commissionen är samma sats som `SATS` i `commission/berakning.mjs` och betalas
via `/commission`-rutinen. Den står INTE i avtalstexten nedan — den är ett
tilläggsvillkor. Skriv in den i nästa version av avtalet.

## Avtalstexten ordagrant (engelska, som signerad)

> # EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
>
> **Employee:** [Name]  **Role:** Full-Time Video Editor
> **Start date:** [Date]  **Employer:** Stonebite Ecom AB
>
> **WORK**
>
> Monday–Saturday, 8 hours/day, Philippine time. The job is to complete the
> daily editing list. The standard is **10 ads/day on average**, depending on
> complexity. But in the beginning we understand it might be slow the first week.
>

> and deliver work on time. The Employer agrees to provide clear assignments,
> required assets, feedback, and reliable payment.
>

>
> Salary is paid twice per month.
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | Start | $520/month |
> | After 1 month | $550/month |
> | After 3 months | $580/month |

> The scheduled raises apply while performance and reliability remain satisfactory.
>
> **WORK OWNERSHIP**
>
> Work created for the company belongs to the company. Company files, data,
> advertising information, and internal information must stay confidential.
>
> **ENDING THE JOB**
>
> **30 day notice for either sides to end the job.** Both sides agree to
> communicate early if something is not working instead of disappearing or
> withholding payment or work. If there where to be a massive misbehavior from
