# UGC outreach — SOP for the VA

**Owner:** the VA (runs `/ugc-va` in her own Claude account, same repo).
**Written:** 2026-09-24, Axel's decision: "VA only — she gets a Claude account
and runs the command herself." No paid platform, no automatic DMs.

This file is the rulebook. `/ugc-va` reads it every run. If this file and a
chat message disagree, this file wins — ask Axel to change the file.

---

## The job in one line

Find Swedish creators → send a personal message → negotiate inside the limits
→ get the product to them → get 3 usable videos → ask Axel to pay.

## What we buy

We buy **videos we run as ads on our own pages**. Followers do not matter.
A creator with 800 followers who films a raw, honest phone video is worth more
than a polished influencer with 80 000. Read `factory/ugc/vetting-framework.md`
§1 (the 7-question quick filter) — every creator passes it before you write.

## Your limits (from `factory/ugc/villkor.md` — the only source of numbers)

| You MAY do alone | You must STOP and ask Axel |
|---|---|
| Offer 1 500 kr per video | Anything above 3 000 kr per video |
| Go up to 3 000 kr for creators with 14–16 points | Paying anything before delivery |
| Offer 3 videos, product included | Exclusivity, or rights with a time limit |
| Accept a longer delivery time (up to 21 days) | Commission / % of sales deals |
| Add a 4th video instead of raising the price | A creator who wants a contract on their own paper |
| Send the product (0 kr order, see below) | Any product not listed as open in `villkor.md` |

**You never pay anyone.** Axel pays by Swish. You send him a payment request
through `/ugc-va delivered`.

## Negotiation — how to hold the price without being rude

Creators often answer "my rate is 3 500 kr per video". That is normal. It is a
start, not a no.

1. **Always start at 1 500 kr.** Even for good creators. Never open with your top.
2. **Trade, don't give.** Before you raise the price, offer something else:
   - "We can do 4 videos instead of 3 at the same price per video" (more work = more money for them, same price for us)
   - "You keep the product (worth X kr)" — say the real price from the product page
   - "If the first 3 work, we order more every month" — only say this if Axel has said it's true for this product
3. **Raise in one step, not five.** If you move, go 1 500 → 2 000 → stop. Only
   14–16-point creators can go to 3 000, and only after two rounds.
4. **Rights are not negotiable.** Ads, all channels, no time limit. If they want
   a time limit or extra fee for ads usage: thank them and walk away (or ask
   Axel if the creator scores 15–16).
5. **Walking away is fine.** There are always more creators. One polite line:
   "Thanks for your time — it doesn't fit our budget right now. Good luck!"
6. **Everything in writing.** The final agreement (price, number of videos,
   rights, delivery days, payment by Swish within 7 days after approved
   delivery) is sent as one message and the creator answers "yes". No yes = no deal.

`/ugc-va reply` writes every answer for you in Swedish, with the English
meaning next to it. You decide which one to send.

## Sending the product

Default until Axel says otherwise:

1. Ask the creator for name, address and phone **in the chat** (never in a file).
2. Create a **draft order at 0 kr** in the store's Shopify admin to that address
   (Orders → Create order → add product → discount 100 % → note "UGC <creator>").
3. Write only the **order number** in the pipeline. Never the address.
4. Tell the creator delivery takes 5–10 business days and send the brief now,
   so they can plan.

## Delivery and quality check

Before you ask Axel to pay, all of this must be true (the command checks it with you):

- [ ] 3 (or the agreed number) videos, vertical 9:16, phone-filmed
- [ ] The product is in the first 3 seconds
- [ ] Swedish spoken, no subtitles burned in (our editors add captions)
- [ ] No music with copyright, no other brand's logo in focus
- [ ] Our store's name is NOT said in the video (we mirror ads between stores)
- [ ] Files uploaded to the product's Notion creative hub or a Drive folder you control

One redo is included. If a video fails the list: ask for a redo, polite and
specific ("the product shows up at 0:09 — can you start with it in your hand?").

## Tone with creators

- Swedish, short, human. Max ~120 words in the first message.
- Mention one real thing they made. No "we love your content".
- Never invent numbers (sales, views, results).
- Never promise more work unless Axel said so.

## Personal data

The pipeline file holds only **public** contact info (profile links, public
email). Addresses, phone numbers and Swish numbers never go in the repo —
they live in the Shopify order and in the chat with the creator.
