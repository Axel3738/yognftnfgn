# UGC outreach kit — start here

A small, standalone kit. You get your own company Google account and
Instagram account — the logins are in **0 - YOUR LOGINS (secret).pdf**.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **0 - YOUR LOGINS (secret).pdf** | Google + Instagram logins. Never upload or share it. |
| **1 - START HERE.pdf** | This page |
| **2 - VA Handbook.pdf** | **Your job, step by step, every case, every message.** Read it once, keep it open. |
| **3 - Paste into Claude project instructions.txt** | Instructions for Claude — you paste it once, you don't need to read it |
| **Google Sheet templates** folder | The columns for your Google Sheet |

## Setup (once, 15 minutes)

### First: the Google account
Open **0 - YOUR LOGINS (secret).pdf** and do part 1. From now on you do
everything signed in to that Google account.

### Claude
1. Signed in to the company Google account: go to claude.ai → **Continue with Google** 
   → **Projects** → **Create project** → name it "UGC outreach".
2. Click **Set project instructions** → open **3 - Paste into Claude project instructions.txt** → select all (Ctrl+A) → copy (Ctrl+C) → paste → Save.
3. Click **Project knowledge** → upload **2 - VA Handbook.pdf**.
4. In the chat box, check that **web search** is on (the tools button).
5. Start a new chat in the project and paste a product link (how you pick products: handbook → step 1).

Other things you can write in the chat: `reply <creator> <their message>`,
`post <product link>`, `brief <product link> <creator>`,
`deal <creator> <price>`, `check <creator>`.

Tip: use **one chat per creator** for replies, so Claude remembers the conversation.

### The Google Sheet
Open the handbook → chapter **"The Google Sheet"**. Import the two files in
the **Google Sheet templates** folder. Share the sheet with Axel.

### Drive
Create a Google Drive folder **"UGC deliveries"** and share it with Axel (edit).
One subfolder per deal.

## Your first day
1. Read the handbook once, top to bottom.
2. Log in to Instagram (login PDF, part 2) and start **day 1 of the warm-up**
   (handbook chapter 1). No DMs for 14 days — use that time for email and
   TikTok outreach and to fill the sheet.
3. Pick your first 2 products (handbook → "You pick the products yourself").
4. Paste the link into Claude → check the creators → save them in the sheet →
   send the first 5 messages by **email** (Instagram waits until day 15).
