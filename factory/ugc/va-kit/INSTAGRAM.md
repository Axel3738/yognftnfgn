# Warm up the Instagram account

A brand-new Instagram account that starts sending messages right away gets
blocked. For **14 days** you make it look like a real, active account — and
only then start DMs. This takes **15–20 minutes a day**. Don't skip days.
(Evolve community 2026-09-24: "did you warm up the account?" is the first
question when an account gets restricted; accounts get blocked around
80–100 DMs a day even after warming up.)

The login is in your secret login PDF. Use **only your phone**, the same phone
every day, no VPN, no automation tools.

---

## Day 1 — set up the profile (30 minutes, once)

Instagram app → profile → **Edit profile**:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| Profile picture | The Stonebite logo (Claude can't make it — download it from **stonebite.org**: right-click the logo → Save image) |
| Name | **Stonebite** |
| Bio | Ask Claude: `bio` — it writes 2 lines in Swedish, e.g. "Svensk e-handel 🇸🇪 Vi samarbetar med UGC-kreatörer 🎥 Skriv till oss för samarbete". **Never name our stores in the bio.** |
| Link | **stonebite.org** |

Then: Settings → **Account type and tools** → **Switch to professional account**
→ **Business** → category "Shopping & retail". (Gives you the contact button
and the message inbox for requests.)

## Days 1–3 — look alive

Every day:
- **Follow 10** Swedish accounts: UGC creators, small Swedish brands, people in
  our product areas (boats, caravans, garden, cars, outdoor).
- **Like 20** posts from them.
- **Comment on 3** posts — Claude writes them: `comment <paste the post text>`.
  Short, real, Swedish. Never copy the same comment twice.
- **Watch Reels for 5 minutes** in your feed (Instagram notices real use).

Posts (1 per day on days 1–3, 3 posts in total): ask Claude `post idea` —
simple things like "Vi söker svenska UGC-kreatörer", a product photo from the
store, a behind-the-scenes photo. A profile with 0 posts looks fake to creators.

## Days 4–7 — a bit more

Every day:
- Follow **20**, like **30**, comment on **5**.
- 1 story every day (a product photo, a question, a repost).
- 1 new post every second day.
- **Reply** to every comment and message you get (Claude writes it).

## Days 8–14 — normal use

Every day:
- Follow **20–30**, like **30–40**, comment on **5–10**.
- 1 story every day, 1 post every 2–3 days.
- **Save** the creators you'll message later in a Collection called "UGC"
  (tap the bookmark on their post → Save to collection). That's your DM list for day 15.
- Unfollow people who didn't follow back after 7 days (max 20 per day).

## Day 15 onwards — DMs start

| Week | DMs per day |
|---|---|
| Week 3 | max **20** |
| Week 4 | max **30** |
| Week 5 | max **40** |
| From week 6 | max **50** — never more |

Keep doing the daily likes, comments and stories. An account that only sends
DMs gets blocked.

**How to send DMs safely:**
- Every message is **different** — Claude personalises each one. Never paste the same text to many people.
- **No links in the first message.** Links come after they reply.
- Spread them out: 5–10, a break, 5–10 more. Not 30 in one minute.
- Message people who **follow you back or liked your post** first — they are warm.

## If Instagram warns you

| What you see | What you do |
|---|---|
| "Try again later" / "We restrict certain activity" | Stop ALL DMs, follows and likes for **48 hours**. Then restart at **half** the number. |
| "Confirm it's you" / security code | The code goes to the Gmail in your login PDF. Open Gmail, type it. |
| Account "suspended" / "disabled" | Tap **Disagree / Appeal** and follow the steps (the business is real). Meanwhile continue outreach by **email and TikTok**. Write it in the sheet's Notes. |
| A DM shows "Message request not delivered" | Normal for strangers — they'll see it in Requests. Don't resend. |

## Never

- Never buy followers or likes, never use follow/unfollow apps.
- Never log in from a computer AND a phone the same day in week 1–2.
- Never change the password, email or phone number on the account.
- Never post anything with a price, a store name or a customer.
