# VETTING — how to tell a good UGC creator before we pay anything

**What we buy:** videos we run as ads on our own pages. **Followers do not
matter.** A creator with 800 followers who films a raw, honest phone video beats
a polished influencer with 80 000. Our best ad ever was one real person, filmed
on a phone, product in hand. That is what we look for.

## 1. Quick filter — 7 yes/no questions, under 2 minutes, from the profile

A NO on question 1, 2 or 7 → stop. Otherwise you need at least 5 YES.

1. Does the creator **speak Swedish** in their videos?
2. Is it a **real person** — name, face, contact — not AI or an anonymous page?
3. Is there a portfolio with UGC samples for **several different brands**?
4. Do the samples look like **real phone footage**, not studio production?
5. Do they film **everyday places** — outdoors, home, garden, car — not only ring-light/beauty?
6. Are there clips where the product is **held/used in close-up with hands**?
7. Is it the **same person and voice** in all portfolio clips? (Different voices = stolen portfolio.)

## 2. Deep check — their 3 latest videos, 0–2 points each

0 = missing/wrong, 1 = partly, 2 = clearly yes.

| | Question | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Hook, first 2 seconds.** Pause at 2 s. A real object close-up = 2. A person talking with no object = 1. Logo/intro/text/black = 0. | ×2 |
| B | **Spoken Swedish sounds natural**, like telling a friend — not reading a script. (Ask Claude: paste the caption/transcript and ask "does this sound natural in Swedish?") | ×1 |
| C | **Shows instead of talks** — before/after, the problem on screen — instead of "amazing, game changer". | ×1 |
| D | **Product visible within 4 seconds.** | ×1 |
| E | **Whole message under ~45 seconds** without feeling rushed. | ×1 |
| F | **Sound and light:** voice clear, no music carrying it, daylight, **no beauty filters**. | ×1 |
| G | **Doesn't look like an ad** — could be a normal post in the feed. | ×1 |

## 3. Red flags — out immediately, whatever the score

- AI face or AI people anywhere
- Fake testimonials ("verified customer" that doesn't exist)
- Stolen samples: different voices, other people's watermarks
- Wants 100 % payment before delivery
- The same generic script ("this product changed my life") for many brands
- Only polished influencer ads, nothing that looks organic
- Slow or vague already in the first messages
- No Swedish
- Price far above the Swedish market without a portfolio to match
  (Swedish market 2026: about 500–3 000 kr per video; beginners 700–2 000 kr)

## 4. Score

A counts double. Max = 4 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = **16**.

| Score | What to do |
|---|---|
| **12–16** | Contact (Tier A). 14–16 may be offered up to the top price in `TERMS.md`. |
| **8–11** | Save as Tier B if they fit an avatar. Contact only if no 12+ creators exist. |
| **0–7** | No row in the sheet. |
