## The price rule in one line

Start at 1 500 for the first deal (1 video + raw footage). Move once to 2 000 if needed. Only 14–16-point creators may go
to 3 000, and only when they agree to the long-term partnership (see `SOP.md`
→ Negotiation). Never above 3 000 — walk away instead.

## Can't invoice?

Many Swedish creators have no company. That is fine: they can invoice through
an invoicing service for freelancers (for example Frilans Finans, Cool Company
or Gigapay — the creator picks one, the service sends us the invoice). We never
pay privately via Swish or bank transfer without an invoice.
