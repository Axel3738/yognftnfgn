# Frågor till Evolve-chatboten om UGC (slutgiltiga 2026-09-24)

Axel klistrar in del 1 i Evolve, väntar på svaret, klistrar in del 2, och
klistrar sedan tillbaka båda svaren till en Claude-session som för in dem i
`factory/ugc/va-kit/`. Engelska med flit: chatboten svarar skarpast där.

Granskade av tre agenter samma dag (VA:ns vardag, svensk juridik/skatt,
annonsresultat). Ändrat efter granskningen: kostnaden rättad (en första affär
är 4 500-9 000 kr + produkt, inte ~3 000), fråga om följare mot UGC struken
(redan beslutat), nya frågor om marknadsplatsernas förskott, AI-dubbning och
samtycke, reklammärkning, inspelningslista för råmaterial, när i produkttestet
UGC ska beställas, dom per kreatör och vad man beställer efter en vinnare.

## Del 1 av 2

```
This is PART 1 of 2. Answer the questions in part 1 now. Part 2 comes in my next message and uses the same context.

I run a Swedish e-commerce company and I'm building a UGC creator process that my VA runs. I need your most concrete, number-backed answers. Answer each question by its number. Where you'd normally say "it depends", tell me what it depends on and what YOU would do in my situation.

CONTEXT
- Business model: GENERAL STORE (Bäverbutiken, Sweden). We test many new products every month (boat engine covers, trimmer shoulder straps, seat covers, caravan roof covers, IBC tank covers, beach slippers etc.). Price points roughly 300-1 200 SEK. Winners get spun out into their own one-product stores.
- Markets: Sweden is the main market (Swedish-language ads). We also sell in Norway, Denmark and Finland, and one one-product store sells in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. We translate winning ads with AI voice-clone + lip-sync (HeyGen).
- Fulfilment: products ship from China, 5-10 business days to the customer.
- Traffic: Meta ads only, CBO. About 87 000 SEK ad spend per week across all stores. Break-even ROAS on most products 1.5-1.7. We judge ads on profit contribution: (break-even CPA - CPA) x purchases.
- What works: our best-ever ad is ONE raw, phone-filmed UGC video from a real Swedish person holding the product. AI faces and fake testimonials died every time. Product in hand in the first 2 seconds beats talking heads.
- Team: our video editors and VA are in the Philippines and do NOT speak Swedish. The VA finds creators and handles all communication, with an AI writing the Swedish messages.
- Current offer: 1 500 SEK per video (max 3 000 for top creators), 3 videos per first deal (so 4 500-9 000 SEK + product), free product they keep, raw footage always included, usage rights included with no time limit and no extra fee, delivery 14 days after the product arrives, one redo, paid by invoice 30 days AFTER we approve. No exclusivity. First DM to usable video takes about 5-6 weeks.
- Negotiation tactic: we sell long-term work ("we test new products every month, good videos = product after product") instead of a higher price.

QUESTIONS

Sourcing
1. Where do you find the best Swedish (and Nordic) UGC creators in 2026 for a general store? Rank the channels (TikTok search, Instagram, Collabstr, Sprww, Vocast, Influee, Facebook groups, other) by quality per hour spent.
2. Marketplaces like Collabstr usually take payment upfront (escrow) and forbid off-platform deals, but we pay by invoice after approval. Accept escrow, or skip marketplaces? Which platform rule must the VA never break?
3. With very different products (boats, garden, caravans, cars), should we build a bank of all-round creators or match a creator to each product avatar? How many active creators can one VA manage?

Price and negotiation
4. Realistic price per video for Swedish UGC in 2026: beginner, mid, top? Do raw footage and perpetual paid-ads usage normally cost extra in Sweden, and how do we avoid paying it?
5. Is "long-term work instead of a higher price" the right lead card? Give me 3 negotiation lines the VA can use.
6. Is 3 videos the right first test, or better 1 video + raw footage from 3 different creators?
7. Pay per video, per product, a monthly retainer for top creators, or a performance bonus (e.g. +1 000 SEK if the ad passes X spend)? What do the best brands do?
```

## Del 2 av 2

```
PART 2 of 2. Same context as part 1 (Swedish general store, Meta ads, Nordic + US/UK markets, AI dubbing of winners, VA in the Philippines who doesn't speak Swedish, 1 500-3 000 SEK per video, raw footage + perpetual rights included, invoice after approval, long-term work as our negotiation card). Answer by number.

Contract, legal, money (Sweden/EU)
8. Is a DM "ja" enough, or do we need a signed contract? Which clauses let us edit, cut, translate and reuse the videos forever under Swedish copyright, name-and-image law and GDPR, so the creator can't revoke later?
9. We dub winning ads into other languages with AI voice-clone and lip-sync and run them in our other stores. What must the creator approve, must we label it (EU AI Act), and do local creators perform better anyway?
10. Creators may also post the video on their own accounts. Under Swedish marketing law, what must they label, and which claims ("I bought it", "used it all winter", "waterproof") make US liable?
11. Many Swedish creators have no company. Invoicing via Frilans Finans / Cool Company / Gigapay: any problems, who pays the fee, and is the free product taxable income?

Brief and quality
12. What must a UGC brief for a general-store product contain to get a winner on the first try? The 3 most common reasons first videos fail?
13. Give the exact raw shot list to require (hook takes, problem shot, B-roll, hands close-ups) so editors who don't speak Swedish can cut 5+ different ads. Creator's own edit or our recut: which usually wins?
14. The VA can't understand Swedish. We have the creator send a written transcript and let an AI check it. Good idea, or a better way to quality-check without a Swedish speaker?

Process and risk
15. What goes wrong most often when a VA runs creator outreach (ghosting, product never filmed, bad quality, legal)? How do you prevent each?
16. DMs from a new Instagram/TikTok account get restricted. Which account and channel should the VA use (brand account, dedicated UGC account, email), and how many messages per day is safe?
17. Typical reply rate and closing rate for cold outreach to Swedish creators, so we know how many to contact per product?
18. First DM to usable video takes 5-6 weeks. At which stage of a product test should we order UGC, so we don't pay for videos of a product we already killed?

Measuring and scaling
19. Judge UGC per creator (all ads cut from their footage) or per video? With our metric (break-even CPA - CPA) x purchases: what threshold, after how many days, and how many recuts before dropping a creator?
20. When a UGC ad wins, what do we order next from that creator: new hooks on the same product, new angles, or the next product? Is a cheaper hooks-only reshoot normal?
21. How many UGC creators per month should a store at ~87 000 SEK/week in ad spend buy, and what monthly production budget is sensible?
22. Anything important I didn't ask about that you'd set up from day one?
```
