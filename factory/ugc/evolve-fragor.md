## Del 1 av 2

```
This is PART 1 of 2. Answer the questions in part 1 now. Part 2 comes in my next message and uses the same context.

I run a Swedish e-commerce company and I'm building a UGC creator process that my VA runs. I need your most concrete, number-backed answers. Answer each question by its number. Where you'd normally say "it depends", tell me what it depends on and what YOU would do in my situation.

CONTEXT
- Business model: GENERAL STORE (Bäverbutiken, Sweden). We test many new products every month (boat engine covers, trimmer shoulder straps, seat covers, caravan roof covers, IBC tank covers, beach slippers etc.). Price points roughly 300–1 200 SEK. Winners get spun out into their own one-product stores.
- Markets: Sweden is the main market (Swedish-language ads). We also sell in Norway, Denmark and Finland (local-language stores), and one one-product store sells in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
- Fulfilment: products ship from China, 5–10 business days to the customer.
- Traffic: Meta ads only, CBO campaigns. Around 87 000 SEK ad spend per week across all stores. Break-even ROAS on most products is 1.5–1.7.
- What already works: our best-ever ad is ONE raw, phone-filmed UGC video from a real Swedish person holding the product. AI faces and fake testimonials have died every time we tried them. Product in hand in the first 2 seconds beats talking heads.
- Team: our video editors and VA are in the Philippines and do NOT speak Swedish. The VA will find creators and handle all communication, with an AI writing the Swedish messages for her.
- Current offer to creators: 1 500 SEK per video (max 3 000 SEK for top creators), 3 videos per first deal, free product they keep, raw footage always included, usage rights included with no time limit and no extra rights fee, 14 days delivery after product arrives, one redo, paid by invoice 30 days AFTER we approve the videos. No exclusivity.
- Negotiation tactic: we sell long-term work — "we test new products every month, if the first videos are good you'll get product after product" — instead of paying a higher price.
- We have a calculation that decides whether a product is worth ~3 000 SEK of UGC (creator fee + product) before we buy it.

QUESTIONS

Sourcing
1. Where do you find the best Swedish (and Nordic) UGC creators in 2026 for a general store like mine? Rank the channels (TikTok search, Instagram, Collabstr, Sprww, Vocast, Influee, Facebook groups, other) by quality per hour spent.
2. For a general store with very different products (boats, garden, caravans, cars), should we build a small bank of "all-round" creators who can film anything, or match a creator to every product avatar? How many creators should one person manage at once?
3. Micro-influencers with an audience vs. pure UGC creators with no audience: which gives better ADS (not organic reach) for a product like ours, and how do you tell them apart fast?

Price and negotiation
4. What is a realistic price per video for Swedish UGC in 2026 — beginner, mid, top? Does including raw footage and perpetual paid-ads usage normally cost extra in Sweden, and how do we avoid paying it?
5. Is our "long-term work instead of a higher price" tactic the right lead card? What else works in negotiation with creators? Give me 3 lines the VA can use.
6. Is 3 videos the right first test? Or is it better to buy 1 video + raw footage from 3 different creators?
7. Should we pay per video, per product, a monthly retainer for our best creators, or a performance bonus (e.g. extra 1 000 SEK if the ad passes X spend)? What do the best brands do?

Contract, legal, payment (Sweden)
8. Is a written "yes" in a DM/email enough as an agreement, or do we need a short signed contract? What are the 5 clauses that must be in it for paid-ads usage in Sweden/EU (GDPR, likeness, perpetual rights, raw footage)?
9. Many Swedish creators have no company. Invoicing through Frilans Finans / Cool Company / Gigapay — any problems, and who should pay that fee?
10. We ship the product for free. Any tax or "benefit" issue for the creator or us in Sweden?
```
