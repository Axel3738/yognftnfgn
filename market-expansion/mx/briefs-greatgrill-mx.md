# GreatGrill Mexico — Video Ad Briefs (Batch 1: 8 ads)

**For:** video editors · **Language of deliverables:** Mexican Spanish VO + Spanish overlays · **Date:** 2026-08-16
**Product:** GreatGrill rolling grill basket — laclinicadelasador.mx
**Format all ads:** 9:16 vertical (Reels/Stories), captions burned in, hook must land in the first 2 seconds (visual + first VO line together).
**Offer in every CTA:** up to 42% off + free shipping across Mexico + lifetime warranty. Never say "40%".
**Social proof allowed:** "4.8 estrellas, más de 240 reseñas" — nothing else.
**B-roll:** supplier footage + own shots. Every ad needs: (1) food falling through grates or foil steam-mush as the villain shot, (2) the fill-close-roll motion, (3) char marks reveal close-up, (4) the dishwasher shot.

Source: these 8 scripts are Mexican adaptations of a proven competitor library (Bryn, Scandinavia). Structure and beats are kept; language, foods and pains rebuilt from our Mexican VoC research (`voc-research.md`). Do not re-translate or "improve" the Spanish — it is written to match how Mexican grillers actually talk.

**Visual hook (0–2 s):** hand generously seasoning skewers, then a flip on the grate visibly scraping it off; VO line 1.

| # | Español (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Si sazonas tus brochetas, estás tirando tu sazón a la basura. | If you season your skewers, you're throwing your seasoning away. |
