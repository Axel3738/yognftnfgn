## Which fact each concept's claims rest on

- **CS (offer):** Verified product page price — $199, was $249 (save $50, 20% off), free shipping in the US, 5–10 business day delivery, size 21.3 × 9.8 ft (6.5 × 3 m), 210D fabric. No false urgency language ("today only") used — the $199 price is the store's standing price, not a countdown.
- **GT (gift):** The concept's own premise as specified in the brief (the man who talks about/loves his RV) + verified installation fact (one person installs alone, elastic straps hook under the body edge) + price/shipping/guarantee facts.
- **PD (problem):** Verified fact that the cover protects only the roof, with the edge hanging 12–16 in (30–40 cm) down the sides — combined with the US RV-owner concept of the spring inspection for water damage (the English equivalent of the Swedish "fukttestet") supplied in the brief.
- **SP (social proof):** Real Judge.me review quote — Lars: "Fits well and protects the roof from the weather." — plus the verified 16 reviews / 5.0 average.

All four messages end with a soft call to action (👇) and use only the verified US facts: $199/$249/$50, free shipping, 5–10 business days, 90-day guarantee (never "14-day," never Swedish law), 16 reviews at 5.0 average, and American terminology (RV/travel trailer/motorhome, "color," feet/inches first with metric in parentheses at most once).
