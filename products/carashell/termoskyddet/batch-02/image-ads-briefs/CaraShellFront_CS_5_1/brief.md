## 1. Why this ad exists
CaraShell is now a two-product shop for the same motorhome owner. This is the cheapest way to learn whether naming the pair helps the thermal cover sell (the destination is the thermal cover's page; the roof cover is the cart upsell) or just confuses the ad.

## 2. Hypothesis
The parallel headline reads as "this shop knows my vehicle" and lifts CTR without hurting CVR, landing under break-even. If it loses to CS_4_1 on CPA, the pairing stays in the cart, not in the ads.

**Hook idea:** Taköverdraget för vintern. Termoskyddet för resan.
