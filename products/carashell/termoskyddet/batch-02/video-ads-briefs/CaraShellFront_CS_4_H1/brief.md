## 1. Why this ad exists
The source account's purchases sit in CS videos that opened with false urgency ("bara idag, lagret minskar"). CaraShell runs the same angle with true terms, and the current CS videos open on the price. The one part of the old script that is true AND proven is the three-scene line. This ad puts it first, so we learn whether the line or the price is what carries the offer angle when the urgency is gone.

## 2. Hypothesis
Opening on the three scenes (cool, warm, dark) and landing the price at 12 s beats CS_2's price-first opening on CPA, because the scenes are what the motorhome owner recognises and the price then reads as the answer, not as the hook. If CS_4_H1 lands at or under CS_2's CPA once both are judgeable, the winning line becomes the standard opening for every CS creative.

**Hook (locked — it is the variable):** Svalt på sommaren, varmt på vintern, mörkt när du sover.
