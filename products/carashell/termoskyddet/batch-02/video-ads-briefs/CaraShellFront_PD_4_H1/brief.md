## 1. Why this ad exists
The one thing this product does that an inside curtain cannot is keep the glass from getting cold on the inside. Every existing PD creative describes the product; none of them names the customer's morning. This is the first ad built on the conflict.

## 2. Hypothesis
Naming the imma on the inside of the glass — and why the curtain cannot fix it — makes the outside mounting the point of the product, and lifts CVR against the mechanics-only PD videos. If PD_4_H1 becomes the first PD creative to pass the gate with a CPA under break-even, the imma scene becomes the PD angle's standard opening.

**Hook idea:** Varje morgon: imma på insidan av framrutan. Precis där gardinen sitter.

1. **0–3 s** — inside the cab, early light: the windscreen fogged on the inside, a curtain half drawn against it. Caption = the hook.
2. **4–7 s** — macro: droplets between the curtain fabric and the glass.
3. **8–11 s** — cut to outside: the cover fitted over the windscreen, taut; then the inside of the glass, clear and dry.
4. **12–15 s** — wide of the full cover over windscreen and both side windows; a flap clamped in the door frame, door shut; a hand smoothing it.
5. **16–18 s** — inside the cab, dark, the cloth lying unused on the dashboard; a hint of dawn light blocked by the cover.
6. **19–20 s** — price and terms card over the covered front, carashell.se.
