## 1. Why this ad exists
Two reasons, both measured on 2026-09-14. First, PD lost its lead in CaraShell's own account — 36 % of spend, CPA 443 kr against SP's 204 kr — after its bearing mechanism line was rewritten. Second, images beat video 3,6× on CPA here (133 kr vs 476 kr). So PD moves into the format that wins, with a sharper consequence: not "costs most to repair" but something you can *see*.

## 2. Hypothesis
Naming the visible consequence — the stain in the inner ceiling by the roof hatch, the meter in April — converts better than the abstract cost claim, and the image format lifts PD back toward SP's CPA.

**Hook idea:** I april mäter du. I november stod vattnet vid takluckan.

## 5. Design brief
- The split must read instantly — one glance, two states. Damage left, dry right.
- No faces. No invented numbers on the meter display.
- The concept is called "Vårsyndet" internally; **that word never appears on the image** — it is a pun the reader would have to decode, and it fails the one-Mississippi test.
- Test it in a feed mock next to other ads before delivering.

## Hard rules
- Price **1 129 kr**, compare-at **1 469 kr**. No other numbers, ever.
- **No repair cost in kronor.** We do not have that figure and will not invent one.
- **Never claim:** storage bag, drawstring, product weight, exact caravan lengths.

- No false urgency of any kind.
