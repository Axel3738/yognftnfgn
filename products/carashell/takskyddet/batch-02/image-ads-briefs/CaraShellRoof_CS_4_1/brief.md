⚠️ **ISOLATED VARIABLE: the urgency.** The ad currently running says "🔥 23% RABATT … – IDAG 🔥". This version removes "IDAG", the flames and every trace of scarcity. Nothing else about the offer changes.

## 1. Why this ad exists
CS is the cheapest angle in the source account and the cheapest video in CaraShell's own — and it has been starved both times (14 % of spend here, 17 % in the source). It also carries a lie: 1 129 kr is the shop's standing price, so "IDAG" is false urgency of exactly the kind that was removed from HeimGuard. This tests whether the discount sells without it.

## 2. Hypothesis
The 23 % discount performs at or better than the urgency version. If it does, the standing-price framing becomes the house default for CS and the last false-urgency line leaves the account.

**Hook idea:** Taköverdraget 6,5 × 3 m: 1 129 kr i stället för 1 469 kr.

## Hard rules
- Price **1 129 kr**, compare-at **1 469 kr**, discount **23 %**. No other numbers, ever.
- **Never claim:** storage bag, drawstring, product weight, exact caravan lengths.
- Guarantee is **14 dagars ångerrätt enligt svensk lag** — never "30 dagars öppet köp".
- **No urgency of any kind.** That is the variable under test — one slip and the ad is unusable.
