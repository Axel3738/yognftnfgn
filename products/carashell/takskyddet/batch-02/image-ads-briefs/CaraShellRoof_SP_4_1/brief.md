## 1. Why this ad exists
Social proof is CaraShell's strongest angle in its own account: 42 % of spend, **5 of 8 purchases**, CPA 204 kr. Within it, the image version carries almost everything. The open question is whether the *format* is doing the work or whether Lars's particular sentence is. Swapping only the quote answers it.

## 2. Hypothesis
Another verbatim review with equally concrete nouns performs at or near CPA 99 kr. If it collapses, the exact wording matters more than the format, and future SP statics must be built around the single best sentence rather than any five-star quote.

**Quote chosen: Anders — "Skyddar husvagnen bra mot regn och smuts."**
It is the only other review with concrete, visualisable nouns (rain, dirt) naming the exact threat the avatar already fears, matching Lars's original in specificity and length rather than trading down to a vaguer one ("lätt att använda", "bra kvalitet").
