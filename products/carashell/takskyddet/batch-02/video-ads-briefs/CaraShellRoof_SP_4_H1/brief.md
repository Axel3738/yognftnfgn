## 1. Why this ad exists
SP wins here, but almost all of it sits in one static. Video as a whole is losing to image 3,6× on CPA. This tests whether SP's strength travels into video — the format the editors can produce most of — or whether SP is really a static-only angle in this store.

## 2. Hypothesis
Verbatim reviews carry the angle in video too, and SP video beats the store's video average of CPA 476 kr. Johan's line is the carrier because it uses the customer's own vocabulary ("står ute" — the owner who parks outdoors over winter); Karin and Lars support because both name *taket*.

**Hook idea:** "Mycket nöjd. Bra skydd när husvagnen står ute." – Johan.
