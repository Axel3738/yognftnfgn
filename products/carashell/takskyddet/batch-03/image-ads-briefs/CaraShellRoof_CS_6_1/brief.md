## 1. Why this ad exists
CS is the store's second-strongest angle and the one the CBO keeps starving. Before we spend more on it we should know which frame the buyer responds to at this price point — "23 %" or "340 kr". At 1 129 kr the percentage sounds modest and the amount sounds concrete, but that is a hunch, and a hunch is exactly what a paired test is for.

## 2. Hypothesis
The kronor framing converts better, because 340 kr is a sum the reader can picture spending on something else while 23 % requires arithmetic before it means anything. If the percentage wins instead, every future CS creative leads on percent and we stop rewriting the same offer.

**Hook idea:** Spara 340 kr.
