## 1. Why this ad exists
`CS_1_H1` is the only CS creative that converts, and it is a video — while images beat video across this account on profit per krona (1,35 vs 0,91). CS is also the angle the CBO starves: 23 % of spend against SP's 42 %. A static version costs nothing to make and tells us whether the offer's performance belongs to the message or to the medium.

## 2. Hypothesis
The offer carries in static, because a discount is read, not watched — there is no story to lose. If image CPA lands at or under the parent's 216 kr, CS becomes an image-first angle and the video slot is freed for angles that need motion.

**Hook idea:** 23 % rabatt på taköverdraget: 1 469 kr blir 1 129 kr.

## 5. Design brief
- **Headline and price line are a locked pair** — one directly under the other, never separated by the photo or a gutter. The percentage alone is abstract; the two kronor figures beneath it are what make it a picture.
- Strikethrough on 1 469 kr, full weight on 1 129 kr.
- **Nothing else on the canvas:** no "IDAG", no flames, no countdown, no stock line. The parent video carries "🔥 23% RABATT … – IDAG 🔥"; that urgency is deliberately dropped here because 1 129 kr is the store's standing price and the claim would be untrue. Do not restore it.
- The parent's payment-method line is not in our verified facts and is left out rather than guessed.

## Hard rules
- Price **1 129 kr**, compare-at **1 469 kr**, discount **23 %**. No other numbers, ever.
- **No urgency of any kind** — no "IDAG", no timer, no stock scarcity. The price is standing.
- **Never claim:** storage bag, drawstring, product weight, exact caravan lengths, any repair cost in kronor, payment methods.
- Guarantee is **14 dagars ångerrätt enligt svensk lag**.
