## 1. Why this ad exists
`PD_4_1` proved the problem is interesting. It did not give anyone a reason to act, because it ends on a fact about damage and leaves the reader with nothing to do about it. This ad keeps the problem and adds the thing PD has never had on the canvas: **the choice, with a price on one side of it.**

The truth we can use is precise: the roof's cost is unknown until the spring moisture test, and the cover's cost is known right now. That asymmetry is the whole ad — and it lets us make the comparison without ever inventing a repair figure, which we do not have and must never state.

## 2. Hypothesis
Putting a known price against an unknown one converts the attention `PD_4_1` already earns. PD's CPA (669 kr, barely under break-even) moves toward the account average when the ad names a next step instead of a problem.

**Hook idea:** Taket eller taköverdraget. Ett av dem kostar 1 129 kr.

## 5. Design brief
- The split must read in one glance: problem left, solution right, and the price visibly on the right.
- **"Priset får du i april" is a statement about *when you find out*, not a number.** No kronor figure for repair may appear on the image, in the copy card, or anywhere else — we do not have it.
- No moisture meter in this one (that was `PD_4_1`); the left half is the bare roof itself.
- No faces, no people.

## 9. KPI
Judged against break-even CPA **693 kr** (never against target). Benchmarks: PD's own CPA 669 kr, and `PD_4_1`'s 16,3 % CTR with 0 purchases — **this ad is a success only if it converts, not if it beats that CTR.** No verdict before 300 kr spend **and** 3 purchases.

## Hard rules
- Price **1 129 kr**, compare-at **1 469 kr**. No other numbers, ever.
- **No repair cost in kronor, anywhere, in any form** — not a figure, not a range, not "upp till".
- **Never claim:** storage bag, drawstring, product weight, exact caravan lengths.
- Guarantee is **14 dagars ångerrätt enligt svensk lag**.
- No urgency of any kind. 1 129 kr is the store's standing price.
