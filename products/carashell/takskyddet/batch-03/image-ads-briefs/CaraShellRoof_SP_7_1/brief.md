## 1. Why this ad exists
Two facts that have sat next to each other unexamined since launch: the product photos are of a motorhome, and the copy is written to a caravan owner. A husbil owner scrolling past has been shown their own vehicle under a headline addressed to somebody else. This ad closes that gap inside SP, the angle that already converts — 42 % of spend, 12 of 20 purchases.

## 2. Hypothesis
Motorhome owners are an unserved segment of the same buyer, and naming them lifts CPA to at least SP's average (266 kr). If it does, avatar becomes a briefing variable of its own and the whole account gets a husbil line. If it does not, the caravan framing is settled and we stop asking.

**Hook idea:** Ett av 16 omdömen – alla fem stjärnor.

## 5. Design brief
- The motorhome has to read as a motorhome instantly — a high, fully integrated body with the cab built into the shell. If it could be mistaken for a caravan the variant tests nothing.
- No faces, no people.
- **Lars's quote is not attributed to a motorhome owner** anywhere, on the image or in the copy card. We do not know which vehicle any reviewer owns and will not imply it. The avatar shift lives in the photo and the bottom line only.

## 9. KPI
Judged against break-even CPA **693 kr** (never against target). Benchmark: SP's own CPA 266 kr, and `SP_2_1` at 169 kr. No verdict before 300 kr spend **and** 3 purchases. The avatar question is **not** settled by one ad — it needs a second husbil creative before the answer counts.

## Hard rules
- Price **1 129 kr**, compare-at **1 469 kr**. No other numbers, ever. No price on the image.
- **Never imply any reviewer owns a motorhome.** We do not know that.
- **Never claim:** storage bag, drawstring, product weight, exact caravan or motorhome lengths, any repair cost in kronor.
- Guarantee is **14 dagars ångerrätt enligt svensk lag**.
- No urgency of any kind. 1 129 kr is the store's standing price.
