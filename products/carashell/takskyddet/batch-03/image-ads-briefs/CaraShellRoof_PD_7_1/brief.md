## 1. Why this ad exists
Every ad we run so far sells the problem or the proof. None of them sells the thing that actually separates this product from the alternative a buyer is comparing it against: a full vehicle cover, which takes two people and an afternoon. "One person, the roof only" is the product's real differentiator and it has been buried in body copy since launch.

It is also the concept that has been stuck: briefed as video on 2026-09-14, still in Draft, nobody assigned to make it. Moving it to the format the factory produces itself is how it gets tested at all.

## 2. Hypothesis
Leading on the one-person mechanism converts a reader who has already decided the roof needs covering and is stalling on the effort. It attacks the hesitation rather than the awareness, which is a different buyer from the one `PD_4_1` reached — and a closer one.

**Hook idea:** Bara taket. En person.

## 5. Design brief
- The hook under the body edge must be **clearly visible and in focus** — it is the evidence for the whole claim.
- The two call-outs point at real things in the photo. If the photo does not show the edge and the hook, the ad does not work.
- No faces, no people — including no hands fitting the cover. The claim is "one person can", not a demonstration with a model in it.
- **No time claim in minutes.** We have never measured one and will not invent one.
