## 1. Why this ad exists
SP carries 12 of the store's 20 purchases, and we do not know *which part* of the proof is doing the work: the human voice, or the number. Those are two different creative strategies going forward — one needs a stream of quotes from real customers, the other needs the review count to keep climbing. Splitting them now decides which one we invest in.

## 2. Hypothesis
The aggregate converts at least as well as the single quote, because the objection at this price is "does this actually fit and hold" and 16 consistent verdicts answer it more completely than one. If the quote wins instead, SP briefs become quote-led and collecting reviews with usable wording becomes a standing task.

**Hook idea:** 16 omdömen. Inte en stjärna under fem.
