## 1. Why this ad exists
SP is CaraShell's strongest angle by a distance — 42 % of spend, 12 of 20 purchases, CPA 266 kr against break-even 693 kr — and `SP_2_1` is the single ad carrying it. The cheapest way to learn something from a winner is to change exactly one thing about it. The parent's quote says "skyddar taket mot väder" while the photo shows no weather at all; the claim and the picture are arguing about different days.

## 2. Hypothesis
Showing the weather the quote names makes the same proof land harder: the reader sees the claim being true instead of reading that it is. If CPA holds at or below the parent's 169 kr, the scene is worth more than the neutral shot and every future SP image moves outdoors into weather.

**Hook idea:** Ett av 16 omdömen – alla fem stjärnor.

## 5. Design brief
- The rain has to be unmistakable at a glance — beading on the fabric, wet ground, grey light. A dry-looking photo defeats the whole point of the variant.
- No faces, no people.
- Keep the parent's proportions: the quote is the hero, the bottom line is small.
- The bottom line was rewritten once during briefing: the first draft ("Regnet står på taket. Inte på husvagnen.") read as the failure state, because the cover *is* on the roof. Do not revert to it.
