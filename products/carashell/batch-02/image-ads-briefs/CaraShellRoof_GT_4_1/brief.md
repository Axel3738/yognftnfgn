## 1. Why this ad exists
This answers the open question in DNA pattern 2: did the *gift angle* carry those purchases, or did that one film? In the source account all 14 GT purchases sit in a single video while the other three GT ads got 61 kr between them. In CaraShell's own account GT has 188 kr and **0 purchases** — far below the gate, so no verdict either way, only starvation (pattern 9).

Meanwhile images beat video 3,6× on CPA in this store. So the angle gets tested in the format that wins.

## 2. Hypothesis
The gift angle carries on its own, independent of the original film. If this static performs, GT is a real angle worth its own test-ABO budget; if it dies with spend behind it, GT was one good creative and should not be rebuilt.

## 5. Design brief
- The present is the hook: it must read as a gift at a glance, next to the vehicle it is for.
- Wrapping should be plain and neutral — no Christmas-specific iconography unless the season calls for it, and no brand names on the paper.
- One thought on the image. The caption carries the rest.
- Test it in a feed mock next to other ads before delivering.

## Hard rules
- Price **1 129 kr**, compare-at **1 469 kr**, discount **23 %**. No other numbers, ever.
- **Never claim:** storage bag, drawstring, product weight, exact caravan lengths.
- Guarantee is **14 dagars ångerrätt enligt svensk lag**.
- No false urgency: no "IDAG", no countdown, no stock shortage.
- No invented reviews or names. The count is 16, average 5,00.
