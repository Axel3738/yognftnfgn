**Parent / source:** Backlog #4, built on the product file's own `usp`: **"Bara taket. En person."** That line appears as a bullet in three of the four inherited angles and has **never been the hook**.

## 1. Why this ad exists
The product's sharpest differentiator has only ever been a supporting bullet. Every inherited angle opens on the problem, the price or a review — never on the thing that actually separates this from a full cover. This makes it second 0.

## 2. Hypothesis
Leading with the one-person install converts better than leading with the problem, because it answers the objection the avatar already holds about full covers (heavy, needs two people, scrapes the paint) before the problem is even stated.

**Hook idea:** Bara taket. En person.

3. **5–9 s** — wide, the fabric settling flat across the full roof.
4. **9–13 s** — side angle showing the edge hanging down over the side.
5. **13–18 s** — macro: the elastic strap stretching, the plastic hook catching under the body edge.
