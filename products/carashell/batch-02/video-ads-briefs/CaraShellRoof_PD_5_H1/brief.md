## 1. Why this ad exists
The product passes for both vehicle types and the product file says the copy should say both. In practice the account says "husvagn" everywhere. If motorhome owners are a real, separately addressable segment, this is the cheapest way to find out; if they are not, the ad dies and the guess is closed.

## 2. Hypothesis
Addressing the motorhome owner in their own word ("husbil") lifts CTR and CVR among that segment enough to beat the store's video average of CPA 476 kr. **Isolated variable: the vehicle word.** Everything else stays as close to the proven problem angle as possible.
