## Global rules — every video in this folder

**1. Price is exactly 299 kr, compare-at exactly 367 kr.** Never another number, never a
percentage other than 19 %.

**2. Product on screen before second 4.** No exceptions. A hook that talks for five seconds before
showing the cover loses the viewer we paid for.

**3. Swedish lines are copied word for word from the brief.** The left column is what gets
recorded and what gets burned in. Never translate the right column yourself, never rephrase, never
fix what looks like a typo — ask instead.

| Correct | Wrong |
|---|---|
| motorhölje | motorhölge, motorhölj |
| utombordare | utombordaren (when it should be indefinite) |
| hästkrafter / hk | HK, Hk |
| 6–250 hk (en dash) | 6-250 hk (hyphen) |
| 420D Oxfordtyg | 420d oxford, 420D-oxford |
| Bäverbutiken | Baverbutiken, Bäverbutiket |

**4. Captions are mandatory and word-for-word.** Burned in, Swedish, matching the voiceover
exactly — not auto-generated, not paraphrased. Most of this audience watches on mute. Å, Ä, Ö must
render; check the caption font before you start.

**5. Banned — never in voiceover, never in on-screen text.**
- Any customer count, rating or review number. No "Hundratals nöjda kunder". We have zero reviews.
- "innan vintern", "innan kylan", any winter or autumn deadline. It is August.
- "innan lagret tar slut". Only permitted urgency: **"så länge lagret räcker"**.
- Any invented testimonial, name, star rating or review quote.
- Any price or percentage other than 299 / 367 / 19 %.
- "vattentät" — it is **vattenavvisande**.
- Any figure for what an engine costs. "Dyr motor" is the ceiling.

**6. Exports.** Every video ships as **9:16 (1080×1920)** and **4:5 (1080×1350)**, H.264 MP4,
30 fps, under 100 MB, audio at −14 LUFS. Re-frame the 4:5 properly — do not centre-crop the 9:16
and hope the captions survive.

**7. Safe area.** Captions and key text inside the middle 80 % vertically.

**8. Colour.** Black cover only.

**9. Cut for retention.** Cut on the beat, never hold a static frame longer than ~2 s in the first
10 s, never open on a logo or a title card.

**10. Hook rate is a diagnostic, not a target.** In this account the ad with the *worst* hook rate
(24,5 %) returns the most profit per krona spent. Do not chase the hook number at the cost of the
rest of the ad.

**11. Do not optimise for clicks.** The ads that buy the cheapest clicks convert the worst here.
Fewer, better-qualified clicks is the goal.

**12. Deliver, then stop.** No extra variants, no music stingers, no "limited time" flashes the
brief does not ask for.
