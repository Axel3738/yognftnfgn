## 1. Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-06 teardown)

The product sells in six size ranges and **no creative has ever explained which one fits**. The
volume ad converts only 9,4 % of landing-page visitors into carts, while the best ads in the
account reach 17–20 %. Size confusion is the most plausible explanation and it has never been
tested properly: `Enginecover_PD_6_1`, the static version, received 28 kr of spend before the
budget went elsewhere, and `PD_6_C1` — meant to be this carousel — shipped as a single image.

This is the third attempt at the same question, and the first one built as an actual carousel.

**Hypothesis:** A swipeable size finder resolves fit inside the ad instead of on the product page, lifting LPV→ATC above 15 %. Carousel also gives Meta more creative surface per impression than one image.

> **Do not optimise this for clicks.** Across all nine judgeable ads in this account, the ads
> that buy the cheapest clicks convert the worst. A version of this ad that gets *fewer* clicks
> but a higher add-to-cart rate is a better ad. Cheap clicks are not an asset here.

## 3. Design brief

- Identical photo treatment on every card so the **size range is the only thing that changes** between them.
- Oversized hk numbers — they must be readable at 300 px wide. If a range does not fit, cut decoration, never a range.
- Black cover throughout.
- The six ranges must appear exactly as written in the table. Do not round, merge or re-group them differently.

## Hard rules — breaking any one makes the ad unusable

- **Price is exactly 299 kr, ordinary price exactly 367 kr.** Never another number, never a
  percentage other than 19 %.
- **Never claim a customer count, rating or review count.** No "hundratals nöjda kunder". We have
  zero verified reviews. This line is live on three ads right now and is being removed.
- **Never reference winter, cold or autumn.** No "innan vintern". It is August, boating season.
- **Never write "innan lagret tar slut".** The only permitted urgency phrase in this account is
  **"så länge lagret räcker"**, and only where the copy card below already contains it.
- **Water-repellent, never waterproof.** "Vattenavvisande", never "vattentät".
- **No invented testimonial, name, star rating or quote attributed to a person.**
- **Do not put a figure on what an engine costs.** "Dyr motor" is the ceiling.
- **Size ranges exactly as they exist in the store:** 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk ·
  60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Never round, merge or invent one.
- **Nobody rinses their outboard after every trip.** At most a wipe or a polish once a season.
  Never imply constant washing.
- **Black cover only.** Mint green and green have sold zero units.

**Spelling traps:** motorhölje · utombordare · hk · 6–250 hk (en dash) · 420D Oxfordtyg ·
Bäverbutiken. Å, Ä and Ö must render — check the font before you set a single word.

## 6. Primary KPI

**LPV→ATC, target above 15 %** against the volume ad's 9,4 %. Plus **card-level drop-off**, which tells us which engine sizes the responsive audience actually owns — a free read on our own market that we currently do not have.

## 7. What we learn regardless of outcome

Whether size confusion is the add-to-cart blocker. If it is, the fix belongs on the product page too, not only in ads.
