# Enginecover_PD_14_2 — "Three ways people protect the engine" (ranked list static)

**Type:** Educational comparison static. Reads as content rather than as an ad.
**Hypothesis:** An honest ranked list, including an honest word about the cheap options, earns trust and cheap attention → CPM below the campaign average of about 128 kr with CTR at or above 4 %.
**Kept:** The comparison argument from PD_14_1.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Format: ranked editorial list instead of a split image.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) (can also run as a 3-card carousel — if so, note "karusell" in Ads Manager) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** the overstock listicle page (see `docs/listicle-map.md`), NOT the plain product page

## Why this ad exists
Educational framing is untested in this account. It is also the natural creative to send to the listicle landing page, which currently receives no traffic at all.

## Design brief
- Three numbered blocks stacked, equal weight, editorial typography.
- Each block: rank label, one honest line.
- The product only wins on the third block. Being fair about options 1 and 2 is what makes the third credible.
- Product photo small at the bottom with the price.

Headline: `Tre sätt att skydda motorn`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr struck through. Verified 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

**Claim discipline:** be genuinely fair to options 1 and 2. Overstating their faults is what makes an ad read as an ad.

## Primary KPI
CPM and CTR (attention efficiency) first, then CPA. This is also the traffic test for the listicle landing page.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether educational framing lowers distribution cost in this niche, and whether the listicle page converts the traffic it gets.
