# Enginecover_SP_8_2 — SP angle as a quote-card (layout variant)

**VARIABELTAGGAR:** vinkel=`social proof/tillit` · hook-typ=`påstående` · format=`testimonial-statisk` · proof=`inget (produktclaim)` · offer-i-creativen=`ingen offer` · visuell stil=`grafik+produkt` · textmängd=`≤5 ord + underrad` · talare=`ingen`
*(Dessa taggar läses av nästa `/cs` för att gruppera vinstbidrag per variabelvärde. Ändra dem inte utan att ändra creativen.)*

**Type:** Static, layout sibling of SP_8_1.
**Hypothesis:** Quotation marks as a graphic device borrow the credibility of a testimonial without claiming one, lifting CTR above the plain headline layout while keeping the SP angle's conversion rate.
**Kept:** The SP angle, the claim and the guarantee.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Layout only: quote-card instead of headline block.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
The single judgeable static in the account (`Motorhölje_SO_2`) returns **1 117 kr profit per 1 000 kr spend** against **512 kr averaged across the videos** — and it runs the **exact same copy, headline and CTA** as `Motorhölje_SO_1_H1`, the weakest judgeable video (343 kr per 1 000 kr). Same words, more than triple the return. That makes format, not copy, the biggest untested lever in the account. One static is not proof, so this batch adds more.

Run as a pair with SP_8_1 to separate the angle from its presentation.

## Design brief
- Same photo family as SP_8_1 so the isolated variable stays clean.
- Large quotation marks around the claim in the upper third.
- Mechanism line small underneath, guarantee badge lower right.
- **No name, no face, no stars, no rating number anywhere.**

## Exact text (Swedish word-for-word, do not re-translate)
| Element | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Quote (graphic device) | ”Skyddar mot sol, regn och salt” | "Protects against sun, rain and salt" |
| Mechanism line | 420D Oxfordtyg, dragsko runt hela kanten | 420D Oxford fabric, drawstring around the whole edge |
| Badge | 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti | 30-day satisfaction guarantee |

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Byggt för att skydda, inte bara se bra ut.
> 420D Oxfordtyg möter dragsko runt hela kanten.
> Passar 6–250 hk.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti.
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Skyddar mot sol, regn och salt`

**No fabricated proof:** no customer names, no faces, no star ratings, no review counts. The account has no verified reviews.

**IMPORTANT:** the quotation marks frame a **product claim**. Attributing it to a named or pictured customer would be a fabricated testimonial.

**Use the black cover in the imagery.** Store data shows black carries almost all sales; two colours have sold nothing. Colour is not a selling angle here.

## Primary KPI
CTR against SP_8_1, CPA as the decider, click-to-purchase floor 5 %.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether quote styling adds credibility on its own — cheap to test, reusable across the store.
