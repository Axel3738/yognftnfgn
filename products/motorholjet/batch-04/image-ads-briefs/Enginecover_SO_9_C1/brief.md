# Enginecover_SO_9_C1 — Offer carousel (price, fit, mechanism, guarantee)

**Type:** **Carousel.** The full argument in four cards.
**Hypothesis:** Sequencing price → fit → mechanism → guarantee lets one ad do the work of four, and gives Meta more surface per impression → CPA ≤ 130 kr with a higher ATC rate than any single-image offer ad.
**Kept:** Every proven claim.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Format and sequence.

**Format:** Carousel, 4 cards, 1:1 · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists
The account has run price statics and fit messaging separately, never in sequence. Carousel is also completely untested here, so this doubles as a format test on the angle we understand best.

## Design brief
- Card 1: price block, hero treatment. Card 2: the six ranges. Card 3: fabric and drawstring close-ups. Card 4: guarantee badge and CTA.
- Consistent visual system so the swipe feels like one argument.
