# Enginecover_SO_12_2 — Overstock static, price-dominant variant

**Type:** Offer static, sibling of SO_12_1.
**Hypothesis:** With the reason-why reduced to one line and the price block dominant, the ad reads faster in feed and lifts CTR while keeping the credibility the reason-why buys → CPA ≤ 135 kr.
**Kept:** The overstock reason-why and the offer.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Hierarchy only: price block dominant, story reduced to a single line.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Simple**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Price block occupies the centre of the canvas: 367 kr struck through, 299 kr large.
- Story line sits above it in small caps; stock qualifier below.
- Product photo as background or inset, but the cover must stay clearly visible.

## Exact text (Swedish word-for-word, do not re-translate)
| Element | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Story line | Vi beställde för många | We ordered too many |
| Price, struck through | 367 kr | 367 kr (crossed out) |
| Price, hero | 299 kr | 299 kr |
| Line | Så länge lagret räcker | While stock lasts |

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Vi beställde för många motorhöljen.
> Nu är priset 299 kr istället för 367 kr.
> Så länge lagret räcker.
> 420D Oxfordtyg, passar 6–250 hk. 👇

Headline: `Lagerrea: 299 kr istället för 367 kr`

**Claim discipline:** same as SO_12_1. No percentage claims, no countdown.

**Landing page:** overstock listicle page.


## Primary KPI
CTR against SO_12_1 with CPA as the decider.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether the reason-why needs room to work or survives as a one-liner — sets the template for all future reason-why offers.
