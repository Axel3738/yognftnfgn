# Enginecover_PD_15_C1 — Objection carousel (one doubt per card)

**VARIABELTAGGAR:** vinkel=`passform/invändning` · hook-typ=`fråga` · format=`karusell` · proof=`demo` · offer-i-creativen=`pris syns (299)` · visuell stil=`grafik+produkt` · textmängd=`≤5 ord per kort` · talare=`ingen`
*(Dessa taggar läses av nästa `/cs` för att gruppera vinstbidrag per variabelvärde. Ändra dem inte utan att ändra creativen.)*

**Type:** **Carousel.** Objection handling as a format.
**Hypothesis:** Answering the five doubts in sequence before the click does the work the product page is currently failing to do, lifting LPV→ATC above 15 % and sending visitors who already decided.
**Kept:** All proven claims.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Structure: the ad becomes a Q&A instead of a pitch.

**Format:** Carousel, 5 cards, 1:1 · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
A buyer stalls for a reason. These are the five reasons the product plausibly stalls on, in the order they occur, and the card-level drop-off will tell us which one is real.

## Design brief
- Each card: the question as headline, the answer as subline, one supporting photo.
- Consistent layout so swiping feels like reading a list.
- Card 5 carries the price and CTA.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Fem frågor om motorhöljet, besvarade.
> Passar din motor, sitter kvar i vind.
> Tål sol, regn och salt.
> Enkel att sätta på och ta av.
> 299 kr. Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Dina frågor, besvarade`

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges exactly as they exist in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent.

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr as the struck-through ordinary price, verified in the store 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

**Use the black cover in the imagery.** Store data shows black carries almost all sales; two colours have sold nothing. Colour is not a selling angle here.

## Primary KPI
LPV→ATC (≥ 15 %) and card-level drop-off.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Which of the five doubts actually stops buyers. That answer feeds the product page, not just the next batch.
