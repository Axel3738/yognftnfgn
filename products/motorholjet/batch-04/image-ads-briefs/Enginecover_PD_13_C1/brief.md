# Enginecover_PD_13_C1 — Objection carousel (one doubt per card)

**Type:** **Carousel.** Objection handling as a format, never tried in this account.
**Hypothesis:** Answering the five doubts in sequence before the click removes the work the product page is currently failing to do → LPV→ATC above 15 % and a higher share of visitors who arrive already decided.
**Kept:** All the proven claims.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Structure: the ad becomes a Q&A instead of a pitch.

**Format:** Carousel, 5 cards, 1:1 · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists
The campaign's biggest leak is qualification, not attention. The volume ad buys the cheapest clicks in the account and converts only 9,2 % of landing-page visitors into carts, while the better ads reach 16-17 %. With six size ranges to choose from, the most likely blocker is that a buyer cannot tell which one fits their engine. A buyer stalls for a reason. These are the five reasons the product plausibly stalls on, answered in the order they occur.

## Design brief
- Each card: the question as headline, the answer as subline, one supporting photo.
- Consistent layout across cards so swiping feels like reading a list.
- Card 5 carries the price and the CTA.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Har du frågor innan du köper?
> Vi svarar på de vanligaste här.
> Passform, blåst, sol och salt, allt är löst.
> Svep igenom 👇

Headline: `Dina frågor om motorhöljet, besvarade`

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges exactly as they exist in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent.

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr struck through. Verified 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

## Primary KPI
LPV→ATC (≥ 15 %) and card-level drop-off — where people stop swiping tells us which objection is real.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Which of the five doubts actually stops buyers. That answer feeds the product page, not just the next batch.
