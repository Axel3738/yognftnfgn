# Enginecover_PD_6_2 — Big-engine static (speaks to where the buyers actually are)

**Type:** Static, segment-targeted. First creative in the account aimed at a specific engine class.
**Hypothesis:** The store's sales are concentrated in 40 hk and up, but every ad so far speaks to a generic "outboard owner". Addressing the bigger-engine owner directly should raise both CTR and ATC rate among the people who actually buy.
**Kept:** The protection message and the offer.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Audience framing: a stated engine class instead of "de flesta utombordare".

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists
Variant-level sales in the store cluster in the 40-60, 100-150 and 175-250 ranges. The 6-18 range is barely moving. No creative has ever reflected that. Speaking to the owner of a larger, more expensive engine also makes the value argument literally more true.

## Design brief
- Base: product photo on a **large** outboard, framed so the engine reads as substantial.
- Headline top, four size rows, value line, price and badge at the bottom.
- Avoid showing a small dinghy engine anywhere in this creative.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> En motor på 40 hk eller mer är en investering.
> Skydda den mot sol, regn och salt.
> 420D Oxfordtyg. Dragsko runt hela kanten.
> Passar 40–250 hk 👇

Headline: `Skydd byggt för stora motorer`

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges exactly as they exist in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent.

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr struck through. Verified 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

## Primary KPI
LPV→ATC and CPA. Watch reach: a narrower message may deliver less, which is acceptable if the ATC rate rises.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether segment-specific creative beats generic creative in a category where the buyer self-selects on a number.
