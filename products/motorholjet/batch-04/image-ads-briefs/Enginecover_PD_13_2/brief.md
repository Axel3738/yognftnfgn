# Enginecover_PD_13_2 — PD static with a qualifying line

**VARIABELTAGGAR:** vinkel=`problem/lösning + kvalificering` · hook-typ=`fråga` · format=`demo-statisk med callouts` · proof=`demo` · offer-i-creativen=`pris syns (299)` · visuell stil=`grafik+produkt` · textmängd=`rubrik+underrubrik` · talare=`ingen`
*(Dessa taggar läses av nästa `/cs` för att gruppera vinstbidrag per variabelvärde. Ändra dem inte utan att ändra creativen.)*

**Type:** Demo static, sibling of PD_13_1 with one line added.
**Hypothesis:** **Proven pattern:** the PD copy converts clicks at 2,3 % in both its ads while the SP copy reaches 6,4 % — identical conversion across two very different videos means the weakness is in the copy, not the edit. Adding a qualifying question at the top should raise click-to-purchase toward 4 % by making the wrong reader scroll past.
**Kept:** Everything in PD_13_1: layout, callouts, photo, price.
**Changed (isolated variable):** **One added line at the top:** a qualifying question naming the horsepower class where the store's sales actually are.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
The teardown's clearest proven pattern: PD copy buys the cheapest clicks in the account and converts them worst, and it does so identically across two completely different videos. That rules out the edit and points at the words. This is the cheapest possible fix to test.

## Design brief
- Identical to PD_13_1 in every respect except the added top line.
- The qualifying question is the first thing the eye hits, above the product.
- Avoid small dinghy engines in the photo: the store's sales are concentrated at 40 hk and up.

## Exact text (Swedish word-for-word, do not re-translate)
| Element | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Qualifying headline | Har du 40 hk eller mer? | Do you have 40 hp or more? |
| Callout, fabric | 420D Oxfordtyg | 420D Oxford fabric |
| Callout, drawstring | Dragsko runt hela kanten | Drawstring around the whole edge |
| Callout, fit | Passar 6–250 hk | Fits 6-250 hp |
| Callout, weather | Skyddar mot sol, regn och salt | Protects against sun, rain and salt |
| Price (small) | 299 kr | 299 kr |

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Kör du en utombordare på 40 hk eller mer?
> Då är det här ditt motorhölje.
> 420D Oxfordtyg, dragsko runt hela kanten.
> Skyddar mot sol, regn och salt.
> 299 kr. Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Har du 40 hk eller mer?`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr as the struck-through ordinary price, verified in the store 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges exactly as they exist in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent.

**Use the black cover in the imagery.** Store data shows black carries almost all sales; two colours have sold nothing. Colour is not a selling angle here.

## Primary KPI
**Click-to-purchase rate against PD_13_1** (target ≥ 4 % vs the PD copy's 2,3 %). Lower CTR with higher conversion is a win.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether a single qualifying line fixes the PD copy's click quality. If it does, the same line goes into the volume video's hook.
