# Enginecover_PD_15_1 — Water-test proof static (split)

**Type:** Proof static. Format transfer of `Enginecover_PD_7_H1` (torture-test video, early signal ROAS 6,17 on 56 kr — not yet judgeable).
**Hypothesis:** A visible before/after of the water test makes the protection claim self-evident in one glance → CTR ≥ 4 % with CVR at or above campaign average, because demonstrated proof beats described benefit for a product whose whole job is invisible.
**Kept:** The proof mechanism from PD_7_H1.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Medium: video → static.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Medium (requires one photo session: hose + outboard, plus a dry-cowling shot)**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Vertical 50/50 split.
- LEFT: water blasting the covered engine, droplets visibly running off the fabric. Label top-left.
- RIGHT: the cover pulled off, a hand on the dry cowling. Label top-right.
- Bottom strip: protection line, price, guarantee badge.
- **Honesty rule:** both halves must be the same engine, same session. No compositing, no stock water effects.
