**Type:** Static in the SP angle. **The SP copy converts clicks at 6,4 %, nearly triple the PD copy's 2,3 %**, but it has only ever run as one video.
**Hypothesis:** The SP angle's conversion power is a copy property, not a video property. As a static it should hold click-to-purchase near 5-6 % and land CPA under 120 kr, while costing a fraction to produce.
**Kept:** The SP trust angle and the guarantee close.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Format: video → static. The unverifiable customer-count line is dropped for good.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
The single judgeable static in the account (`Motorhölje_SO_2`) returns **964 kr profit per 1 000 kr spend** against **504 kr for the videos** — and it runs the **exact same copy, headline and CTA** as `Motorhölje_SO_1_H1`, the weakest judgeable video (343 kr per 1 000 kr). Same words, roughly triple the return. That makes format, not copy, the biggest untested lever in the account. One static is not proof, so this batch adds more.

And separately: the SP copy is the account's best converter per click but has run on exactly one asset. It is the most under-supplied thing we own.

## Design brief
- Base: the black cover on an outboard with boat and water visible, so the trust claim has a real setting.
- Headline across the upper third, mechanism line beneath, guarantee badge lower right.
- Product sharp and unmistakable.
