**Type:** Demo static. The PD copy carries two thirds of the account's spend and 59 % of its profit but has never run as a static.
**Hypothesis:** The PD angle works as a static and inherits the format advantage, giving profit per krona above the PD videos' 435 kr per 1 000 kr.
**Kept:** The PD problem-first angle.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Format: video → static with four annotated callouts.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
The single judgeable static in the account (`Motorhölje_SO_2`) returns **964 kr profit per 1 000 kr spend** against **504 kr for the videos** — and it runs the **exact same copy, headline and CTA** as `Motorhölje_SO_1_H1`, the weakest judgeable video (343 kr per 1 000 kr). Same words, roughly triple the return. That makes format, not copy, the biggest untested lever in the account. One static is not proof, so this batch adds more.

The PD angle is the profit engine but exists only as video. If the format advantage is real, this is where it is worth the most.

## Design brief
- Base: sharpest on-engine photo of the black cover.
- Four thin callout lines pointing at: fabric surface, drawstring edge, engine size, weather.
- Small price bottom corner. No other decoration.

Headline: `Motorhölje med fyra fördelar`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr as the struck-through ordinary price, verified in the store 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges exactly as they exist in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent.

**Use the black cover in the imagery.** Store data shows black carries almost all sales; two colours have sold nothing. Colour is not a selling angle here.
