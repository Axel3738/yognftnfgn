**Type:** Static in the SP angle, the account's best-converting angle.
**Hypothesis:** The SP angle's conversion power (10,9 % of landing-page visitors buy, against 4,4 % for the volume ad) survives in static form → CPA under 135 kr at a fraction of video production cost.

**Changed (isolated variable):** Format: video → static.

**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists
The SP angle converts best and is the least supplied with assets. Its earlier static attempts got almost no delivery, so the angle is effectively untested in static form.

## Design brief
- Base: on-engine product photo with boat and water visible.
- The claim set large in the upper third, with quotation marks as a **graphic device only**.
- Mechanism line small beneath, guarantee badge lower right.
