# Enginecover_SP_10_1 — Trust static (SP angle, sharper than SP_4)

**Type:** Trust static. Second iteration of the SP angle as a static; `Enginecover_SP_4_1/2` got almost no delivery in batch #2 (5–12 kr), so the angle is untested in static form.
**Hypothesis:** The SP angle's conversion power (CPA 116,79 kr in video, ~2× the campaign's LPV→purchase rate) survives in static form when the wind claim is the headline → CPA ≤ 135 kr at a fraction of production cost.
**Kept:** The SP angle: trust plus the stays-on-in-wind claim, guarantee as proof.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Format (video → static) and a sharper, shorter headline than SP_4_1.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Simple**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Base: on-engine product photo with visible boat/water context, ideally with some movement in the water so "blåst" reads visually.
- Headline across the upper third, mechanism line under it, guarantee badge lower right.
- Product must be clearly visible and in focus.

> Ett motorhölje båtägare litar på.

> Sitter kvar även i kraftig blåst.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti. 👇

Headline: `Håller kvar i blåst, garanterat`

**IMPORTANT:** no customer name, star rating or review count — we have no verified reviews. This is a product claim, not a testimonial. The moment verified reviews exist, version 2 swaps in a real quote.


## Primary KPI
CPA (≤ 135 kr) with CVR check.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether the SP angle is angle-driven or video-driven — the same question SP_4 was meant to answer but never got budget to.
