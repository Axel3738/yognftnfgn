# Enginecover_PD_16_1 — Three-step demo static (ten seconds)

**Type:** Demo static. Rebuild of the callout idea from `Enginecover_PD_8_1`, which got 21 kr of delivery in batch #2 and never became judgeable.
**Hypothesis:** Showing the whole job as three numbered steps answers "is this a hassle" at a glance and lifts CTR ≥ 4 %, because ease of use is the friction-remover that both winning copy batches lean on.
**Kept:** The PD demo angle and the proven ease-of-use messaging.
**Changed (isolated variable):** From four static callouts to a three-step sequence with an explicit time promise.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Simple**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Three numbered panels across (or stacked on 4:5): step 1 pulling the cover over the cowling, step 2 hands tightening the drawstring, step 3 the finished covered engine.
- Oversized numerals 1 2 3. Arrow or thin divider between panels.
- Bottom strip: ease line, product name, price.
- All three photos from the same engine and session so the sequence reads as one continuous job.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Tre steg, klart på tio sekunder.
> Dra höljet över kåpan.
> Dra åt dragskon runt hela kanten.
> 299 kr istället för 367 kr, så länge lagret räcker. 👇

Headline: `På och av på tio sekunder`

**Claim discipline:** "tio sekunder" must be achievable on camera. If the shoot shows it takes longer, change the number in the image, the copy and the headline.


## Primary KPI
CTR (≥ 4 %) → CPA.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether sequence-style statics can stand in for demo video — the cheapest possible creative-volume lever if it works.
