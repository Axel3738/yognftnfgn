# Enginecover_SO_10_2 — Colour-choice static (same offer, different hero element)

**Type:** Offer static, sibling of SO_10_1. Isolates layout hierarchy against the same offer.
**Hypothesis:** Leading with the five colour options instead of the price attracts buyers who hesitate on fit/looks rather than price, giving a different (and possibly cheaper) slice of the audience at CPA ≤ 135 kr.
**Kept:** Offer, guarantee, product photo family, ad copy structure.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Hero element only: colour range instead of price block.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Simple**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Base: the five colour variants shown as five covers (or five swatches beside one hero cover): Svart, Blå, Grå, Grön, Mintgrön.
- Colour row occupies the upper two thirds; price sits small at the bottom next to the guarantee badge.
- Product must be clearly recognisable as an engine cover, not abstract swatches.

Headline: `Motorhölje i fem färger, 299 kr`

**Variant check:** only show colours that are actually in stock in the store.


## Primary KPI
CPA with CTR comparison against SO_10_1 (same offer, different hero).

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether price or choice is the stronger hero element for this audience — reusable across every variant-rich product in the store.
