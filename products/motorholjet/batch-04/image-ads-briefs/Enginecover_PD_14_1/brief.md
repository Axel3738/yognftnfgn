**Type:** Comparison static against what boat owners actually do today. No competitor brand is named.
**Hypothesis:** Every ad so far argues against doing nothing. Arguing against the tarp instead reframes the purchase as a choice rather than an expense, which should lift LPV→ATC above 13 %.
**Kept:** The weather-protection message.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The frame: comparison instead of description.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Medium** (needs one honest photo of a tarp on an engine)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
An unclaimed argument, and winnable on the mechanism: a tarp has no drawstring, so it flaps, rubs and lets water in. Nothing in the account has ever made this case.

## Design brief
- Vertical 50/50 split. LEFT: a real tarp on a real engine, loose. RIGHT: the fitted black cover, drawstring visible.
- Labels and three short lines per side.
- **Fairness rule:** photograph a normal tarp in normal conditions. Do not stage it torn, undersized or deliberately badly fitted. The argument only works if the comparison is honest.
