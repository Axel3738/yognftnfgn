# Enginecover_PD_14_1 — Tarp versus cover (split static)

**Type:** Comparison static against the real alternative. No competitor product is named.
**Hypothesis:** Framing the purchase as a choice between two protection methods, rather than as an expense, raises LPV→ATC above 13 %.
**Kept:** The weather-protection message.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Frame: comparison instead of description.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Medium** (needs one photo of a tarp on an engine)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists
Every ad so far argues against doing nothing. Nobody has argued against the tarp, which is what many owners actually use. It is an unclaimed argument and it is winnable on the mechanism: a tarp has no drawstring.

## Design brief
- Vertical 50/50 split. LEFT: a real tarp on a real engine, loose and rucked. RIGHT: the fitted cover, drawstring visible.
- Labels top-left and top-right, captions beneath each half.
- Bottom strip: fabric claim, price, guarantee.
- **Fairness rule:** photograph a normal tarp in normal conditions. Do not stage it torn, undersized or deliberately badly fitted.
