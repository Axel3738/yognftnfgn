# Enginecover_SO_12_1 — Overstock static (matches the listicle landing page)

**Type:** Offer static with a true reason-why. Built to match the overstock landing page (`docs/listicle-map.md`).
**Hypothesis:** A stated, honest reason for the price ("we over-ordered") converts better than an unexplained discount and avoids the curiosity-click failure of `Motorhölje_SO_1_H1` (CPA 175,62 kr), because a believable reason removes the "what is the catch" hesitation.
**Kept:** Real price proof (367 → 299 kr) and the guarantee.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The reason-why: stock overage instead of an unexplained "kampanjpris".

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Simple**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Base: warehouse-flavoured or stacked-product shot if available, otherwise the standard hero product photo.
- Story line at the top, price line under it, stock qualifier at the bottom. Plain and un-hyped: the honesty IS the creative.
- No countdown timers, no flame icons, no "sista chansen" styling.

## Exact text (Swedish word-for-word, do not re-translate)
| Element | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Vi beställde för många | We ordered too many |
| Line 1 | Därför 299 kr istället för 367 kr | So 299 kr instead of 367 kr |
| Line 2 | Så länge lagret räcker | While stock lasts |
| Badge | 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti | 30-day satisfaction guarantee |

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Vi beställde in för många motorhöljen.
> Nu säljer vi dem för 299 kr istället för 367 kr.
> 420D Oxfordtyg, passar 6–250 hk.
> Gäller så länge lagret räcker. 👇

Headline: `299 kr istället för 367 kr`

**Claim discipline:** "så länge lagret räcker" is the ONLY urgency allowed and it must be true. Never "innan lagret tar slut" (the store is on continue-selling), never a countdown, never a discount percentage — 299/367 is roughly 19 %, so any "30 %" style claim would be false.

**Landing page:** send this ad to the overstock listicle page, not the plain product page, so the story matches.


## Primary KPI
CPA (≤ 135 kr) with CVR check — high CTR alone is the SO_1_H1 trap and counts as a fail.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether a stated reason-why rescues the offer angle that failed without one — the single most important open question in the SO batch.
