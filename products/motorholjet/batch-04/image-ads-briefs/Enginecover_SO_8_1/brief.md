# Enginecover_SO_8_1 — Offer static WITH the real numbers

**VARIABELTAGGAR:** vinkel=`offer/kostnad` · hook-typ=`siffra/pris` · format=`offer-grafik statisk` · proof=`inget` · offer-i-creativen=`pris syns (367→299)` · visuell stil=`grafik+produkt` · textmängd=`rubrik+underrubrik` · talare=`ingen`
*(Dessa taggar läses av nästa `/cs` för att gruppera vinstbidrag per variabelvärde. Ändra dem inte utan att ändra creativen.)*

**Type:** Offer static. Half of a controlled pair with `Enginecover_SO_8_2` that isolates one variable: whether showing the actual numbers in the image matters.
**Hypothesis:** Showing 367 kr struck through and 299 kr large converts better than claiming a campaign price without numbers, holding CPA at or under SO_2's 120 kr at higher spend.
**Kept:** The SO offer angle that SO_2 already proved at 12 purchases.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The numbers become visible in the image. **This is the pair's only difference.**

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
The single judgeable static in the account (`Motorhölje_SO_2`) returns **1 117 kr profit per 1 000 kr spend** against **512 kr averaged across the videos** — and it runs the **exact same copy, headline and CTA** as `Motorhölje_SO_1_H1`, the weakest judgeable video (343 kr per 1 000 kr). Same words, more than triple the return. That makes format, not copy, the biggest untested lever in the account. One static is not proof, so this batch adds more.

On top of that: **no judgeable ad in this account has ever shown the actual price in the creative.** A previous DNA note claimed SO_2 had "pris i bild" — that was never verified and the image cannot be opened from this environment, so it is removed as unfounded. This pair tests the variable properly for the first time.

## Design brief
- Base: hero product photo of the black cover on an outboard.
- Price block is the hero element: 367 kr struck through, 299 kr at roughly double its size.
- Kicker above, three reassurance lines and the guarantee badge below.
- Test legibility at 300 px wide.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Motorhöljet till din utombordare.
> 420D Oxfordtyg som skyddar mot sol, regn och salt.
> Dragsko runt hela kanten håller det på plats.
> Nu 299 kr, ordinarie pris 367 kr.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti. Handla nu 👇

Headline: `299 kr, ordinarie pris 367 kr`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr as the struck-through ordinary price, verified in the store 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

**Use the black cover in the imagery.** Store data shows black carries almost all sales; two colours have sold nothing. Colour is not a selling angle here.

## Primary KPI
CPA against SO_2's 111,45 kr, and **head-to-head against SO_8_2**. The pair must run in the same ad set with comparable budget or the test is void.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether visible price proof is worth anything, or whether the offer angle works on the claim alone. Either answer changes every offer creative we make.
