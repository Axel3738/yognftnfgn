# Enginecover_SO_8_1 — Overstock static (matches the listicle landing page)

**Type:** Offer static with a true reason-why.
**Hypothesis:** A stated, honest reason for the price converts better than an unexplained discount → CPA ≤ 130 kr, because it removes the "what is the catch" hesitation.
**Kept:** Real price proof and the guarantee.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The reason-why replaces manufactured urgency.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** the overstock listicle page (see `docs/listicle-map.md`), NOT the plain product page

## Why this ad exists
The offer ad that only claimed a "kampanjpris" without numbers produced the account's worst CPA at 175,66 kr on high CTR: curiosity clicks. The ones showing real numbers are among the best. What none of them had was a reason the price exists.

## Design brief
- Eyebrow line top, headline, then the price block.
- Plain and un-hyped: the honesty is the creative. No countdown, no flames, no "sista chansen" styling.
- Product photo supporting, guarantee badge bottom.

## Exact text (Swedish word-for-word, do not re-translate)
| Element | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |

| Eyebrow | Vi beställde för många | We ordered too many |
| Headline | Lagret är fullt, priset är sänkt | The warehouse is full, the price is cut |
| Price, struck | 367 kr | 367 kr (crossed out) |
| Price, hero | 299 kr | 299 kr |
| Qualifier | Så länge lagret räcker | While stock lasts |
| Feature 1 | 420D Oxfordtyg | 420D Oxford fabric |
| Feature 2 | Dragsko runt hela kanten | Drawstring around the whole edge |
| Badge | 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti | 30-day satisfaction guarantee |

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Vi köpte in för många motorhöljen.
> Lagret är fullt, så priset går ner.
> 299 kr i stället för 367 kr.
> 420D Oxfordtyg. Dragsko runt hela kanten.
> Så länge lagret räcker 👇

Headline: `Överfullt lager: 299 kr`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr struck through. Verified 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

**Urgency discipline:** "så länge lagret räcker" is the only urgency allowed and it must be true. Never "innan lagret tar slut" (the store is on continue-selling), never a countdown.

## Primary KPI
CPA (≤ 130 kr) with CVR check. High CTR alone is a fail.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether the reason-why is what the offer angle was missing.
