# Enginecover_PD_15_2 — Water-test proof static, text-heavy variant

**Type:** Proof static, sibling of PD_15_1. Text-led layout.
**Hypothesis:** Stating the test as a bold claim before the eye reaches the photo earns the stop in feed, lifting CTR above PD_15_1 while keeping the same proof.
**Kept:** The water-test proof and the offer.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Layout only: text-led instead of image-led.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Medium (same photo session as PD_15_1)**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Top two thirds: the claim set in large condensed type on a clean or dimmed-photo background.
- Bottom third: the product photo (covered engine) small, plus the price line.
- The number in the claim is a placeholder: **replace [X] with the amount of water actually used in the shoot, in both the image and the ad copy.**
