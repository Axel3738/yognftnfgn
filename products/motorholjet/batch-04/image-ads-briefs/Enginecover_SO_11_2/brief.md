# Enginecover_SO_11_2 — Guarantee-first static (risk reversal as hero)

**Type:** Risk-reversal static, sibling of SO_11_1.
**Hypothesis:** Leading with the 30-day guarantee removes the "will it even fit my engine" objection before price is considered, lifting LPV→purchase above the campaign average of ~6 % even if CTR drops.
**Kept:** Insurance/value framing in the supporting line, offer, product photo.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Hero element only: guarantee badge instead of value claim.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Simple**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Base: same product photo family as SO_11_1 for a clean read of the isolated variable.
- The guarantee badge is the largest graphic element, top or centre. Main line sits beside/under it.
- Price small at the bottom.

## Exact text (Swedish word-for-word, do not re-translate)
| Element | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Main line | Testa utan risk i 30 dagar | Try risk-free for 30 days |
| Badge (dominant) | 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti | 30-day satisfaction guarantee |
| Support line | Motorn är värd tiotusentals kronor | The engine is worth tens of thousands of kronor |
| Price (secondary) | 299 kr (ord. 367 kr) | 299 kr (was 367 kr) |

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Skydda motorn utan att riskera något.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti, enkelt och tryggt.
> Motorn är värd tiotusentals kronor, höljet knappt något.
> 299 kr istället för 367 kr, så länge lagret räcker. 👇

Headline: `Testa riskfritt i 30 dagar`

**Claim discipline:** the guarantee is 30 days, satisfaction-based. Do not upgrade it to a warranty, a lifetime guarantee or a fit guarantee.


## Primary KPI
LPV→purchase CVR (≥ 6 %) first, CPA second.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether risk reversal outperforms value framing as the opening move — informs every high-consideration product in the store.
