# Enginecover_SP_10_2 — Trust quote-card (graphic quotation marks, no attribution)

**Type:** Trust static, sibling of SP_10_1. Quote-card layout.
**Hypothesis:** Quotation marks as a graphic device borrow the credibility of a testimonial without claiming one, lifting CTR above the plain claim layout while keeping CVR.
**Kept:** The SP claim and guarantee.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Layout only: quote-card instead of headline block.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350). Production level: **Simple**.
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP · **CTA:** Handla nu

## Design brief
- Base: same on-engine photo family as SP_10_1 so the isolated variable stays clean.
- Large quotation marks as a graphic element around the claim, upper third.
- Mechanism line small underneath, guarantee badge lower right.
- **No name, no photo of a person, no stars, no rating number anywhere.**

## Exact text (Swedish word-for-word, do not re-translate)
| Element | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Quote (graphic element) | ”Sitter kvar oavsett väder” | "Stays on no matter the weather" |
| Sub line (mechanism) | Dragsko runt hela kanten | Drawstring around the whole edge |
| Badge | 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti | 30-day satisfaction guarantee |

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> ”Sitter kvar oavsett väder.”
> Dragsko runt hela kanten håller höljet på plats.
> 420D Oxfordtyg, tåligt i alla väder.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti. 👇

Headline: `Sitter kvar oavsett väder`

**IMPORTANT:** the quotation marks are a design device around a product claim. Attributing it to a named or pictured customer without a verified review would be a fabricated testimonial — do not do it.


## Primary KPI
CTR against SP_10_1, CPA as decider, CVR floor ≥ 5 %.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether quote styling adds credibility on its own — cheap to test, reusable across the store.
