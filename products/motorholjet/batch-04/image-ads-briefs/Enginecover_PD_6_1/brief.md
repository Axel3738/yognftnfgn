# Enginecover_PD_6_1 — Size guide static (the add-to-cart leak)

**VARIABELTAGGAR:** vinkel=`passform/invändning` · hook-typ=`påstående` · format=`listicle-bild` · proof=`inget` · offer-i-creativen=`pris syns (299)` · visuell stil=`grafik+produkt` · textmängd=`lång listicle` · talare=`ingen`
*(Dessa taggar läses av nästa `/cs` för att gruppera vinstbidrag per variabelvärde. Ändra dem inte utan att ändra creativen.)*

**Type:** Educational static. First creative in the account that answers the size question.
**Hypothesis:** The volume ad converts only 9,2 % of landing-page visitors into carts while better ads reach 16-17 %. With six size ranges and no ad explaining them, size confusion is the most likely blocker. Showing the ranges should lift LPV→ATC above 15 %.
**Kept:** The proven fit claim.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The ad's job: qualify instead of persuade.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists (from the 2026-08-05 teardown)
The add-to-cart step is where the funnel actually leaks, and the product has six size options that no creative has ever explained. This is the cheapest test of that diagnosis.

## Design brief
- Product photo top third, size table bottom two thirds.
- Six rows, generous spacing. The numbers are the design.
- Price at the bottom.
- If a range is unreadable at 300 px, cut decoration, not rows.

## Ad copy

> Sex storlekar, ett motorhölje för alla.
> Passar 6–250 hk.
> 420D Oxfordtyg med dragsko runt hela kanten.
> 299 kr.
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Hitta din storlek`

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges exactly as they exist in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent.

**Price integrity:** 299 kr with 367 kr as the struck-through ordinary price, verified in the store 2026-08-05. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is about 19 %).

**Use the black cover in the imagery.** Store data shows black carries almost all sales; two colours have sold nothing. Colour is not a selling angle here.

## Primary KPI
**LPV→ATC rate** (target ≥ 15 %, the volume ad sits at 9,2 %). CPA second.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether size confusion is the add-to-cart blocker. If yes, the fix belongs on the product page too, not just in ads.
