# Enginecover_PD_6_1 — Size guide static

**Type:** Static size guide. Single-image counterpart to the carousel, so we can read format against content.
**Hypothesis:** All six ranges in one glance resolves the fit question without a swipe, giving the same ATC lift as the carousel at lower production effort.
**Kept:** The fit claim and the offer.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Format: everything on one canvas instead of seven cards.

**Format:** Static, 1:1 (1080×1080) + 4:5 (1080×1350) · **Production level:** **Simple**
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd

## Why this ad exists
The campaign's biggest leak is qualification, not attention. The volume ad buys the cheapest clicks in the account and converts only 9,2 % of landing-page visitors into carts, while the better ads reach 16-17 %. With six size ranges to choose from, the most likely blocker is that a buyer cannot tell which one fits their engine. This is the cheapest possible test of whether simply showing the ranges is enough.

## Design brief
- Product photo top third, size table bottom two thirds.
- Six rows, one per range, generous spacing. The numbers are the design.
- Price and guarantee as a bottom strip.
- Test at 300 px wide: if a range is unreadable, cut the decorative elements, not the rows.
