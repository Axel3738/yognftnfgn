# Enginecover_PD_6_H1 — Find your engine size in ten seconds

**Type:** New concept, funnel-repair. Answers the size question **before** the click.
**Hypothesis:** Only 9,2 % of the volume ad's landing-page visitors add to cart, against 16-17 % for the better ads. The most likely blocker is that a buyer cannot tell which of six sizes fits their engine. Showing an owner finding the hp number on their own cowling should raise LPV→ATC above 15 % and cut wasted clicks.
**Kept:** Nothing — this is a new asset.
**Changed (isolated variable):** n/a (new concept).

**Format:** Video 20 s, 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** **Medium** (one filming session at a boat, phone camera fine)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje PD · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd
**Source footage:** newly filmed.

## Why this ad exists
The campaign's biggest leak is not attention, it is qualification. The volume ad buys the cheapest clicks in the account (6,65 kr per outbound click) and converts them worst (4,4 % of landing-page visitors buy, against 10,9 % for the social-proof ad). This ad deliberately trades reach for fit: an owner who has just read their own hp number off the cowling is a qualified click.

**Voiceover:** none. Overlays only.

**Filming note:** the hp number must be a real rating plate on a real engine. If the engine filmed is not a 115 hk, change the number in the overlay to whatever is actually on the plate and change the highlighted range to match.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Vet du hur många hk din motor har?
> Den står oftast på kåpan.
> Matcha numret med rätt storlek på baverbutiken.se.
> Dragsko runt hela kanten, perfekt passform 👇

Headline: `Hitta din motors hk på 10 sek`

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges are exactly as they appear in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent ranges beyond what a brief explicitly states.

## Primary KPI
LPV→ATC rate (target ≥ 15 %, campaign volume ad is 9,2 %). Secondary: CPA. A lower CTR with a higher ATC rate is a **win**, not a loss.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether pre-qualifying on size fixes the add-to-cart leak. If it does, the same treatment applies to every multi-variant product in the store.
