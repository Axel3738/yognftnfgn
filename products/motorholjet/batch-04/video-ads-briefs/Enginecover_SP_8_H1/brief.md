# Enginecover_SP_8_H1 — UGC: "I have a 115 hk" (owner names their own engine)

**Type:** Creator UGC in the SP angle, the account's best-converting angle (10,9 % of landing-page visitors buy, against 4,4 % for the volume ad).
**Hypothesis:** A real owner naming a specific engine size out loud lets a viewer with a similar engine self-identify, which both qualifies the click and answers the fit doubt → LPV→purchase above 10 % with LPV→ATC above 15 %.
**Kept:** The SP angle and the guarantee close.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The proof is a stated, specific engine size rather than a general trust claim.

**Format:** Video 25 s, filmed 9:16, delivered 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** **Advanced** (creator who genuinely owns an outboard in the 40-250 hk range)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd
**Source footage:** newly filmed by the creator.

## Why this ad exists
Most buyers in this account own engines of 40 hk and up (the store's sales are concentrated in the 40-60, 100-150 and 175-250 ranges). Ads have never spoken to that owner specifically. A creator saying "jag har en 115 hk" is the cheapest possible way to make that owner feel addressed.

**Creator direction:** 35-65, genuinely owns the boat and engine. Dry and matter-of-fact, never salesy. Product in hand or on the engine within the first 4 seconds. Jump cuts on beat changes, captions always on.

**BLOCKER:** the engine size the creator names must be their real engine. If they own a 60 hk, change every number in the script and the ad copy to 60 hk and the 60-90 range. Never have a creator state a size they do not own.

**No fabricated proof:** no customer names, no star ratings, no review counts. We have no verified reviews.

LPV→purchase (> 10 %) and LPV→ATC (≥ 15 %). Hold p50 as diagnosis.

Whether specificity (a named engine size) outperforms general trust language in the angle that already converts best.
