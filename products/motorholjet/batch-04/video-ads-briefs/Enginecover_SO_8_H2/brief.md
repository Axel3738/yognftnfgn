## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Din motor är värd mycket mer än 299 kr.
> Se motorhöljet som en billig försäkring för den.
> 420D Oxfordtyg. Skyddar mot sol, regn och salt.
> 299 kr (ord. 367 kr). 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti.
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Billig försäkring för din motor`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr / 367 kr verified against the store. If the store price changes, update BOTH numbers before launch. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is ~19 %, so any "30 %" claim would be false).

**Wording note:** "som en billig försäkring" keeps the framing clearly metaphorical. Never imply the cover IS an insurance policy, and never state a figure for the engine's value.

## Editing direction
- Calm, matter-of-fact read. No hard sell on the price line.
- Overlays mirror the voiceover keywords only.

## Primary KPI
CPA (< 110 kr) with CVR floor ≥ 6 %.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether value reframing can carry the offer angle without discounts — critical for margin when the 367→299 price eventually ends.
