# Enginecover_SO_10_H1 — Retargeting: risk reversal for the hesitant

**Type:** Retargeting video, pure risk reversal.
**Hypothesis:** Someone who added to cart and did not buy has one unresolved doubt. Leading with the guarantee and the fit range removes it without pressure → recovered purchases at a CPA well under the cold-traffic average.
**Kept:** The proven guarantee and fit claims.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Audience and framing: speaks to someone who has already seen the product.

**Format:** Video 15 s, 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** **Simple** (re-edit of existing footage + overlays)
**Ad set:** **New retargeting ad set required** (add-to-cart last 14-30 days, excluding purchasers) · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd
**Source footage:** `Motorhölje_PD_EXTRA` video ID **2210430979804731** (CPA 97,07 kr, CTR 5,46 %, hook 41,4 %).

## Why this ad exists
The campaign is 100 % cold prospecting. The volume ad alone generated 177 add-to-carts and 84 purchases, so roughly 93 carts were left behind by that ad alone, and nothing in the account speaks to them. This is the cheapest unclaimed volume available.

**Voiceover:** none. Calm pacing, no urgency music.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Fortfarande osäker på passformen?
> Dragsko runt hela kanten. Passar 6–250 hk.
> Passar den inte din motor får du pengarna tillbaka.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti. 👇

Headline: `Osäker på passformen? Testa riskfritt.`

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges are exactly as they appear in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent ranges beyond what a brief explicitly states.

**Claim discipline:** verify the return policy wording before launch. "Får du pengarna tillbaka" must match what the store actually does. Never imply we know what is in someone's cart, never invent a deadline.

## Primary KPI
CPA on the retargeting audience (should be well under 135 kr) and recovered add-to-carts. If the audience is too small to spend, report that rather than widening it.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether the account has been leaving cheap volume on the table by running cold traffic only.
