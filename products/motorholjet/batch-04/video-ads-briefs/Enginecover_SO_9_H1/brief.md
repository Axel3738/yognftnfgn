# Enginecover_SO_9_H1 — Overstock story (matches the listicle landing page, backlog B3)

**Type:** New offer concept with a true reason-why. Built to match the overstock landing page (`docs/listicle-map.md`) — pulled from `backlog.md` item B3.
**Hypothesis:** An honest, stated reason for the price converts better than an unexplained discount → CPA ≤ 120 kr with CVR ≥ 6 %, because a believable reason removes the "what is the catch" hesitation that unexplained discounts create.
**Kept:** Real price proof and the guarantee.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The reason-why: stock overage, stated plainly, replacing manufactured urgency.

**Format:** Video 20 s, 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** **Medium** (warehouse footage or stacked-product shot + voiceover)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu
**Source footage:** New warehouse/stock footage if available, otherwise `Motorhölje_PD_EXTRA` video ID **2210430979804731** (best ad in the account: CPA 96,59 kr, ROAS 4,18, CTR 5,48 %, hook 41,4 %, completion 10,3 %).

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Vi beställde för många motorhöljen.
> Nu är lagret fullt, så priset sänks.
> 299 kr istället för 367 kr, så länge lagret räcker.
> 420D Oxfordtyg. 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti.
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Lagret är fullt. 299 kr just nu`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr / 367 kr verified against the store. If the store price changes, update BOTH numbers before launch. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is ~19 %, so any "30 %" claim would be false).

**Claim discipline:** the overstock reason must be TRUE. "Så länge lagret räcker" is the only urgency allowed. Never "innan lagret tar slut" (the store is on continue-selling), never a countdown, never a percentage.

**Landing page:** send this ad to the overstock listicle page, not the plain product page, so the ad and the page tell the same story.

## Editing direction
- Plain and un-hyped. No countdown graphics, no flame icons, no shouting.
- If no warehouse footage exists, a simple stacked-product shot on a table works; do not fake a warehouse.

## Primary KPI
CPA (≤ 120 kr) with CVR check. Compare directly against SO_8_H1 (same offer, no reason-why).

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether a stated reason-why beats a bare discount — the single most important open question in the SO batch, and it decides the LP strategy too.
