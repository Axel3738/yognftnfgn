## Ad copy
Primary text:
> En ny kåpa kostar tusenlappar.
> Det här kostar 299 kr (ord. 367 kr).
> 420D Oxfordtyg. Skyddar mot sol, regn och salt.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti.
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Kåpa: tusenlappar. Det här: 299 kr`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr / 367 kr verified against the store. If the store price changes, update BOTH numbers before launch. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is ~19 %, so any "30 %" claim would be false).

**Claim discipline:** "tusenlappar" stays deliberately unspecific. Do NOT put a figure on a replacement cowling in the overlay, the copy or the headline — we have no verified source for that price. If a sourced figure ever exists, it can replace the vague wording, not before.

## Editing direction
- The weathered-cowling shot must be a real weathered engine, not our product darkened or retouched.
- Price graphic must be readable at 300 px wide.

## Primary KPI
CPA (< 110 kr at 500 kr spend) with CVR check (LPV→purchase ≥ 6 %). High CTR alone = fail.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether a cost-of-replacement frame rescues the offer angle that failed on urgency alone.
