> Två motorer, samma brygga, samma sommar.

> Min har haft det här höljet på hela säsongen.
> 420D Oxfordtyg, dragsko runt hela kanten.
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Skillnaden syns efter en sommar`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr / 367 kr verified against the store. If the store price changes, update BOTH numbers before launch. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is ~19 %, so any "30 %" claim would be false).

**Honesty rule:** NEVER film an identifiable neighbour's registration numbers or face. Frame tight on surfaces. If no genuinely weathered engine can be found, shoot sun-faded gelcoat on any boat part instead and change line 2 to "Så här ser gelcoat ut efter några somrar utan skydd".

## Editing direction
- Real marina, handheld, walking energy. Talk like you are showing a friend, not presenting.
- Jump cuts on beat changes, captions always on.

## Primary KPI
Hold (p50/plays > 20 %) → CPA. Story ads live or die on retention.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether narrative comparison beats direct demo for this audience — unlocks or closes the story lane for the whole boat category.
