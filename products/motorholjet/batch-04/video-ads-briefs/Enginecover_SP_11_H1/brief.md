# Enginecover_SP_11_H1 — Retargeting UGC: "does it fit MY engine?"

**Type:** Creator UGC in the SP angle. Retargeting asset.
**Hypothesis:** For someone who already added to cart and did not buy, the unresolved question is almost certainly fit. A creator naming their own engine and showing the drawstring adjust should recover a meaningful share of those carts at a CPA well under the cold-traffic average.
**Kept:** The SP angle and the guarantee close.
**Changed (isolated variable):** New creator-filmed asset.

**Format:** Video, filmed 9:16, delivered 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** **Advanced** (creator with boat access)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP — **new retargeting ad set required** · **CTA:** Handla nu · **Landing page:** https://baverbutiken.se/products/marin-motorholje-420d-universellt-skydd
**Source footage:** newly filmed by the creator.

## Why this ad exists
The social-proof angle converts at 10,9 % of landing-page visitors against 4,4 % for the volume ad, and its add-to-cart rate is 16 % against 9,2 %. It is the best-converting angle we own and it is under-supplied with assets. On top of that, the volume ad alone produced 177 add-to-carts that did not convert, and nothing in the account currently speaks to those people.

## Creator script (Swedish word-for-word)
| Beat | Time | Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doubt | 0:00–0:04 | "Passar den min motor? Det undrade jag också." | "Does it fit my engine? I wondered that too." |
| Own size | 0:04–0:08 | "Jag har en 90 hk utombordare." | "I have a 90 hp outboard." |

| Confirm | 0:14–0:17 | "Sitter perfekt." | "Fits perfectly." |
| Close | 0:17–0:20 | "Passar inte din? 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti. Handla nu." | "Does yours not fit? 30-day satisfaction guarantee. Shop now." |

**Creator direction:** 35-65, genuinely owns a boat. Dressed normally, no studio feel. Dry, honest, slightly amused. Product in frame within 4 seconds. Raw UGC grade, jump cuts on beat changes, captions always on.

**MEDIA BLOCKER:** this ad needs a retargeting ad set (add-to-cart in the last 14-30 days, excluding purchasers). The campaign is currently 100 % cold prospecting, so that audience and ad set have to be created before launch. **BLOCKER:** the creator must state their real engine size.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Passar den min motor? Det undrade jag också.
> Jag har en 90 hk utombordare.
> Dragsko runt hela kanten. Sitter perfekt.
> 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti om jag har fel. 👇

Headline: `Passar din motor? Så testade jag`

**No fabricated proof:** no customer names, no star ratings, no review counts. We have no verified reviews.

**Size accuracy:** the six ranges are exactly as they appear in the store: 6 - 18 hk · 20 - 30 hk · 40 - 60 hk · 60 - 90 hk · 100 - 150 hk · 175 - 250 hk. Do not round, merge or invent ranges beyond what a brief explicitly states.

## Primary KPI
CPA against the cold-traffic average, plus recovered add-to-carts. Retargeting CPA should be well under 135 kr; if it is not, the audience is too small or too cold.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether cart abandoners are stalled on fit specifically — if this works, fit is the store-wide bottleneck for variant-heavy products.
