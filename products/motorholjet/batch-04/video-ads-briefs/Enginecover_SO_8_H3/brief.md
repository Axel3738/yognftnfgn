# Enginecover_SO_8_H3 — Guarantee-first cut (risk reversal as hook)

**Type:** Third structural variant of the SO offer. Opens on the risk reversal instead of the price.
**Hypothesis:** Leading with the 30-day guarantee removes the "will it fit my engine" objection before price enters the frame, lifting LPV→purchase above 6 % even if CTR is lower than the price-led cut.
**Kept:** The SO offer and proof stack.
**Changed (isolated variable):** Opening move only: guarantee first, price last.

**Format:** Video ~15 s, 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** **Medium** (short voiceover + re-edit)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SO · **CTA:** Handla nu
**Source footage:** `Motorhölje_PD_EXTRA` video ID **2210430979804731** (best ad in the account: CPA 96,59 kr, ROAS 4,18, CTR 5,48 %, hook 41,4 %, completion 10,3 %).

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Testa i 30 dagar, helt riskfritt.
> Passar den inte din motor, får du pengarna tillbaka.
> 420D Oxfordtyg. Skyddar mot sol, regn och salt.
> 299 kr (ord. 367 kr).
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr / 367 kr verified against the store. If the store price changes, update BOTH numbers before launch. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is ~19 %, so any "30 %" claim would be false).

**Claim discipline:** the guarantee is 30 days and satisfaction-based. "Får du pengarna tillbaka" must match the store's actual return policy — verify before launch and reword if the policy differs.

## Editing direction
- Badge animation should be quick and confident, not gimmicky.
- Captions on, centre-safe.

## Primary KPI
LPV→purchase CVR (≥ 6 %) first, CPA second.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether risk reversal or price proof is the stronger opening for the offer angle — settles the SO playbook.
