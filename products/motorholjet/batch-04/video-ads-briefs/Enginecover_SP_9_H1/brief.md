# Enginecover_SP_9_H1 — "The skeptic" creator UGC, second iteration

**Type:** Creator UGC. Iteration of `Enginecover_SP_5_H1`, the strongest early signal in batch #3 (ROAS 5,50 and hook 38,2 % on 63 kr — not yet judgeable).
**Hypothesis:** Sharpening the objection into the first line (universal covers fit like a sack) and proving it on camera converts doubters → LPV→purchase above 10 %, because pre-empting the objection from inside the customer's head beats claiming the benefit.
**Kept:** The SP angle, the guarantee close, the dry and honest creator tone.
**Changed (isolated variable):** The hook is now the objection itself, stated before any product benefit.

**Format:** Video 25–35 s, filmed 9:16, delivered 9:16 + 4:5 · **Production level:** **Advanced** (creator with boat/outboard access, natural light, phone camera fine)
**Ad set:** Motorhölje SP · **CTA:** Handla nu
**Source footage:** Newly filmed. No existing footage.

## Ad copy
Primary text:
> Jag trodde aldrig ett universalhölje kunde sitta bra.
> Den här har dragsko runt hela kanten.
> 420D Oxfordtyg som skyddar mot sol, regn och salt.
> 299 kr (ord. 367 kr), med 30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti.
> Handla nu 👇

Headline: `Universalhölje som faktiskt passar`

**Price integrity:** 299 kr / 367 kr verified against the store. If the store price changes, update BOTH numbers before launch. Never a discount percentage (299/367 is ~19 %, so any "30 %" claim would be false).

**Creator direction:** male or female 35–65 who genuinely owns a boat. Dressed normally, no studio feel. Dry, honest, slightly amused. The creator may adjust wording by about 10 %, but every claim above must stay exactly as written.

## Editing direction
- Raw UGC grade, no logos until the end card. Jump cut on every beat change.
- The 0:10–0:18 fit demonstration must be ONE unbroken take so it cannot read as edited.
- Captions always on, word-for-word Swedish. One badge at the close: `30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti`.

## Primary KPI
LPV→purchase CVR (> 10 %), secondary hold p50 (> 20 % of plays).

## What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether objection-first UGC outperforms polished claim-first edits — sets the template for every creator brief in the store.
