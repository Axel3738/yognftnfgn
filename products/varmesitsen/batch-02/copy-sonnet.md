# Värmesitsen 45 × 90 cm, batch #2 (2026-10-03): the Swedish lines, with the three-question test

Copy by sonnet 2026-10-03 against `docs/copy-regler.md`, `docs/briefs/farsdag-2026/copy/GEMENSAMT.md` and BOF-GEMENSAMT. Facts only from the product page read live 2026-10-03 (599 kr, compare-at 779 kr; 45 × 90 cm; 4 värmezoner över sits och rygg; 3 lägen med en knapp; USB, powerbank ingår inte; the cold-seat scene; bilsätet, kontorsstolen, campingstolen). Allowed numbers: 599, 779, 45, 90, 4, 3; in Father's Day ads also 8 and 19. Banned in every line: question hooks, dashes, "direkt", "viks ihop", "ryms i väskan", "funkar med powerbank", any other kronor amount or percentage, reviews, the store name. Max three words per second of screen time (the multiplication sign counts as a word). Each hook line was written in three variants; the picked one goes into the brief, the other two sit in the brief's Hook table as archive.

Test: V = can it be visualised, F = can it be falsified (true or false, not an opinion), K = can a competitor sign it (❌ is the good answer: nobody else could say it).
