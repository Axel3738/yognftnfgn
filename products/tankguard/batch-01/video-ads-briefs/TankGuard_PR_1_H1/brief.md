## 1. Why this ad exists
Every other ad sells on algae. It is September: storms and frost are the season, and the tarp-and-rubber-band solution is what most tank owners actually have. The ad shows the failure of the alternative, then ours staying on.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook:** Presenningen blåser av vid första höststormen.

- **No generated people.** Hands, the tank, the water, the cover, the tap (hook-visual rule 2026-08-04).
