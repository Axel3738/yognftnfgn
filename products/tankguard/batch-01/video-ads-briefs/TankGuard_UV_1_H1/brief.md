## 1. Why this ad exists
Algae is the problem the customer already knows and every ad repeats. The brittle tank is the cost they do not see until it cracks. Same cover, second reason — and it is the reason that costs the most.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook:** Solen gör plasten spröd, år efter år.
