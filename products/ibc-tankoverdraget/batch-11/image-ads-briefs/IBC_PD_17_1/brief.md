## Rules

- The ad never names the store: no store name, URL or logo in copy, picture, voice-over, captions or end card (rule since 2026-09-18).
- Store-neutral: no free shipping, no Klarna, no returns, no delivery time, no "14 dagar", no "öppet köp", no guarantee, no review, no star, no quote.
- Every mechanism line is the page's (read live 2026-10-02): the 210D oxford fabric shuts out the light so the algae never get started; it takes the sun instead of the plastic; slip it over the tank and close the zip, on and off with a zip; the opening on top gives access to the lid without taking anything off, topping up without removing the cover; the measurements 120 × 100 × 116 cm fit a standard tank of 1000 litres. Never a durability number, never "år efter år".
- Never "Luckan upptill når locket" (Brief review 2026-09-29, rule 1: the flap does not reach the lid). Write "Luckan upptill: du kommer åt locket" or "Öppningen upptill: han kommer åt locket utan att ta av något".
- No invented urgency: no "IDAG ENDAST", no "få kvar", no countdown. The old CS ads in the account carry such lines; never copy them.
- Only these numbers: 489, 636, 210, 120, 100, 116, 1000. No other kronor amount, no percent.
- No colour word the picture does not show.
- No dashes in any Swedish line (no "–", no "—"): a period, a comma or "och" instead.
- Product in frame before second 4 (static: from the first render).
- Hook is a declarative, one clause pair, readable in 3 s; never a question.
- Maximum three words per second (numbers count).
- Price is a swappable slot: read it live from the landing page before upload.
- The image model never renders the text: the lines are burned on afterwards, word for word from the Design brief.
- Each fact once: the price lives in the price band only, the headline, sub-line and button carry nothing the band says.
- Export: 4:5 (1080x1350) + 1:1 (1080x1080), PNG or JPG.
