## Rules

- The ad never names the store: no store name, URL or logo in copy, picture, voice-over or captions. This hub mirrors into another shop.
- Store-neutral: no free shipping, no Klarna, no returns, no delivery time, no "30 dagar", no "öppet köp" — shop policy differs between the two shops.
- Every mechanism line is the page's (read live 2026-09-28): straps on all four sides, 2,5 m, set in a buckle, hooked under the body's bottom edge; two reinforced 10,5 m ratchet straps across the roof, included; the edge hangs 30 to 40 cm down over the sides so the roof/wall seam is covered; water runs off the 210D weave instead of standing; one person; folds to an arm-sized pack after the season; on from autumn to spring, heavy wet snow shovelled off. - The edge hangs 30 to 40 cm down over the sides: never "stannar vid takkanten", never a frame that hides the hanging edge (Brief review 2026-09-25, rule 1).
- A "never this product" row is a generic tarp or cover; this cover is never shown flapping, leaking, failing or in a heap.
- Never promise it cannot blow off, never a lifetime or "år efter år": the ad shows the fastening, the season and the fold, and names them, nothing more.
- The objection is conceded first and the buyer is never wrong; no customer quotes, no reviews, no "5,0 av 5" (the product has only seeded reviews). The comment in invandningar.md is research, never a testimonial.
- No invented urgency: no "IDAG", no "lagret är begränsat", no "innan det är för sent", no countdown.
- Only these numbers: 1 129, 1 469, 340, 23 %, 6,5 × 3 m (3 × 6,5 m), 2,5 m, 10,5 m, 30–40 cm, 3 m. No other kronor amount, no other percent.
- No dashes in any Swedish line (no "–", no "—"): a period or "och" instead; "30 till 40 cm" in text, "trettio till fyrtio centimeter" in VO.
