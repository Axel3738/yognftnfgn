## Rules

- The ad never names the store: no store name, URL or logo in copy, picture, voice-over or captions. This hub mirrors into another shop.
- Store-neutral: no free shipping, no Klarna, no returns, no delivery time, no "14 dagar", no "30 dagar", no "öppet köp", no guarantee — shop policy differs between the two shops.
- Every mechanism line is the page's (read live 2026-10-01): straps on all four sides, 2,5 m, set in a buckle, plastic hook under the body's bottom edge; two reinforced 10,5 m ratchet straps across the roof, included; the edge hangs 30 to 40 cm down over the sides so the roof/wall seam is covered; water runs off the silver-coated 210D weave instead of standing; one person; folds to an arm-sized pack after the season; on from autumn to spring, heavy wet snow shovelled off; nine sizes, 3 m wide, 5,5 to 13,5 m long. Never "dragsko", never "förvaringspåse" or "påse", never "andas" or "ventilerad", never "vattentät" as a bare claim (Brief review 2026-09-29, rule 2: the ad says what happens — the water runs off), never "duken", "presenning" or "skynke" for our product.
- Never a dry-roof, no-condensation or no-mould promise: the owner has confirmed the customers are right about moisture and a spacer product is on its way (kommentarer/leads.md 2026-09-28). The ad shows the run-off and the free sides, nothing more.
- The edge hangs 30 to 40 cm down over the sides: never "stannar vid takkanten", never a frame that hides the hanging edge (Brief review 2026-09-25, rule 1).
- A "never this product" row is a generic tarp or cover; this cover is never shown flapping, leaking, failing or in a heap. Never promise it cannot blow off, never a lifetime or "år efter år".
- No customer quotes, no reviews, no "5,0 av 5", no star, no customer name (the product has only seeded reviews). No competitor named — a cheaper cover is "ett tunt överdrag" or "en tunn presenning", never a brand or a marketplace.
- No invented urgency: no "IDAG", no "lagret är begränsat", no "innan det är för sent", no countdown. The season (autumn rain, first frost, wet snow) may be named as what it is.
- Only these numbers: 1 129, 1 469, 340, 23 %, nio, 3 m, 5,5, 6,5, 13,5, 2,5 m, 10,5 m, 30 till 40 cm, 210D. No other kronor amount, no other percent, no repair cost.
- No dashes in any Swedish line (no "–", no "—"): a period, a comma or "och" instead; "30 till 40 cm" in text, "trettio till fyrtio centimeter" in VO.
- Product in frame before second 4 (static: from the first render).
- Hook is a declarative, one clause, readable in 3 s; never a question.
- Price is a swappable slot; read it live from the landing page before upload.
- The image model never renders the text: the lines are burned on afterwards, word for word from the Design brief.
