## Hard rules for this ad (AdventLane)
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" with "jämförpris 649 kr" (or "649 kr → 499 kr"). Never a percentage, never any other number. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **Packs:** 2-pack and 3-pack exist but their prices are being A/B tested on the page right now. **Never show or say a pack price or a pack discount.** "Finns som 2-pack" is the most you may say.
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager", "slut innan jul", "skynda", "priset går upp". 499 kr is the standing price. No countdown, no exclamation marks.
- **Terms:** "Fri frakt inom Sverige och Norge" · "14 dagars ångerrätt" · "5–10 arbetsdagar" (delivery). There is NO 30-day guarantee, no "öppet köp", no "nöjd eller pengarna tillbaka".
- **Facts only:** 24 numbered doors in mixed order, one car behind every door, all different (start numbers such as 67, 33, 07; checkered; flames), wheels roll, CE + CPC, small parts — not under 3 years. Size in cm, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN — never claim them. Never "tusentals", never "bestseller".
- **Reviews:** ten real five-star reviews, quote verbatim with first name or not at all. Never "Verifierad kund", never an age, never an invented quote.
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no emojis in the creative.
- **No generated people.** Hands, the box, the doors, the cars, the table, the wrapper. A generated face breaks the ad (hook-visual rule 2026-08-04).
- **Look:** real photo/video of the calendar at home — kitchen table, wooden floor, morning light, candles. Never red-green kitsch, never stock families smiling at the camera. Palette if graphics are needed: midnight blue #152444, warm paper #F7F2EA, lingonberry #A8283A (only accent), spruce green #2F6B4F.
- **Spelling traps:** adventskalender · jämförpris · ångerrätt · luckor (plural of lucka) · AdventLane (capital A, capital L). Å, Ä, Ö must render in captions.
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
- **Export:** video 9:16 + 4:5 (file `_1` = 4:5, `_2` = 9:16); image 4:5 (1080×1350) + 1:1. Product in frame before second 4. Swedish captions word for word from the script.
- **Delivery:** upload to the Notion row (video: Drive link at the end of the page body "Link for approval: …"; image: attachment in "Filer och media"), set Status to `To be Reviewed`.
