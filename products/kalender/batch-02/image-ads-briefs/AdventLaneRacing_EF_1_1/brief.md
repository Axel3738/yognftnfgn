## 1. Why this ad exists
The proven claim is a promise: the cars are still there on the 24th. Move one day and the promise becomes a picture anyone with children knows: the chocolate calendar flat in the paper recycling, and 24 small cars on the floor being driven around. Left half is the enemy, right half is the product in use. The headline reads across the split.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook idea:** Juldagen: papper eller 24 bilar.
"What is left on the 25th" is a stronger reason than "still there on the 24th" on the image format. Measured against PD_2_1 (the top spender, the benchmark) on profit contribution and CTR.

## 8. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" with "jämförpris 649 kr". Never a percentage, never any other number. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **Packs:** 2-pack and 3-pack prices are being A/B tested on the page. **Never show or say a pack price or a pack discount.**
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager", "slut innan jul", "skynda". No exclamation marks.
- **No durability claim:** "körs på golvet" describes the picture. Never "tål", "håller i åratal", never a material. Size, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN.
- **Review:** Sofia's line verbatim, first name only. Never "Verifierad kund", never an age, never a star count written out.
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no emojis on the image.
- **No generated people.** A real small hand pushing a car is fine; a generated child is not. No faces.
- **No brand on the chocolate calendar.**
- **Spelling traps:** juldagen (the 25th) · julafton (the 24th) · återvinningen · jämförpris · AdventLane (capital A, capital L).
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
