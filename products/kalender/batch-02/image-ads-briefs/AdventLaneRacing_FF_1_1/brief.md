## 1. Why this ad exists
The source's gift ad speaks about the child ("han älskar bilar"). Grandparents buy advent calendars every year and want the thing to still be there when they come to visit at Christmas. Same product, same proven "still there" claim, addressed to a different giver. The picture is the unopened gift box on a table beside a coffee cup and reading glasses — the giver's morning, not the child's.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook idea:** Något som väntar till julafton.
Addressing the grandparent converts better than GT's CPA 297 kr (inherited, 2 purchases). Judged on profit contribution against break-even CPA 323 kr; CPA below target 198 kr would make it a scaling candidate.

## 8. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" with "jämförpris 649 kr". Never a percentage. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **Packs:** 2-pack and 3-pack prices are being A/B tested on the page. **Never show or say a pack price or a pack discount.**
- **No urgency, ever:** "5–10 arbetsdagar" is a delivery fact, never "beställ nu", never "hinner inte", no deadline date. No "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager". No exclamation marks.
- **No pronouns for the child:** "barnbarnet" only. Never han/honom/hon/henne.
- **Facts only:** 24 numbered doors, one car behind every door, all different, wheels roll. Size, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN — never claim them. No review is used here (none of the ten is written by a grandparent) — do not add one.
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no emojis on the image.
- **No generated people.** The box, the cup, the glasses, the table. No faces, no hands needed.
- **Spelling traps:** julafton · barnbarnet · jämförpris · arbetsdagar · AdventLane (capital A, capital L).
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
