## 1. Why this ad exists
The proven hook counts seconds ("Chokladen är uppäten på 10 sekunder"). Copy rule: change the timeframe until the number becomes striking, but keep it true. The same conflict counted as a month — 24 mornings, no chocolate before breakfast — is the reframe. Same angle, same product, same format that already scaled (image). Only the number moves.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook idea:** 24 frukostar. Bilar i stället för choklad.
"24 breakfasts" as the number in the headline beats "10 seconds" on the image format. Measured against PD_2_1 on profit contribution and CTR.

## 8. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" with "jämförpris 649 kr". Never a percentage, never any other number. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **Packs:** 2-pack and 3-pack prices are being A/B tested on the page. **Never show or say a pack price or a pack discount.**
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager", "slut innan jul", "skynda". No exclamation marks.
- **"Inget godis" / "i stället för choklad" is a fact about the box** (nothing edible inside) — never "nyttigare", never "sockerfri", no health claim.
- **Facts only:** 24 numbered doors, one car behind every door, all different, wheels roll. Size, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN — never claim them. Never "tusentals", never "bestseller".
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no emojis on the image.
- **No generated people.** The box, the cars, the breakfast, the wrapper. A generated face or hand that looks AI breaks the ad.
- **No brand on the chocolate wrapper.**
- **Spelling traps:** frukostar · jämförpris · julafton · AdventLane (capital A, capital L).
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
