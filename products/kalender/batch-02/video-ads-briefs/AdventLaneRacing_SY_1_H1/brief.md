## 1. Why this ad exists
One car per door and two children at the table is an argument every parent of two knows. No ad in the account speaks to it, yet a third of the source's revenue above the single price says people already buy two. This film names the argument and shows the fix — two boxes, one door each, two cars — without ever saying a pack price (the pack prices are being A/B tested live).

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook idea:** Bakom luckan finns bara en bil. Två barn vill ha den.
A conflict-motivated pack lifts AOV above 632 kr without raising CPA above break-even 323 kr. Judged on profit contribution and AOV (read from Meta purchase value, cross-checked with spend × ROAS).

## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **Pack price: NEVER.** The 2-pack and 3-pack prices are being A/B tested on the page right now (two different levels). Say "Finns som 2-pack" and the single price "499 kr" — nothing else about the pack. No percentage, no "spara", no pack total.
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" (with "jämförpris 649 kr" if a compare price is shown). Never a percentage. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager", "slut innan jul", "skynda". No exclamation marks.
- **Terms:** "Fri frakt inom Sverige och Norge" · "14 dagars ångerrätt" · delivery "5–10 arbetsdagar". NO 30-day guarantee, no "öppet köp".
- **Facts only:** 24 numbered doors, one car behind every door, all different, wheels roll, CE + CPC, small parts — not under 3. Size, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN — never claim them.
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no emojis in the creative.
- **No generated people. No faces.** Two small hands, the boxes, the cars, the table. No names, no ages, no pronouns for the children.
- **Spelling traps:** luckan · jämförpris · AdventLane (capital A, capital L).
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
- **Export:** 9:16 (`_2`) + 4:5 (`_1`). Delivery: Drive link at the end of this Notion page ("Link for approval: …"), then set Status to `To be Reviewed`.

## 10. Primary KPI
**Profit contribution against break-even CPA 323 kr** together with **AOV against 632 kr** (read as purchase value / purchases, cross-checked with spend × ROAS — the raw value field is unreliable, ANALYSMETOD step 1).

## 11. What we learn regardless of outcome
Whether a reason beats a discount for selling the pack. If AOV rises at the same CPA, every pack mention in future copy gets a reason, never a price. If AOV does not move, the pack sells on the page by itself and ads stay single-product.
