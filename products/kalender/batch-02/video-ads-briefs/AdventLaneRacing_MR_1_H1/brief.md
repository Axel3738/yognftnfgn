## 1. Why this ad exists
Every ad in the source account shows the product first. None shows the thing the reviews actually describe: the morning. This film is the routine itself — one door at seven o'clock, one car placed next to yesterday's, and a row that grows. If a scene holds attention longer than a product demo, the brand's core scene becomes a format.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook idea:** Varje morgon i december kommer en ny bil ut.
Scene-first holds better than product-first: hold rate above PD_2_H1's 28,1 % (inherited). Judged only with profit contribution against break-even CPA 323 kr.

## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" with "jämförpris 649 kr" (or 649 kr struck through). Never a percentage. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **Packs:** 2-pack and 3-pack prices are being A/B tested on the page. **Never show or say a pack price or a pack discount.**
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager", "slut innan jul", "skynda". No exclamation marks.
- **Terms:** "Fri frakt inom Sverige och Norge" · "14 dagars ångerrätt" · delivery "5–10 arbetsdagar". NO 30-day guarantee, no "öppet köp".
- **Facts only:** 24 numbered doors, one car behind every door, all different, wheels roll, CE + CPC, small parts — not under 3. Size, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN — never claim them.
- **Review:** Johan's line verbatim, first name only. "Sonen" is the customer's own word and stays. Never "Verifierad kund", never an age.
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no emojis in the creative.
- **No generated people. No faces.** Hands, the box, the doors, the cars, the table, the breakfast. A generated child breaks the ad.
- **The count must be right** in the three cuts: 1, 8, 24 cars.
- **Spelling traps:** gårdagens · jämförpris · december (lower case) · AdventLane (capital A, capital L).
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
- **Export:** 9:16 (`_2`) + 4:5 (`_1`). Delivery: Drive link at the end of this Notion page ("Link for approval: …"), then set Status to `To be Reviewed`.

Whether the brand's core scene works as an ad format on this product. If it holds and converts, the routine becomes a series (one film per week of December).
