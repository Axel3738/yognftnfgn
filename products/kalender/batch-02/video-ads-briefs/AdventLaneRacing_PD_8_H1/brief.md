## 1. Why this ad exists
The proven PD copy ends with "Ge honom något som varar längre än ett ögonblick" and locks the buyer to a boy. The brand must not lock itself to boys or to cars (`factory/butiker/kalender.yaml` branding) — the buyer is a parent or grandparent, 28–65, of a child 3–10. This brief keeps the exact footage and the exact conflict (chocolate gone before breakfast, the cars still there on the 24th) and removes every gendered word from the voice, the captions and the copy card. If it holds PD_2_H1's CVR, the neutral form is at least as good and reaches more buyers.

## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **No gendered word anywhere:** not han, honom, hon, henne, pojke, flicka, son, dotter, killen, tjejen. The only person word allowed is "barnet" (copy card line 3). Check every caption.
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" with "jämförpris 649 kr" (or 649 kr struck through). Never a percentage. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **Packs:** 2-pack and 3-pack prices are being A/B tested on the page. **Never show or say a pack price or a pack discount.**
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager", "slut innan jul", "skynda". No exclamation marks.
- **Terms:** "Fri frakt inom Sverige och Norge" · "14 dagars ångerrätt" · delivery "5–10 arbetsdagar". NO 30-day guarantee, no "öppet köp".
- **Facts only:** 24 numbered doors, one car behind every door, all different, wheels roll, CE + CPC, small parts — not under 3. Size, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN — never claim them.
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no emojis in the creative.
- **No generated people.** Hands, the box, the doors, the cars, the table.
- **Spelling traps:** adventskalender · jämförpris · julafton · morgnar · AdventLane (capital A, capital L).
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
