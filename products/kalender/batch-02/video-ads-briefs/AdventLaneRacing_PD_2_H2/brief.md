## 1. Why this ad exists
95 % of drop-off happens in the first 3 seconds, so the opening shot is the cheapest variable we can test (`docs/creative-strategy.md` §3). PD_2_H1 opens on the product. This version opens on the enemy: a flattened chocolate advent calendar, all 24 doors open, lying in the paper recycling. A physical, slightly ugly object in second 0 — the thing the hook-visual rule asks for. Everything from second 3 onward is PD_2_H1, untouched, so any difference in hook rate and CVR belongs to the opening.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook idea:** the empty chocolate calendar in the recycling, then cut to the cars.
An object-first hook lifts hook rate above PD_2_H1's 28,7 % without lowering its CVR (4,92 %). Measured head-to-head against PD_2_H1.

## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "499 kr" with "jämförpris 649 kr". Never a percentage, never any other number. Verified live on adventlane.se 2026-09-12.
- **Packs:** 2-pack and 3-pack prices are being A/B tested on the page. **Never show or say a pack price or a pack discount.**
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "begränsat lager", "slut innan jul", "skynda". No countdown, no exclamation marks in new text.
- **Terms:** "Fri frakt inom Sverige och Norge" · "14 dagars ångerrätt" · delivery "5–10 arbetsdagar". There is NO 30-day guarantee, no "öppet köp".
- **Facts only:** 24 numbered doors, one car behind every door, all different, wheels roll, CE + CPC, small parts — not under 3. Size, material and pull-back are UNKNOWN — never claim them.
- **Never** "Bäverbutiken", never Klarna, never "bonus" or "gratis", no new emojis in the creative.
- **No generated people.** The recycling bin, the flat box, the cars. A generated face breaks the ad.
- **No brand on the chocolate calendar** in the new shot.
- **Spelling traps:** adventskalender · återvinningen · jämförpris · AdventLane (capital A, capital L).
- **Landing page:** https://adventlane.se/products/adventskalender-racingbilar
- **Export:** 9:16 (`_2`) + 4:5 (`_1`). Delivery: Drive link at the end of this Notion page ("Link for approval: …"), then set Status to `To be Reviewed`.
