## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "799 kr i stället för 1 000 kr" — spoken, on screen and in the copy. Never a percentage, never any other number. Verified in the store 2026-09-08 (heimguard.se/products/overvakningskameran).
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "snart slut", "priset går upp", "missa inte", "så länge lagret räcker". 799 kr is the standing price. HeimGuard's tone is calm and factual — no exclamation marks.
- **Shipping:** "Fri frakt i hela Sverige" — never "över 300 kr".
- **Social proof:** exactly 10 reviews, all five stars. Quote them verbatim with the first name or not at all. Never "tusentals hushåll", never a sleep quote attributed to a customer.
- **No Klarna, no Bäverbutiken, no setup time in minutes, no specs not on the product page** (resolution, battery, night vision, IP class, storage are deliberately unknown).
- **Never point at a group of people as the threat.** The threat is a sound on the property, never who.
- **No generated people.** Hands, screens, the camera, the yard. A generated person breaks the ad (hook-visual rule 2026-08-04).
- **Spelling traps:** övervakningskamera · dubbellins · persondetektering · nöjd-kund-garanti · HeimGuard (capital H, capital G).
- **Landing page:** https://heimguard.se/products/overvakningskameran
