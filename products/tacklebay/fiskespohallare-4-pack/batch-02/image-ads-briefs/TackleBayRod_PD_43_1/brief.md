## 1. Why this ad exists
PD_6_1 proved that the demo sells as a static (dna.md pattern 2) — but it is one picture, so the proof is for that picture, not for statics in general. TackleBay's angle is transport, not storage. This ad keeps every word of the winner and changes only where the rod is: from the jetty to the back seat of a car. Read together with **PD_44_1** (same picture as the winner, new headline) — between them they tell us whether the scene or the headline carries the ad.

## 2. Hypothesis
The winner's text works because it is concrete ("Ett klick. Spöet stängt."); the scene is interchangeable as long as the clamp is the biggest thing in frame. If the transport scene holds CPA under 294 kr, the transport angle can carry the next statics. **Hook (kept):** Ett klick. Spöet stängt.

## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "289 kr" for the 4-pack — on screen and in the copy. No compare-at price, never a percentage, never 149 kr / 148,75 kr. Verified in the store 2026-09-12 (https://tacklebay.se/products/fiskespohallare-4-pack: 289,00, no compare-at).
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "snart slut", "så länge lagret räcker". 289 kr is the standing price.
- **No exclamation marks.** TackleBay's tone is dry and plain — anglers smell sales talk at once.
- **It is a TRANSPORT clamp, not a wall mount.** Never "vägg", "väggmontering", "garage", "montera på väggen", "skruv". The source store's text says wall mount — it is wrong and must not be reused. The promise is: the folded rod and the line stay still on the way to the water.
- **No colours.** Never orange / blå / grön / rosa — the colour question is unresolved.
- **Social proof:** exactly seven reviews, all five stars, quoted verbatim with the first name or not at all. Never "tusentals", never an invented quote.
- **No Bäverbutiken anywhere.** Landing page is TackleBay only.
- **No generated people.** Hands, rods, the clamp, the back seat, the boat deck. A generated person breaks the ad (hook-visual rule 2026-08-04).
- **Photos:** real gear, boat, jetty, car. Nordic grey weather, no sunset clichés, no smiling stock models (store config).
- **Spelling traps:** spöhållare · ihopfällt spö · hög-elastisk svamp · spinnspö · havsfiskespö · TackleBay (capital T, capital B).
- **Landing page:** https://tacklebay.se/products/fiskespohallare-4-pack
