## 1. Why this ad exists
Every demo so far is a studio shot. A phone in the hand of someone packing the car is a different visual language — and cheap to make.

## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "289 kr" for the 4-pack — on screen and in the copy. No compare-at price, never a percentage, never 149 kr / 148,75 kr. Verified in the store 2026-09-13 (https://tacklebay.se/products/fiskespohallare-4-pack: 289,00, no compare-at).
- **No urgency, ever:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "snart slut", "så länge lagret räcker". 289 kr is the standing price.
- **No exclamation marks.** TackleBay's tone is dry and plain — anglers smell sales talk at once.
- **It is a TRANSPORT clamp, not a wall mount.** Never "vägg", "väggmontering", "garage", "montera på väggen", "skruv", never "förvaring" as the promise. The rod travels folded; the clamp holds the two sections and the line still on the way to the water.
- **Guarantee:** only "14 dagars ångerrätt". Never "30 dagars nöjd-kund-garanti" (that is the source store's line), never Klarna.
- **No colours.** Never orange / blå / grön / rosa — unresolved.
- **Social proof:** exactly seven reviews, all five stars, quoted verbatim with the first name or not at all. Never "tusentals", never an invented quote.
- **No Bäverbutiken anywhere.** Landing page is TackleBay only.
- **No generated people.** Hands, rods, the clamp, the back seat, the boat deck. A generated person breaks the ad (hook-visual rule 2026-08-04).
- **Photos:** real gear, boat, jetty, car. Nordic grey weather, no sunset clichés, no smiling stock models (store config).
- **Spelling traps:** spöhållare · ihopfällt spö · hög-elastisk svamp · spinnspö · havsfiskespö · rullfäste · TackleBay (capital T, capital B).
- **Landing page:** https://tacklebay.se/products/fiskespohallare-4-pack
