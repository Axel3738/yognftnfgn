## 1. Why this ad exists
Slide-down is the failure mode of every cheap gaiter the buyer has owned. The answer is two anchors nobody notices in a normal demo. A walking shot that keeps the hook and strap in frame is the proof.

## 2. Hypothesis
**Hook:** Glider de ner när man går?

## 9. Hard rules for this ad
- **Price:** exactly "389 kr per par (ord. 649 kr)" — never any other number, never a percentage. 2-pack 661,30 kr and 3-pack 933,60 kr may be mentioned only as written here. Verified on drytrek.se/products/damasker 2026-09-13.
- **Never claim waterproofing:** no "vattentät", "vindtät", "stoppar regn", "alla väder". Show what happens (dry sock, wet stays outside) — never the material property. The source ad's voiceover made these claims; that is exactly what this batch removes.
- **No time claims:** no "på 10 sekunder", "hela vandringen".
- **No social proof:** the store has zero reviews and zero customers to quote. Never "tusentals", "kunderna älskar", stars, ratings. A creator's own first-person experience is allowed; a customer quote is not.
- **No urgency, no exclamation marks:** no "bara idag", "sista chansen", "snart slut". 389 kr is the standing price.
- **Return policy:** "14 dagars ångerrätt" — never "30 dagars öppet köp". **Shipping:** "Fri frakt till Sverige och Norge".
- **Never mention Bäverbutiken or Klarna.** The brand is **DryTrek** (capital D, capital T).
- **No generated people, no faces required.** Hands, legs, boots, the gaiter, the trail. A generated person breaks the ad (hook-visual rule 2026-08-04).
- **Spelling traps:** damasker · kardborre · snörning · kängan · neongrön · DryTrek.
- **Captions:** burned in, Swedish, word for word, inside the middle 80 % of the frame. Å, Ä, Ö must render.
- **Landing page:** https://drytrek.se/products/damasker
- **Naming:** deliver the file named exactly as this Notion row. Never `Damasker_*` without the `DryTrek_` prefix — those are Bäverbutiken's ads.
