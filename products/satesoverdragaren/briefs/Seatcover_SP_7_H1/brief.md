**Primary KPI:** CPA (target < 300 kr, break-even 474 kr) · diagnostic: hold rate at 20s and 45s

## Why this ad exists

The swipe's signature is **the offer comes first and the problem second**. A reason-why discount buys the attention, then the product argument earns the click. Every claim in it is a mechanism or a sensation, never an adjective: *"the holes are shaped so flame and smoke reach every piece"*, not *"great basket"*.

Our account has never run offer-first, and it has never run the clearance as the reason to act. Every video we have opens on the problem. This tests the inversion on a body of proven material.

**Isolated variable: argument order** (offer-first vs problem-first). Footage, captions style and CTA stay as in the winner.

### The one thing that is deliberately NOT copied: the percentage

The original leads on "40 %". Ours states **kronor only, never a percentage.** The owner's brief says ordinary prices sit roughly 25 % higher on average but that the exact discount varies per product, and that we must not state a rate more precisely than each product's own data supports. For this product Shopify carries 649 kr against a compare-at of 811 kr, so those two numbers are documented and safe; any percentage derived from an average across the range is not. **Do not add one in the edit, the thumbnail or the ad copy.**

## VO and edit direction

- **Voice: one ordinary person talking to another.** Not an announcer, not a radio ad. Same register as someone explaining a thing they made. Record in a room, not a booth — a little room tone is correct for this account.
- **No face on camera.** The voice carries the insider role; the picture stays on product and hands. This deliberately separates it from `SP_1`–`SP_6`, which all used a creator on camera and produced 0 purchases across 7 ads. The variable being tested here is argument order, not whether creators work.
- Burned-in Swedish captions word-for-word from the table, white text with black outline, bottom third. **Must work fully muted** — the captions carry the whole script.
- Product on screen before second 4.
- Cut on the beat boundaries. Every beat is a new shot; no beat may run over two shots.
- Never re-translate the Swedish. Check å/ä/ö at export.
- Export 9:16 (1080×1920) and 4:5 (1080×1350), for both the full version and the 30s cutdown.

## What we learn regardless of outcome

- **Offer-first beats problem-first** → the account's whole opening convention is wrong and every future video leads with the clearance and the price. That would be a bigger finding than any single ad.
