# Seatcover_SP_5_H1 — Story-led before/after ("8 år gammal klippare, säte som nytt")

**Type:** New concept (FAS 8) — story-led persuasion. Concept code SP is a judgment call: the story carries social-proof mechanics (relatable owner narrative), closest fit among PD/SP/SO.
**Format:** 30–35s narrative video · 9:16 + 4:5 · burned-in Swedish captions
**Production level:** Advanced (new shoot; needs a genuinely worn seat + same-day "after")
**Primary KPI:** CPA (< 314 kr) · diagnosis: p50/3s ≥ 25% (story must hold past the reveal)

## Hypothesis
The account's before/after attempt (`Seatcover_PD_2_1_H1`, ROAS 1.85) showed the transformation but without narrative stakes. Wrapping the same transformation in a 3-beat story (neglect → 60-second fix → morning-after payoff) increases hold and makes the price feel like a win, without needing discount pressure. Variable vs PD_2: **narrative structure**, not the transformation itself.

## Exact hook (0–3s)
Split-frame: left = cracked, faded seat; right = same mower with cover on. Caption line 1. The split resolves to the "before" side at 3s.

## Creator direction
- No on-camera talent required; hands + optional calm male/female VO (40+, Swedish) reading caption lines.
- The worn seat must be real. If no 8-year seat is available, adjust line 2 to the true age — never overstate ("åtta somrar" only if true; otherwise "flera somrar" / "several summers").

## Editing direction
- Captions word-for-word, winner style.
- Grade "before" slightly desaturated, "after" neutral — no fake sunset glow.
- Music: low, warm, resolves at beat 5.
- "649 kr" plain text.
