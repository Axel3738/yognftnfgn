# Seatcover_PD_5_H1 — 15s cutdown of the winner (Reels-first)

**Type:** Format transfer of proven winner (`Seatcover_PD_1_3_H1`)
**Format:** 15s product demo · 9:16 primary (Reels/Stories), 4:5 secondary · burned-in Swedish captions
**Production level:** Simple (pure re-edit)
**Primary KPI:** ThruPlay rate + CPA (target < 258 kr); watch CPM (Reels should come in below feed's 130 kr)

## Hypothesis
Retention data on the 38s winner: ~half of hooked viewers are gone by 9.5s; only 13% of starters reach 19s. The persuasion payload is spread over 38 seconds that most people never see. A 15s version that delivers demo + top-2 USPs + price inside the attention window converts the same viewers at lower cost. One isolated variable: **length/pacing** (same footage, same captions style, same message order).

## What is kept / what changes
- KEPT: winner's footage, caption style, message order (problem → on in seconds → dry + padded → price/CTA), headline, copy.
- CHANGED: duration 38s → 15s; tighter cuts; 9:16-first framing.

## Editing direction
- Cut on action every 2–3s; no shot longer than 3.5s.
- Captions word-for-word from table; keep inside 9:16 safe area (Reels UI covers bottom ~250px — place captions center-lower, not absolute bottom).
- Keep music, raise tempo edit-to-beat. Muted-view must carry 100% of message.
- No price strikethrough graphics; "649 kr" as plain text.

## CTA
Button: **Handla nu** (SHOP_NOW).

## Ad copy
Same primary text + headline as winner ("Torrt och skönt säte – hela säsongen" family). Do not shorten the body copy — the test variable is the video only.

## What we learn regardless of outcome
If CPA ≤ winner with higher ThruPlay → length was waste; future masters default to 15–20s and 38s becomes the exception. If CPA worsens despite better ThruPlay → the long demo does real persuasion work; keep 38s as BOF/feed format and use 15s only for cheap reach. Either way we learn where the persuasion actually happens.
