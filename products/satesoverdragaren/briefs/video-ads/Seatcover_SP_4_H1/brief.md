# Seatcover_SP_4_H1 — Proof-led UGC (real Swedish owner)

**Type:** New concept (FAS 8) — proof-led persuasion
**Format:** 25–30s UGC video · shot 9:16 vertical · exports 9:16 + 4:5 · burned-in Swedish captions
**Production level:** Advanced (new shoot with a real customer or creator who owns a riding mower)
**Primary KPI:** CPA (< 314 kr) · diagnosis: LPV→purchase CVR (the old SP ads clicked at 6.7% CTR but converted at 0% — this brief exists to fix that)

## ⚠️ BLOCKER before production
The quote-based script below may only be used if it matches a REAL review (Judge.me export pending) or the filmed person's genuine experience. Do not stage a "customer" reading invented lines; if no real customer is available, brief the creator to speak from actual product use after receiving a sample. Never overlay "Verifierad kund". (A previous static with a fabricated verified-customer quote is banned from reuse.)

## Hypothesis
The account has zero proven proof-led ads: prior UGC ran 79–146s and lost viewers before the argument (p50 = 11% of hooked viewers). A 25–30s UGC with the winner's caption discipline and a qualifying hook (talks to owners, not curious scrollers) converts the trust mechanism instead of collecting curiosity clicks. Variable vs winner: **messenger** (real person vs supplier footage), everything else follows winning DNA.

## Exact hook (0–3s)
Creator stands next to their mower, hand on the worn seat, looks at camera: line 1 spoken + captioned. Product (cover in hand) visible in frame from second 0.

## Creator direction
- Profile: Swedish speaker 40–65, house with lawn, own riding mower (any brand; brand logos may be visible, do not mention brand names verbally).
- Delivery: plain, unpolished, "grannen berättar"-tone. No ad-voice, no smiling-into-camera intensity. One take feel.
- Wardrobe/setting: everyday yard clothes, daylight, garden/garage background. No studio light.

## Editing direction
- Captions word-for-word, winner style (white/black outline).
- Keep natural audio bed; no music louder than -18 LUFS under voice.
- Jump-cuts OK; total runtime hard cap 30s.
- End frame: covered seat + caption line 6 held 1.5s.

Button: **Handla nu** (SHOP_NOW).
