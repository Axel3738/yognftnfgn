## Rules (every one of these is a stop — an ad that breaks one is sent back)

1. **Price:** only 399 kr / 798 kr may appear, and only if the brief says so. Never "299 kr", never "3-par 369 kr", never a percentage, never "rea", never "spara X kr". The offer is always written exactly **KÖP 1 – FÅ 1 GRATIS** (or **KÖP 2 – FÅ 2 GRATIS** where the brief says so), en dash, capitals in the badge.
2. **No size** anywhere (no "onesize", no "36–44"). No material or quality claims ("mjuka", "sköna", "kvalitet"). No invented reviews, quotes or reactions. Never say anyone was "lurad" — the socks *look like* sushi.
3. **No people and no faces** in the picture (031 allows adult feet only). The product fills at least half the frame. The box must match the real product photos (`reference-assets/product-photos/`) — same box, same five designs, chopsticks wooden.
4. **Text is set as real typography, never generated by an image model.** Swedish letters å ä ö must be exact. Use the Swedish lines below **word for word**. Headline max 8 words, readable at phone feed size (min 60 px x-height on the 1080 canvas).
5. **Christmas is allowed only in 033 and 036.** Everything else is season-neutral: no tree, no stocking, no snow, no gift wrap.
6. **AI images:** the scene may be AI-generated (as the 2026-08-27 BOGO set was: kie.ai scene + Poppins Bold typography), but the product must be true to the photos. Report any AI use — the ad is created with Meta's AI-disclosure ON.
7. **Export:** 4:5 at 1080×1350 (feed) **and** 1:1 at 1080×1080. PNG or high-quality JPG. File name = the Meta ad name.
8. **Spelling traps:** "sushilåda", "ätpinnar", "Köp 1 – Få 1" (en dash "–", not hyphen), "förklädda", "byrålåda", "julstrumpan".
