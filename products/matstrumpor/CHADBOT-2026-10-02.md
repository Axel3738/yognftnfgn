```
Context: Swedish DTC store, one novelty gift product (~45 USD AOV), spending ~10k USD/month on Meta, one CBO campaign. One UGC video from one creator is a breakthrough: ~50 % of campaign spend, ~2x break-even ROAS, campaign grew +30 % the week it launched. Everything else we upload gets starved: Meta gives the new ads 1–10 USD a week in the other ad sets.

Four questions:

1. The breakthrough creator made only ONE video. What do you do first: order more videos from the same person, or give her exact script to 2–3 new creators (copy / iterate / imitate) to see if it's the script or the face? We can afford both, but can only read one result at a time.

2. We have a spend winner with high hold (~75 % of viewers past 3 s) but low conversion (~1 % of landing page views) on this low-price product. Do you fix the funnel (landing page, offer, trust) or the ad's urgency first? What tells you which?

3. How do you count hit rate per idea source (research vs iteration vs imitation), and what hit rate is normal at 5–15k USD/month? Our current hit rate (breakthrough + spend winner over all labelled ads) is about 2 %.

4. Routing: in a CBO, the ad set holding the breakthrough takes ~85 % of spend and starves every new ad set. Our only new ads that got real delivery were uploaded INTO the breakthrough's ad set (~300 USD each in a week, with purchases). Your course says one ad set per concept and never test in the champion's ad set. Given this data, would you still keep testing in separate ad sets, or put new iterations into the champion's ad set? What's the risk you're protecting against?
```
