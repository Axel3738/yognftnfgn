# UGC script pack: Sofie and Katarina, three videos each

**For:** the UGC coordinator (Lovely) and the two creators. **Parent ad:** `09-17 Nathalie captions musik`, our best ad by far: in its first week it brought in two out of every three sales the whole campaign made.
**Recipe (Evolve):** each creator films three videos from the winner's components. **A** = copy, **B** = iteration, **C** = imitation.
**Deliverable per creator:** A (one full take) · B (three separate 3-second hook takes + one body take) · C (one video in ONE chosen format; the two creators must not pick the same format).
**Film:** vertical 9:16, phone, natural light, her own home, no filters. She says every line out loud, on camera. Editors burn the same words in as captions (max 2 lines, middle of the frame) and add the music. The store's name is deliberately not written anywhere in this pack.

**Our hypothesis (not proven):** the winner works because of three things at once: a real person in her own home, a real recipient who opens it, and one concrete scarcity fact. Every video below keeps those three.

**Facts you may state about the product. Nothing else:**
- Ten sushi pieces with wooden chopsticks.
- Pizza, burger and donut, each in its own box.
- The sushi box sold out last November (confirmed by the owner 2026-09-24).

**How to read the tests.** Column 6 answers the three questions from our copy rules: can you see it / can it be false / can only we say it. For video the unit is the line together with the picture. A row with a ❌ does not go out, with one exception: the unchanged winner lines in A (and the same lines reused in B, marked "W"). Those are the control, so a ❌ there is information, not a stop.

## Must be on camera

- A real creator in her own home, face visible, speaking to the camera in her own rhythm.
- One salmon maki sock unrolled by hand, close-up.
- The tray opened and filmed from above: ten pieces, wooden chopsticks visible.
- The pizza, burger and donut boxes on the table.
- A real recipient opening it without knowing what is inside, face in frame the whole time.
- The creator's feet in the socks at the end (A and B: on the sofa).
- B only: the candle, soap and plain grey socks; the drawer; the stretch close-up; the three hook takes filmed separately.
- C only: whatever the chosen framework lists under "Must be visible".

## Never

- Never say, write or show the store's name or web address. This covers captions, voice, stickers, end cards, packaging, inserts, shipping labels, phone and browser screens. Check the box and its contents before filming; if the name or address is printed anywhere that will be in frame, turn it away or cover it.
- No prices, no offers, no discounts, no percentages, no "buy one, get one", no "free".
- No delivery times or shipping claims. No "30 days", no money-back, no guarantee, no talk of returns.
- No claims about material, softness, thickness, quality, size or fit, washing, or how long they last. Showing is allowed (stretching, putting a sock on); describing is not.
- No invented facts or numbers. Only the three product facts listed at the top.
- No dashes (long or short) in any on-screen text. No other brands or logos in frame.
- No AI-generated people, no stock footage.
- Never let anyone think it is edible food: the word "strumpor" or a sock visibly unrolled must appear within the first 10 seconds of every video.
- Never fake, coach or re-stage a reaction. Never film anyone who has not agreed to be in the video.
- Do not add music or captions in-camera; the editors do that.
