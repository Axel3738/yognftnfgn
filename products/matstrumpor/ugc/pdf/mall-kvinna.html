Film with your phone at home, vertical, daylight. You send us the raw files, no music, no captions, no filters. We cut everything. Everything below is in Swedish on camera; the English column is only so you know what each line means.

Five rules

  • Only the sushi box. The other boxes are not in this round.
  • Never say or show the store name. Cover the label on the parcel.
  • No prices, no delivery time, no guarantees, nothing about material.
  • The person who opens the box must not know what is inside. Film the reaction once. Never do it again.
  • Ask them right after if we may use the clip. No yes, no clip.

The script

This is our best-selling video. Say the lines word for word, in your own rhythm, to the camera. Give the box to someone who does not know what is inside. If that person is a man, say "han" instead of "hon".

Extra takes for video 1 · five short clips

Film these separately. We cut them in.

Each one is its own clip. The three silent ones get a caption from us, so you say nothing in them.

A. The boring presents · about 6 seconds, you speak
Kitchen table. Put down a plain candle, a bar of soap and a pair of plain grey socks, one at a time. Then look at the camera and say: "Men ingen av dom vet vad hon faktiskt gillar." (But none of them knows what she actually likes.) Then drop all three into a drawer and shut it.
B. The lid · 3 seconds, silent
Put the phone where the person will sit, pointing straight down at the closed box through the clear lid. No hands, no face. After two seconds, hands come in, lift the lid, pick one piece with the chopsticks and let it unroll into a sock.
C. The box on the table · 3 seconds, silent
Phone still on the table. Two hands slide the closed box, chopsticks on the lid, to the middle of the table and go away. Nobody speaks, nobody laughs.
D. After the meal · 3 seconds, silent
An empty plate and a fork pushed aside. Two hands slide the closed box into the free space. Then chopsticks lift one piece and it unrolls into a sock. Nothing else that looks like food in the picture.
E. The stretch · 4 seconds, you speak · optional
Close up: stretch one unrolled sock wide in front of the lens, hold it, let it snap back. Say only: "Material? Titta, jag drar i den." (Material? Look, I'm pulling on it.) Skip this one if the sock looks thin.

Video 2 · about 20 seconds

The prank: "Jag tog med sushi"

At a real fika table at work, or a dinner table at home. Prop the phone so both faces are in the picture. You put the box on the table and say you brought sushi. The other person knows nothing, lifts the lid and takes a piece with the chopsticks. Whatever happens is the video. At a dinner, say "vännen" instead of "kollegan" and "middagen" instead of "fikat".

#Say this (Swedish)MeaningWhat we see
1Jag tog med sushi.I brought sushi.You set the closed box in the middle of the table and say it to the other person, not to the camera.
2(their own words)Camera on their face. They lift the lid, look, pick up a piece. Do not help, do not explain.
3Dom sålde slut i november.They sold out in November.You pick up the phone and hold one unrolled sock up by the cuff. The other person still in the picture.
4Ge bort dom. Eller ta med till fikat.Give them away. Or bring them to the coffee break.You hold the closed box toward the lens.

Extra takes for video 2 · three silent 3-second clips

F. What they see first · silent
Phone in the other person's chair, pointing down at the closed box through the clear lid, a coffee cup in the corner. After two seconds, hands lift the lid and one piece unrolls into a sock. Film this alone, before or after the prank.
G. Set it out and wait · silent
Phone still. Table with two coffee cups. Your hands set the closed box between the cups and go away. Then, filmed after the prank: the open box from above with one piece unrolled next to it.
H. Twins · silent
From above: the closed box, pieces laid out so each one sits next to its twin of the same colour. After two seconds, hands lift the lid and one piece unrolls into a sock; its twin stays in the tray.
