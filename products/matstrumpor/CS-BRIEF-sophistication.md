# Sushi socks: market sophistication, new mechanisms, new information

**For the creative strategist and the editors. Written 2026-10-01.** Everything
below is read from the product page, the ad account, Judge.me, Meta's Ad
Library and published sources. Every number has a source and a date. Full
files (Swedish, with every source link): `products/matstrumpor/mekanismer.md`
and `products/matstrumpor/ny-information.md`.

## 1. Where the market stands (Schwartz's five stages)

| Market | Stage | Evidence (Ad Library, 1 Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| "Fun socks as a gift", Sweden | **3: the promise has been heard** | Five advertisers already say "a funny gift they'll actually use / remember": Soxo, My Angry Socks, svenskhusman_socks, Presenthuset, Bäverbutiken, and our own "Rolig i kväll. På fötterna i morgon." |
| The sushi tray, globally | **2: superlatives** | Three dropship pages run the identical headline "Sushi Box Funny Socks – The Perfect Gift & Fun Addition to Your Wardrobe!" |
| The sushi tray, Sweden | **1: we are alone** | No other Swedish advertiser runs the tray |

**What this means for every brief:** a bigger promise ("the funniest", "perfect")
does not reach the buyer any more. What reaches her is HOW this gift does what
other funny gifts do not (a mechanism), or a fact about her world she did not
know (new information). The account's only winner proves the recipe: a real
creator, a real recipient opening the box, and one true scarcity line ("sold
out last November"). AI faces, adjective listicles and AI slideshows all died.

## 2. New mechanisms (how it works), tested against the account's own transcripts

| Mechanism | Verdict | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Serve it, don't hand it over.** The tray goes on the table (dinner, fika, Christmas table) with the chopsticks; the recipient discovers it. | **New.** Never carried an ad. ## 4. The rules every line follows

- The fact is the hook (seconds 0–3). The product is the second beat. Cut
  after the full stop; let the picture show the tray.
- Numbers exactly as the source writes them, with the country when it is not
  Swedish ("i USA", "av britterna").
- Never the store name in a caption, voice or end card.
- No claims about material, quality, thickness, washing or delivery speed
  (the product page makes none). Only "399 kr", "5 par", "tio bitar", "36–44",
  "5–10 arbetsdagar" and the offers exactly as the page writes them.
- Educate, never scare. Relationship research is stated, never promised.
- Real creator, real recipient, real reaction. No generated people.
