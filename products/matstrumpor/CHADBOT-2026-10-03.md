```
Context: Swedish DTC brand, novelty gift product (apparel accessory, ~45 USD AOV, sold in bundles), hard Christmas seasonality: last December did roughly 1,500 orders, near zero in spring, and we sold out in November. Right now we are sold out again and selling on pre-order until mid-October, when a big restock lands. Home market plus a dozen foreign markets launched this week with translated UGC (~1.3k USD/day total, no verdicts yet).

Creative: ~250 ads tested this year, hit rate ~2-4%. One breakthrough: a UGC creator with a gift angle (real recipient, "sold out last November"). Her champions ad set takes ~90% of spend; ~10 iterations on her are too young to judge. Christmas angle: 0 for 11 so far. We run 3:2:2 test ad sets inside the CBO, a min-spend gate before any verdict, weekly editor reports, a post-purchase survey, and email flows live in every language.

Pipeline: ~10 UGC creators and some influencers approached, 0 footage back yet, the winning creator is negotiating a monthly deal, and our external creative strategist is slow. I have done the Q4 masterclass and already have a long BFCM site/offer checklist (promo CBO, discount ladder, seasonal statics, dates, backend), so skip all of that.

My worry: I am waiting on people instead of building. I want to be ahead of everyone in my niche before November, and I will scale hard when the stock lands.

Question: if this were your brand in the first week of October, what are the 10 things you would start working on right now, outside the BFCM checklist, to be ready to scale in November and December? For each: what it is, why it matters for a seasonal gift product, and what you would check or measure first. Rank by leverage. Separate what can be done with zero new footage (research, offers, audiences, whitelisting or partnership ads, landing pages, markets, budget ramp) from what needs footage. Where the course has a lesson on it, name it.
```
