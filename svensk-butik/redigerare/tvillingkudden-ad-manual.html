The product — locked facts (never change these)

🔒 What we sell
  • Tvillingkudden — a plush pillow cut to the pet's own outline. The fabric pieces are sewn together and turned inside out, so the seam sits inside: no visible border, no white margin, no embroidered edge. Knitted polyester, printed on both sides, fully sewn, no zipper. Machine washable 30°C.
  • One size: 45 cm tall. Price: 599 kr incl. VAT. Free tracked shipping in Sweden.
  • How ordering works: order → reply to the order confirmation with a photo → approve a digital draft → sewn to order, home in 8–12 business days.
  • Guarantee: nothing is sewn until the customer approves the draft. Wrong vs. the approved draft → remade free.
🚫 Never in any ad
  • No discounts, sales, countdowns or urgency. The store says "the price you see is the price you pay" — and a new store may not legally advertise reference prices in Sweden.
  • No reviews, star ratings, "verified customer" or "10,000+ sold". We have zero customers. Invented proof is illegal and contradicts our own site.
  • No "made in Sweden" — it is sewn abroad and the site says so openly.
  • No fast-delivery claims. Only "8–12 business days" is true.
  • Never mention returns or right of withdrawal. Custom-made = no ångerrätt; the draft approval is the safety net.

If a winning ad you copy relies on any of these, keep its structure and replace that part.

The 4 concepts for this product

A concept is the main message; a hook is the first 3 seconds. Note: this product does not use the usual Clearance Sale and Social Proof concepts — they are replaced below, for the reasons in the red box.

Concept 1
Demo Most important

Photo becomes pillow. Show the transformation: phone photo → digital draft → the finished pillow, hugged on the sofa.

Hook seed: the ordinary, slightly goofy everyday photo works better than a posed one — "that photo in your camera roll? It's a pillow now."
Concept 2 · replaces Social Proof
Recognition

The pet reacts to its own pillow — sniffing, head-tilt, stealing it. Honest social proof without fake reviews: the dog itself is the witness.

Hook seed: "Even the dog thinks it's the dog." We already have a working clip of this motif (see Assets).
Concept 3 · replaces Clearance Sale
Longing

One story only: the daughter who moved away to study and misses the family dog. New town, tiny dorm room, and every time she calls home the first question is whether she can talk to the dog. The buyer is the parent — sending her the one thing she actually misses from home.

This is the angle that carries best for the US original, and Sweden is full of students in corridor rooms. Keep it to that one story; do not widen it to "everyone who lives apart from a pet".

Taste rules: never show or say that a pet died, no sad music manipulation. The feeling is distance, not loss.

Hook seed: "She calls home to talk to the dog."
Concept 4
Gift

Talks to the gift-buyer, not the pet owner: the partner, the adult child buying for mom/grandma, the friend of a dog person. Sell the receiver's reaction and the pride of finding "the perfect gift".

Hook seed: grandma laughing with her schnauzer pillow while the real schnauzer stares at it (see Assets — we have this image).
