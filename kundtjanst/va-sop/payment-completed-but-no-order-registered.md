# SOP: Payment completed but no order registered

**This is the same case as "Money charged but no order visible".** Two SOPs were
written for it; the procedure is one.

**Open that page and follow it.** Holding reply first, then check orders, abandoned
checkouts, drafts and the payment record, then the owner decides.

---

## The two things that differ, and neither changes the steps

- The customer here usually says "I paid and got nothing", rather than "money is
  missing". Same investigation.
- They sometimes have a payment reference from their bank. If they do, send it to the
  owner with the rest — it is what the payment provider searches on.

---

## Do not

- Do not refund, and do not create the order yourself. Both need the owner.
- Do not promise a number of days for money to come back. That is the bank's, not
  ours.
- Do not say "the payment failed" before you have seen the payment record. A captured
  payment with no order is a different problem from a failed checkout, and the
  customer will quote you either way.
