# SOP: Invoice requests and companies wanting to buy

**Use this when** a customer asks for an invoice or a receipt, or a business asks to
buy against an invoice.

Two different cases. Read which one you have before you answer.

---

## A — A private customer wants a receipt or invoice

**Owner approval:** not needed.

1. **The order confirmation email is the receipt.** It lists the items, the amount and
   the date. Tell them that first.
2. Ask them to search their inbox **and spam** for the support address of that store
   (Store facts). It is the sender.
3. Not there? Look the order up in the admin and resend the confirmation. If the email
   address on the order is misspelled, correct it on the order first, then resend.

> Hej [NAMN]! Orderbekräftelsen du fick när du handlade är ditt kvitto — där står
> varorna, summan och datumet. Sök i inkorgen och i skräpposten efter avsändaren
> [SUPPORTADRESS UR STORE FACTS]. Hittar du den inte skickar jag den igen, säg bara
> till.

---

## B — A business wants to buy on invoice

**Owner approval:** not needed. The answer is the same every time.

We sell to private individuals. We do not invoice companies, and there is no
exception the VA can make.

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för intresset. Vi säljer i dag till privatpersoner och kan inte
> fakturera företag, så beställningen behöver läggas som en vanlig order i butiken.
> Orderbekräftelsen fungerar som kvitto och våra företagsuppgifter — organisationsnummer
> och adress — skickar jag gärna om du behöver dem för bokföringen.

*Meaning: thanks for the interest; we sell to private individuals and cannot invoice
companies, so the order goes through the shop as normal; the order confirmation is the
receipt and I can send our company details for your bookkeeping.*

**Do not** promise that we might do it later, and do not forward the customer to the
owner. If they insist or the order is unusually large, note it and tell the owner —
but the answer to the customer stays the same.

⚠️ **Do not say "we sell tax-free" or "momsfritt".** The old version of this SOP put
that sentence in the VA's mouth with no explanation. If a customer asks why VAT is
not shown, say you will check and ask the owner.

---

## C — Definition of done

- [ ] Which case it is, decided before answering.
- [ ] **A:** confirmation named as the receipt, spam check asked for, resent if
      missing, address on the order corrected first if wrong.
- [ ] **B:** declined in one message, company details offered, nothing promised.
- [ ] Reply in the customer's language, same day.
- [ ] Unusually large business request noted to the owner **after** the customer was
      answered.
