# SOP: Wrong product delivered

**Use this when** a customer says the item they received is not the one they ordered
— wrong model, wrong size, wrong colour, or someone else's order entirely.

**Owner approval:** required before you promise a replacement, a refund or a return
label. Asking for photos needs no approval.

---

## 1. Ask for photos, promise nothing

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för att du säger till, och ursäkta att det blev fel.
>
> Kan du skicka tre bilder så ordnar jag det snabbast möjligt: en på varan du fick, en
> på förpackningen, och en på fraktetiketten. Då ser jag direkt vad som gått fel.

*Meaning: sorry about that; send three photos — the item, the packaging, the shipping
label — and I will sort it.*

Write in the customer's language (DeepL). Do not offer a solution in this first
message. The photos decide which one it is.

**No photos after three days:** send one reminder. Still nothing after another three
days: note it on the order and close the case politely. Do not chase forever.

---

## 2. Check what was actually ordered and sent

1. Open the order: which **variant** was ordered — size, colour, model?
2. Read the fulfillment: which variant was picked and shipped?
3. Compare with the photo of the item and the shipping label.
4. Check the fulfillment timeline that this parcel really is the one delivered to
   this customer.

Three different faults, three different owners:

| What you see | What it is |
|---|---|
| Ordered A, fulfilment says A, photo shows B | The supplier packed the wrong item |
| Ordered A, fulfilment says B | Picked wrong on our side |
| The label has someone else's name | Two parcels swapped — tell the owner immediately, another customer has this one's item |

---

## 3. Send the owner the case

```
Wrong product — [STORE]
Order: [ORDER NUMBER]      Customer: [EMAIL]
Ordered: [VARIANT]         Fulfilled: [VARIANT]        Received: [WHAT THE PHOTO SHOWS]
Label name matches the customer: yes / no
Photos: attached
My reading: supplier packed wrong / picked wrong here / parcels swapped
```

The owner decides: replacement at no cost, full refund, or return first. Tell the
customer you have reported it and will come back the same day the decision lands.

---

## 4. When the decision comes

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för bilderna — du har helt rätt, det är fel vara. Vi
> [skickar en ny till dig utan kostnad / betalar tillbaka hela beloppet].
>
> [Om ersättning:] Du får ett nytt leveransmejl med knappen "Spåra paketet" och ett
> eget paketnummer så snart den skickats.
>
> [Om retur begärs:] Behåll den felaktiga varan tills vi hör av oss om hur den ska
> skickas tillbaka.
>
> Ursäkta besväret.

Never ask the customer to pay the return postage on our mistake. If the owner wants
the wrong item back, we arrange it.

---

## 5. Definition of done

- [ ] Photos of item, packaging and shipping label requested in the first reply.
- [ ] Ordered variant compared with fulfilled variant and with the photo.
- [ ] Label checked against the customer's name — swapped parcels escalated at once.
- [ ] Owner messaged with the block in §3, photos attached.
- [ ] Customer answered the same day the decision landed.
- [ ] Outcome written on the order so the next person sees it.
