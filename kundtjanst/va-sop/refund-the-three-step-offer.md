# SOP: The three-step refund offer

**Use this when** a customer has changed their mind and wants to send the item back,
and nothing is wrong with it. The offer is: keep the product and take part of the
money back, instead of posting it to us.

**Owner approval:** required for every refund, 30 %, 50 % or full.

---

## 0. First: is this actually a change of mind?

Run this check before you offer anything. The offer is for buyer's remorse only.

| What the customer says | Where it goes instead |
|---|---|
| The item arrived broken or damaged | Product arrived broken |
| Wrong item, or something missing | Wrong product delivered |
| It never arrived | Order not arrived within expected timeframe |
| **They invoke their statutory right of withdrawal** (ångerrätt, angrerett, fortrydelsesret, peruuttamisoikeus) | Customer wants to return product — **full refund, no offer** |

⚠️ **A customer who invokes the statutory right of withdrawal is entitled to a full
refund.** It runs alongside the store's own return policy and cannot be signed away.
Do not steer them into a partial refund — offer the return and take it from there.
The window is in Store facts and it counts **from the day they received the item**.

---

## 1. Step one — 30 %

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för att du hör av dig, och tråkigt att [VARAN] inte blev som du
> tänkt dig.
>
> Innan du postar tillbaka den: vi kan betala tillbaka 30 % av vad du betalade, och
> du behåller produkten. Då slipper du returfrakten och besväret med att packa och
> posta, och pengarna är på väg tillbaka samma dag. Passar den inte dig kanske den
> passar någon annan i familjen.
>
> Säg bara till om du vill ha det så, annars ordnar vi returen som vanligt.

*Meaning: sorry it was not what you hoped for. Before you post it back: 30 % back and
you keep the product, no return postage, no packing, money on its way the same day.
Say the word, or we arrange the return as normal.*

---

## 2. Step two — 50 %

Only if they say no to step one.

> Hej igen [NAMN]! Jag förstår. Jag kan gå upp till 50 % tillbaka om du behåller
> produkten — det är så långt jag kan sträcka mig. Vill du hellre returnera den
> ordnar vi det direkt i stället, säg bara till.

*Meaning: I understand. I can go to 50 % back if you keep it, that is as far as I can
go. If you would rather return it, we do that instead.*

⚠️ **Never write that you "discussed it internally" or "asked if there was anything
more we could do" unless it happened.** The old version of this SOP told everyone to
write that sentence to every customer. It is untrue, it reads as a script the moment a
customer gets it twice, and it is the kind of thing that ends up in a screenshot.

---

## 3. Step three — the normal return

If they say no again, or they are clearly unhappy, stop offering. Hand them the
return.

> Självklart, vi ordnar returen. Jag skickar adressen och hur du gör i nästa mejl.

Then follow **Return address and return instructions**. The full refund is made once
the parcel is back and has been checked.

**Stop offering earlier than this if the customer is upset.** Pushing a third time
turns a return into a dispute, and a dispute costs more than the return.

---

## 4. How to actually do it

1. Get the owner's approval for the amount.
2. In the admin: the order → **Refund** → type the agreed amount → refund.
3. Write on the order: what was agreed, which step, who approved, and the date.
4. Tell the customer it is done. **Do not give a number of days** for it to appear —
   that is their bank's, not ours.

---

## 5. Definition of done

- [ ] Reason for the return checked — this page is for change of mind only.
- [ ] No statutory right of withdrawal invoked (if it was: full refund, no offer).
- [ ] Offer made at most twice, and stopped early if the customer was upset.
- [ ] Nothing untrue written in the email.
- [ ] Owner approved the amount before it was paid.
- [ ] Refund made in the admin and written on the order with the step and the date.

⚠️ **OWNER: the partial-refund offer against the statutory right of withdrawal.**
Where a customer is inside the statutory window, they can demand a full refund even
after accepting a partial one. Confirm how far you want the offer pushed inside that
window before this is used at volume.
