# PRODUCT ARRIVED BROKEN (SOP: Customer Received Damaged Product)

> Works for every store. "The store's tracking page" and "the store's support address"
> are the values in the **Store facts** table at the bottom.

## Use this when

A customer writes that [fill from Store facts] arrived damaged or broken. Not for: parcel not arrived (delivery SOP), wrong item, or a fault after weeks of use — ask the owner once and write the answer into Store facts.

## OVERVIEW

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Customer says the product arrived damaged during shipping. |
| First step | Ask for photos of the product AND the packaging if not attached. Do nothing else until you have them. |
| Owner approval | **Required before you promise a replacement, refund or compensation** (fact pack §5; the old "not required for replacement" wording is withdrawn (⚠️ OWNER: confirm this)). Asking for photos needs no approval. |
| Where to look | Shopify admin → the order → fulfillment timeline; the store's tracking page ("Tracking page — how to look up any parcel"). |
| Reply time | Within 1 business day. |
| Delivery promise | See **Store facts**. Never quote it in a reply that carries the tracking link |

## STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1 – Open the order in Shopify

1. Search by order number or email.
2. Note order date, fulfilment date, shipping method and which product or part is damaged.
3. Read the **fulfillment timeline** on the order page: the carrier scans are there. Confirm the parcel is marked delivered and note the date. Not delivered → delivery SOP, not this one.
4. The carrier tracking number is for internal use only. The customer never sees it.

### Step 2 – Get the photos

1. No photos → send **Reply template A** and stop. Never assess or promise anything without photos.
2. Photos must show the damaged product and, if possible, the outer packaging.

### Step 3 – Assess the damage

1. Is the **whole product** damaged, or only a part or accessory ([fill from Store facts])?
2. **Shipping damage**: packaging crushed or torn, product damage matches it, reported right after delivery. **Manufacturing defect**: packaging intact, product broken out of the box. **Customer damage**: happened after use. Unsure → write what you see; the owner decides.
3. Do not offer refunds or returns proactively.
4. Internal note on the order: arrived damaged, photos received, whole product or part, shipping / defect / unclear.

### Step 4 – Owner approval, then offer the solution

1. Send the owner: order number, delivery date, photos, your assessment and the option you propose.
2. Once approved, send **Reply template B**.
3. Return or discard the damaged item: (⚠️ OWNER: per case). Do not ask the customer to send anything back unless told.
4. Customer asks for a refund or return instead → follow the refund/return SOP ("3 Step Process") in this database.

### Step 5 – Create the replacement order

1. Confirm the shipping address with the customer.
2. Create the replacement order in Shopify at zero cost, so it is not counted as a new sale and sends no payment request (⚠️ OWNER: the exact method).
3. Internal notes: arrived damaged, photo evidence, approved by owner on date, original order number.

### Step 6 – Confirm and follow the replacement

1. Send **Reply template C** in the customer's language.
2. When the replacement is fulfilled, the customer automatically gets the shipping emails with the "Track your parcel" button and their store parcel number (BB-… or CS-…). Silent tracking the first 2–4 days after shipping is normal.
3. Customer asks for status → read the fulfillment timeline on the replacement order, open the store's tracking page with the carrier number, copy the **store parcel number** the page shows, and send it with the link <tracking page>?nummer=<parcel number>. Never send the YT…/4PX… carrier number. Details: "Tracking page — how to look up any parcel".
4. Backup only: carrier portals (17track.net, or the carrier named on the fulfillment) — same scans. Use them when the page still says "We can't find that number" after the look-up page's checks, or for a store without a page.
5. No new scan for 7 days, or past day 14 undelivered → check the carrier's own tracking, request a carrier investigation, escalate. Never say "lost" until the carrier confirms non-delivery.

## ESCALATION

- Repeated compensation demands, refund beyond standard policy, or unclear damage cause → owner before replying.
- Threatens a chargeback → follow the dispute handbook (chargeback SOPs in this database) and tell the owner the same day.
- Replacement undelivered by day 14 → carrier investigation + owner.

## Store facts

Every store-specific value — tracking page, parcel number prefix, support address,
delivery promise, return window — is on the page **Store facts**. Take it from there
and never type a store name, a domain or an address into this procedure.


> Never quote another customer's parcel. Never send the carrier number to a customer —
> always the store parcel number and the store's page link.
