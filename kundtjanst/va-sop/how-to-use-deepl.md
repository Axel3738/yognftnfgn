# SOP: Translating customer replies with DeepL

**Use this when** you are writing to a customer in a language you do not write
fluently. Our customers write Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish and English.

---

## 1. How

1. Open **deepl.com/translator**.
2. Write the reply in English, clearly and simply. Short sentences translate well;
   idioms do not.
3. Translate into the customer's language — the language **they wrote in**, or the
   store's language if they never wrote (Store facts).
4. Read the result. If a sentence looks odd in the original, it will be worse in
   translation. Rewrite the English and translate again.

**Answer in the language the customer used.** Not the store's language, not English
because it is easier.

---

## 2. Strip personal data before you paste

DeepL is a third-party website. Take out the customer's name, email, address, order
number and parcel number before you paste, and put them back in afterwards.

Translate this:

> Hi [NAME], your parcel [NUMBER] is on its way. You can follow it here: [LINK].

Not the version with their real name and address in it.

---

## 3. Where machine translation is not enough

Do not let DeepL write these — take them from the SOP that covers the case, which is
already written in both languages:

- Anything about refunds, returns or money.
- Anything about a delivery date or a promise.
- Anything legal: right of withdrawal, return window, customs.

The reply templates in the SOPs are Swedish with the English meaning underneath
exactly so these sentences never have to be invented.

---

## 4. If DeepL stops translating

The free version has a daily limit. When you hit it, **stop and tell the owner** — we
buy a licence rather than work around it. Do not open private windows to get past the
limit; that breaks their terms and it is not a professional way for a company to run.

Meanwhile: use the SOP templates, which are already translated.

---

## 5. Definition of done

- [ ] Reply is in the language the customer wrote in.
- [ ] Personal data was not pasted into DeepL.
- [ ] Money, dates and legal wording came from an SOP template, not from DeepL.
- [ ] Translation read through before sending.
