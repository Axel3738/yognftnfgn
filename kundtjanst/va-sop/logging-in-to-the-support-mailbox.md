# SOP: Logging in to the support mailbox

**Use this when** you start a shift, or you need to read and answer customer email
for one of the stores.

---

## 1. Where to log in

Go to **https://webmail.loopia.se/** and sign in with the mailbox address and its
password.

**The username is the full email address**, not the part before the @.

---

## 2. Which mailbox

One mailbox per store. The address is the support address for that store, and it is
in **Store facts** — never in this page, so it cannot drift out of date.

Answer each store's customers from **that store's** mailbox. A Swedish customer who
gets a reply from the Norwegian store's address will not believe the email is from
the shop they bought from.

---

## 3. Passwords

**The passwords are not written in this SOP, and they must not be written into any
document, Notion page or chat message.** The owner gives them to you once, in a
password manager or directly, and you keep them there.

⚠️ If you find a password written in a document, tell the owner so it can be changed
and removed. A shared password in a shared document is the same as no password.

---

## 4. What the mailbox is, and is not

- It is where customers reach us and where you answer them.
- The **Sent** folder is how anyone can see what was answered and when. Always reply
  from the mailbox, never from a private address.
- Everything in it is customer data: names, addresses, order numbers. Do not paste it
  into third-party tools without stripping the personal parts (see "Translating
  customer replies with DeepL").

---

## 5. When you get in

1. Read the oldest unanswered mail first, not the newest. The oldest one is the
   angriest.
2. Find the case in this SOP base and follow it. The start page lists them by what the
   customer asked.
3. Anything with an evidence deadline — a payment dispute — comes before everything
   else that day.

---

## 6. Definition of done

- [ ] Logged in to the **correct store's** mailbox for the customer you are answering.
- [ ] Password came from the password manager, not from a document.
- [ ] Replies sent from the mailbox so they land in Sent.

⚠️ **OWNER: two things to fix once.** A password was printed in plain text three
times in the old version of this page — change it and keep it in a password manager.
And that page listed only one brand's mailboxes while sitting in another brand's SOP
base; the addresses now come from Store facts instead.
