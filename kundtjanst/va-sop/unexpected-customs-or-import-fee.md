# SOP: Unexpected customs or import fee

**Use this when** a customer is charged a customs or import fee on delivery and did
not expect it.

**Owner approval:** required for any refund or goodwill payment. Explaining the rule
needs no approval.

---

## 1. Check the order before you answer

Do not recite the policy at someone whose case is different.

1. **Which market did they buy in?** (the order's country and currency). Customs
   rules differ per country — take the line for that market from **Store facts**.
2. **Has the parcel been delivered, or is it being held?** A held parcel that the
   customer refuses is a different case, see §4.
3. **How much is the fee?** A small fee and a large one get different answers from the
   owner.

---

## 2. The answer

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för att du hör av dig, och tråkigt att det kom en avgift du inte
> räknat med — jag förstår att det är en tråkig överraskning.
>
> Varan skickas från vårt lager utomlands, och när ett paket passerar tullen kan
> tullavgift och importmoms tillkomma. Den avgiften tas ut av tullen och fraktbolaget,
> inte av oss, och den står kunden för. Det står i våra fraktvillkor: [POLICY-LÄNK UR
> STORE FACTS].
>
> Jag beklagar att det inte var tydligare innan du handlade. Vill du skicka en bild på
> avgiftsavin tar jag upp den med ägaren.

*Meaning: sorry for the surprise; the goods ship from our warehouse abroad and customs
may add duty and import VAT; that fee is charged by customs and the carrier, not by
us, and it is the customer's; here is the policy; send a photo of the charge and I
will raise it with the owner.*

Write in the customer's language (DeepL).

**Never** say the fee is "normal" or "everyone pays it" — say who charges it and why.
**Never** promise that a future order will be fee-free.

---

## 3. What not to claim

- Do not name a country the order did not go to. Customs is the **customer's**
  country, not ours.
- Do not quote a VAT rule you have not read. If the customer asks for the exact rate
  or threshold, say you are checking and ask the owner.
- Do not state that our prices "exclude VAT" as an explanation. It is a separate
  question and it confuses the customer further.

---

## 4. The customer refuses the parcel because of the fee

This happens, and it costs more than the fee. The parcel goes back to the sender.

1. Tell the owner **before** the customer refuses if you can — a refused parcel is
   rarely recoverable.
2. If it is already refused, continue with "Package returned to sender without
   delivery".
3. Whether the customer gets the full amount back or the amount minus shipping is the
   owner's call, not yours.

---

## 5. Escalate

Send the owner: order number, market, fee amount, the photo of the charge, and
whether the customer has accepted or refused the parcel. Then wait.

⚠️ **Several customers with fees in the same market in the same week is not a support
problem.** It means the market needs its own line in Store facts about customs, and
the customer should be told before checkout, not after. Say that in the message.

---

## 6. Definition of done

- [ ] Market and currency of the order read before answering.
- [ ] Answer names who charges the fee and links the policy from Store facts.
- [ ] No claim about a country, a rate or a threshold that is not written down.
- [ ] Refused parcel handed to the returned-to-sender procedure.
- [ ] Owner messaged if a refund or goodwill is on the table, or if the market keeps
      producing fees.

⚠️ **OWNER: customs in the English-speaking markets.** Duty can be charged at the
door in some of them. What should the VA say to a customer there — before purchase
and after? Until answered, the VA says only that the goods ship from abroad and any
customs charge is the customer's.
