# Matstrumpor: the delivery delay email, and who gets a refund

*Written 2026-10-03, the day the owner decided to send it.*

## What is happening

Matstrumpor parcels are delayed. There is a disruption in the supply chain, and deliveries can take
**5-10 business days longer than usual**. Do not add any other details about the cause in your
replies.

Most parcels say "fulfilled" in Shopify, but that only means a shipping label exists. Many of them
have not been picked up by a carrier yet.

## What the customers get

An apology email from Matstrumpor, in the customer's own language:

- **Who:** everyone who bought from 23 September until the morning of 3 October (the email goes
  out one day after the order). New orders after that do not get it: the store now sells the
  boxes as a **pre-order** and tells the customer before checkout that the boxes ship on
  12 October. A pre-order customer already agreed to wait, so do not apologise for a delay.
- **What it says:** the delivery can take 5-10 business days longer than usual.
- **The offer:** if the parcel has not been shipped yet, the customer can get a **full refund** by
  replying with their order number. Customers who keep the order do nothing.

Replies land in kundsupport@matstrumpor.se like every other email. The auto-reply bot flags refund
requests to you. It never refunds anything itself.

## Who gets a full refund (the owner's decision, 2026-10-03)

**Every order whose parcel has not been scanned by a carrier yet.** Not only unfulfilled orders.

1. Find the order in Shopify (order number, or the customer's email address).
2. **Unfulfilled** (no tracking number): full refund, shipping included. Cancel the order.
3. **Fulfilled:** open the tracking page with the parcel number (starts with **MS-**) and read the
   headline at the top.
   - **"Paketet är bokat"** ("The parcel is booked"): no carrier has the parcel yet. **Full refund**,
     shipping included.
   - **"Paketet är på väg"** ("The parcel is on its way") or anything later: the parcel is moving.
     The refund offer does not apply. Reply kindly that it is on its way, give the tracking link and
     the MS- number, and say it can take 5-10 business days longer than usual. If they still want to
     return it when it arrives, the normal return policy applies.
4. No order number and you cannot find the order from the email address: ask for the order number
   once.
5. Every refund under this offer: no questions, no counter-offer, no partial refund. Reply that it
   is done.

Never promise a delivery date, and never promise when the money is back on their account.

## Other emails while this lasts

- **"Where is my parcel?"** Same as always: the tracking page. Add that delivery can take 5-10
  business days longer than usual right now.
- **Angry, or a chargeback threat:** handle as usual, and offer the full refund if the tracking page
  says "Paketet är bokat" or the order is unfulfilled.
