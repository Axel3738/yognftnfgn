# SOP: Suspicious or phishing email in our name

**Use this when** a customer reports an email that looks like it came from us but the
sender address is odd, or it asks for card details, a password or a payment.

**Urgency: same day.** Someone is using the store's name against its customers.

**Owner approval:** required before you confirm or deny that an email is genuinely
ours.

---

## 1. First reply — protect the customer, promise nothing

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för att du säger till, det var precis rätt att höra av dig.
>
> Klicka inte på några länkar i mejlet och svara inte på det så länge. Kan du skicka
> mig: avsändaradressen exakt som den står, ämnesraden, och gärna en skärmbild av
> mejlet? Vidarebefordra det inte — en skärmbild räcker och är säkrare.
>
> Våra mejl kommer alltid från [SUPPORTADRESS UR STORE FACTS], och våra leveransmejl
> länkar bara till vår egen spårningssida på [BUTIKENS DOMÄN]. Vi ber aldrig om
> kortuppgifter, lösenord eller betalning via mejl.
>
> Jag kollar upp det här direkt och återkommer.

*Meaning: do not click anything and do not reply to it; send me the exact sender
address, the subject line and a screenshot; our emails always come from our support
address and our shipping emails only link to our own tracking page; we never ask for
card details, passwords or payment by email; I am looking into it now.*

Write in the customer's language (DeepL).

**Do not write "that email is fake" or "that one is ours" before the owner has
looked.** You do not yet know which it is, and both guesses are expensive.

---

## 2. If the customer already clicked or paid

Say this immediately, before anything else:

> Om du hunnit ange kortuppgifter: ring din bank och spärra kortet nu. Har du fyllt i
> ett lösenord du använder på andra ställen, byt det där också.

Their bank is faster than we are. Then continue with §1 and tell the owner it is an
active case, not a report.

---

## 3. Tell the owner, same day

Send: sender address, subject line, screenshot, what the email asked for, whether the
customer clicked or paid, and the order number if they have one.

**Then check whether it is one person or many.** Search the support inbox for the same
sender address or subject line. Several customers reporting the same email is an
attack on the store, not a curiosity — say that in the message.

---

## 4. Keep the evidence

Save the screenshot with the date and the customer's order number. If the owner
reports it to the domain host or the bank later, that file is what they send.

---

## 5. Definition of done

- [ ] Customer told not to click and not to forward, the same day.
- [ ] Sender address, subject line and screenshot collected.
- [ ] Our real support address and tracking domain confirmed to the customer from
      Store facts.
- [ ] Clicked or paid → bank and password advice given first.
- [ ] Owner messaged the same day, with whether other customers reported the same.
- [ ] Screenshot saved.
- [ ] Nothing confirmed or denied about the email's authenticity before the owner
      answered.

you look? Until you answer, the VA uses the escalation channel in Store facts and
chases after two hours.
