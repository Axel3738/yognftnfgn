# SOP: Refund not received after an approved return

**Use this when** a customer says they returned the item and the money has not come
back.

**Owner approval:** required before you confirm that a refund is done or promise one.
Checking the facts needs no approval.

---

## 1. The question that decides the case: did the parcel arrive?

Most of these are not slow refunds. They are returns that never reached us, or
reached us without a name on them.

1. **Read the order** — is a refund already recorded on it? If yes, you have your
   answer; go to §3.
2. **Read the return tracking number** (noted on the order when the address was sent).
   A return parcel is **not** on our own tracking page — paste the number on
   17track.net or the carrier's site.
   - Delivered to us → the refund is ours to chase. Go to §2.
   - Still in transit → tell the customer where it is, and that we refund once it
     arrives. Nothing to escalate yet.
   - No tracking number was ever given → ask the customer for it before anything else.
3. **No refund on the order, parcel delivered to us** → the owner, today.

---

## 2. What to send the owner

```
Refund chase — [STORE]
Order: [ORDER NUMBER]        Amount: [AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]
Return approved: [DATE]      Address sent: [DATE]
Return tracking: [NUMBER] — delivered to us [DATE] / in transit / none
Refund on the order: none / partial [AMOUNT] on [DATE]
Customer has waited: [N] days since delivery back to us
```

Chase once if it is quiet for 24 hours. Meanwhile the customer gets §3B, not silence.

---

## 3. Reply templates

**A — The refund is done**

> Hej [NAMN]! Återbetalningen på [BELOPP] [VALUTA] för order [ORDERNUMMER] är gjord
> och gick tillbaka till samma betalsätt som du betalade med. Hur snabbt den syns på
> ditt konto beror på din bank. Syns den inte alls om några dagar, säg till så tar jag
> tag i det igen.

**B — We have the parcel, the refund is not done yet**

> Hej [NAMN]! Din retur är framme hos oss och jag har bett om att återbetalningen
> görs. Jag hör av mig så snart pengarna är på väg tillbaka — du behöver inte påminna
> mig.

**C — The parcel has not reached us**

> Hej [NAMN]! Jag har följt returen på spårningsnumret du skickade, och den är ännu
> inte framme hos oss. Senaste händelsen är [STATUS] den [DATUM]. Så fort paketet
> kommer fram och kontrollerats betalar vi tillbaka. Jag håller koll och hör av mig.

**D — No return tracking number**

> Hej [NAMN]! För att hitta din retur behöver jag spårningsnumret från kvittot du fick
> när du postade paketet. Skicka det så följer jag paketet åt dig.

Write in the customer's language (DeepL). **Never claim a refund was made before you
have seen it on the order**, and never give a number of days for it to appear.

---

## 4. Close the loop

The customer should not have to ask twice. When the owner confirms the refund, reply
the same day with template A and write it on the order.

---

## 5. Definition of done

- [ ] Order checked for an existing refund.
- [ ] Return tracking read on the carrier's site — not on our own tracking page.
- [ ] Case correctly identified: refunded / with us but unrefunded / still in transit /
      no tracking number.
- [ ] Owner messaged with the block in §2 where a decision is needed, chased after 24 h.
- [ ] Customer answered with the matching template, no promised dates.
- [ ] Customer told the day the refund goes through.
