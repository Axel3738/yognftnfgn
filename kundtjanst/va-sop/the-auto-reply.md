# The auto-reply: what it answers, what it leaves to you, how you see it

Internal use only. Same for every store — the store values are in **Store facts**.

## What it is

A robot reads the support inbox every hour and sorts every new customer email into
one of three piles. It has no AI in it: it follows fixed rules, and it only writes
facts it can read in Shopify or on the tracking page. It never invents a date, a
refund or a promise.

**Today it runs in dry-run mode.** That means it writes its replies into the
**Drafts** folder and sends nothing. The owner switches it to live once 20 drafts in
a row have been correct. Until then, every draft in Drafts is yours to read, send
or delete — see "Drafts" below.

## The three piles

| Pile | What the customer wrote | What the robot does |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | Where is my parcel? · When will it arrive? · How long is delivery? · Change my address (before shipping) · Opening hours · Company / org number · Tracking says delivered but I have nothing · Item broken / wrong / missing, written calmly · How do I return? | **Answers itself.** Facts from Shopify and the tracking page. Then it stars the email so you still see it |
| **Upset** | Angry wording, threats (bank, review, authority), CAPS, third email with no reply | **Calms the customer** ("I fully understand your frustration…", the problem in plain words, "escalated as urgent, reply within 48 hours"), then moves the email to the folder **VA-PRIO** and stars it. **You reply next, within 48 hours** |
| **For you** | Return decision · Refund · Exchange or size · Dispute / chargeback · Customs · Discount · Invoice · Anything it cannot verify | **Does nothing** except put a star on it. You answer it from the SOP page for that question |

It skips newsletters, Shopify's own notifications, auto-replies and anything with an
attachment it has not read.

**One rule above all: the robot answers a thread once, ever.** If the customer writes
again, the second email always goes to you. If you have written to a customer in the
last 14 days, the robot leaves that customer alone entirely.

## How you see what it did — in the webmail, no new tool

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| The **replied arrow** on an email in INBOX | The robot (or you) already answered this thread. Do not answer it again unless the customer wrote something new |
| A reply in **Sent** signed with the store's support name | That is the robot's reply. Read it before you continue the conversation — the customer has already read it |
| A **star** on an email in INBOX | The robot could not answer it. It is yours |
| The folder **VA-PRIO** | Upset customers who got a calming reply. **Open this folder first every morning.** Answer these before anything else, within 48 hours of the robot's reply |
| A draft in **Drafts** in the robot's wording | Dry-run mode. The robot would have sent this. Read it: correct → send it; wrong → delete it and write your own. Never send a draft without reading it |

## Your morning, in order

1. Open Discord **#customer-service**. The **Morning list** is posted there before your
   shift starts: the VA-PRIO emails, the starred emails that are still unanswered,
   and any dispute with a deadline within 3 days. You are tagged in it — nothing
   urgent can pass you by.
2. Open the folder **VA-PRIO**. Answer every email there. Move it back to INBOX when
   done, so the folder is empty at the end of your shift.
3. Go through the **starred** emails in INBOX, oldest first. Remove the star when
   answered.
4. Read **Drafts**. Send or delete each robot draft.
5. Everything else in INBOX as usual.

## When the robot got it wrong

It happens. Two things, both the same day:

1. Reply to the customer yourself with the correct information.
2. Post one line in Discord **#customer-service**:
   `AUTO-REPLY WRONG — #order — what was wrong, in one sentence`

That line is how the owner counts the robot's mistakes. No post, no count, no fix.

## Escalation

Same channel as everything else: Discord **#customer-service**, tag the owner.
Use the message shape in **60 — ESCALATION**: one question, your own recommendation,
and what you will do if nobody answers. The robot never escalates to the owner — it
escalates to **you**, through VA-PRIO.

## What the robot will never do

- Send a second reply in a thread, or reply to a customer you wrote to in the last 14 days.
- Promise a refund, a replacement, a discount or a delivery date it did not read in Shopify.
- Mention another customer's order.
- Reply to anything with the words dispute, chargeback, Klarna dispute, ARN.
- Delete or mark as read.

If you see it do any of these, post it in #customer-service the same day.
