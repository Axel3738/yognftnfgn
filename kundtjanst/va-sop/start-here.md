# Start here

Welcome. This base is everything customer support needs to answer a customer without
asking anyone first. Read this page once; after that you only open the page for the
question in front of you.

---

## 1. We are one group of stores

We run several stores. They sell different products in different countries, but the
back end is the same everywhere: the same admin, the same payment flow, the same
tracking system, the same rules.

**So the procedures below are the same for every store.** The only thing that changes
per store is the page **Store facts**: the tracking page, the parcel number prefix,
the support address, the return window, the delivery promise. When a procedure says
"take it from Store facts", that is why.

If a page ever tells you something that contradicts Store facts, Store facts wins and
you tell the owner.

⚠️ **One exception:** Grillkliniken has no tracking page. There the old
carrier-portal procedure applies — look the parcel up on the carrier's site instead.

## 3. Four rules that override everything

1. **No refund, no accepted return, and never "your parcel is lost", without the
   owner's approval.** Those three sentences cost money and cannot be taken back. The
   pages tell you when to ask.
2. **Never write something you have not checked.** Not a delivery date, not "that
   sounds wrong", not "it is on its way" before you have read the tracking. It is
   quoted back to us in disputes.
3. **Answer in the language the customer wrote in.** Templates are in the pages;
   DeepL does the rest.
4. **Answer fast.** The target is in Store facts. A customer who waits opens a
   dispute, and a dispute costs far more than the thing they were asking about.

---

## 4. When you are unsure

Ask the owner in the escalation channel (Store facts). One message with the facts
beats a guess, every time.

Tell the customer you are checking and will come back — **never leave them in
silence** while you wait. Silence is what turns a question into a chargeback.

If the owner has not answered in 24 hours, chase once and tell the customer you are
still on it.

---

## 5. Your day

1. **Payment disputes first.** They have hard deadlines and losing one is final. The
   daily alarm lists them.
2. **Then the oldest unanswered email**, not the newest.
3. **Then the rest.**

The dispute handbook is a separate, deeper set of documents for chargebacks. The page
**Chargeback or dispute via bank or Klarna** tells you what to do in the first hour
and where to go next.

---

## 6. If a page is wrong

Tell the owner. These pages are written from real cases, and when a case shows one is
wrong it gets fixed for everyone rather than worked around by one person.
