# SOP: Customer disputes the charged amount

**Use this when** a customer says they were charged more than expected, charged
twice, or that a discount code did not apply.

**Owner approval:** required for every refund, full or partial. The VA establishes
the facts and keeps the customer informed.

---

## 1. Ask, then send a holding reply

Ask for three things in one message: the order number, the amount that was charged,
and the amount they expected.

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för att du hör av dig. Jag kollar upp ordern direkt. Kan du
> skicka ordernumret, beloppet som drogs och vad du väntade dig att betala? Jag
> återkommer så snart jag har gått igenom den.

*Meaning: I am checking the order now; send the order number, the amount charged and
the amount you expected.*

**Never confirm or deny that we charged wrong before you have looked.** A sentence
like "that does sound wrong" is quoted back to us in a dispute.

---

## 2. Read the order

| Check | Where |
|---|---|
| Order total, line by line | The order page |
| Quantity per item — two of something is the most common cause | The line items |
| Discount applied, and which code | The order's discount row |
| Shipping and any fees | The totals block |
| **The currency and market the order was placed in** | The order's presentment currency |

⚠️ **The currency is a real cause, not a footnote.** Our stores sell in several
currencies, and a customer who compares the figure on their statement with the price
they remember can be looking at two different currencies, or at their bank's
conversion. Read which market the order was placed in before you answer.

**Also check for a duplicate order** on the same email and day. Two orders look
exactly like one double charge to the customer.

---

## 3. Send the owner the facts

```
Disputed amount — [STORE]
Order: [ORDER NUMBER]      Charged: [AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]
Customer expected: [AMOUNT]
Items: [QTY × ITEM at PRICE]
Discount on the order: [CODE / none]
Market and currency: [...]
Duplicate order on the same email: yes [ID] / no
My reading: the total is correct / the total is wrong because [...]
```

Then wait for the decision. Chase once if it is quiet for 24 hours, and keep the
customer updated — silence is what turns this into a chargeback.

---

## 4. Reply templates

**The total is correct**

> Hej [NAMN]! Jag har gått igenom order [ORDERNUMMER] rad för rad. Du beställde
> [ANTAL × VARA] och summan blev [BELOPP] [VALUTA], inklusive [FRAKT/RABATT]. Det är
> beloppet som dragits, och jag ser ingen dubbeldragning. Säger din bank något annat
> vill jag gärna se en skärmbild, så kollar jag igen.

*Meaning: I went through the order line by line; here is what you ordered and what it
came to. No double charge. Send a screenshot if your bank says otherwise.*

**A code was missed, the owner approved goodwill**

> Hej [NAMN]! Du har rätt — rabattkoden gick inte igenom i kassan. Vi har betalat
> tillbaka mellanskillnaden, [BELOPP] [VALUTA], till kortet du betalade med. Tack för
> att du sa till.

**A duplicate charge, the owner approved the refund**

> Hej [NAMN]! Det blev två ordrar på samma köp, och det är vårt fel. Vi har betalat
> tillbaka [BELOPP] [VALUTA] till ditt kort och behåller den ena ordern. Ursäkta
> krånglet.

Never state how many days the money takes to appear. That depends on their bank.

---

## 5. Definition of done

- [ ] Order number, charged amount and expected amount collected.
- [ ] Order read line by line, including quantity, discount, shipping and currency.
- [ ] Checked for a duplicate order.
- [ ] Owner messaged with the block in §3 if anything looks wrong, chased after 24 h.
- [ ] Customer answered with the matching template, no promised dates.
- [ ] One line in the order notes with what was decided.
