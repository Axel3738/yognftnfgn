# SOP: Reseller and wholesale inquiries

**Use this when** a business or a private person asks to buy in bulk, resell our
products, or become a distributor.

**Owner approval:** required for anything beyond the holding answer below. **Never**
quote a wholesale price, a minimum order or terms.

---

## 1. Answer the same day — these are worth money

> Hej [NAMN]! Tack för att du hör av dig, kul att du är intresserad.
>
> Vi säljer i dag direkt till konsument och har ingen återförsäljarlösning på plats
> ännu. Men vi tittar på det, så jag vill gärna ha dina uppgifter så att vi kan höra
> av oss när det blir aktuellt: namn, företag, land och vad du skulle vilja
> sälja — ungefär vilka volymer tänker du dig?
>
> Jag skickar vidare det du svarar till min kollega som äger den frågan.

*Meaning: thanks for the interest; we sell direct to consumer today and have no
reseller setup yet, but we are looking at it; send name, company, country, which
products and roughly what volumes, and I will pass it on.*

Write in the customer's language (DeepL).

**Do not** say "we do not sell to businesses" as a flat no — it closes a lead that
might be worth more than a month of orders. And do not promise it will happen.

---

## 2. Pass it on, once

Send the owner: the whole email, the answers to the four questions, and the country.
One message, in the escalation channel (Store facts) — not three different pings on
three different apps.

Log the contact details where they can be found again. A lead that lives only in one
inbox is a lead that is lost.

---

## 3. If the owner wants to take over

Say so plainly to the customer and step back:

> Min kollega hör av sig direkt till dig om det här.

After that the owner owns the thread. Do not answer follow-ups about terms yourself.

---

## 4. Definition of done

- [ ] Answered the same day, positively, with the four questions.
- [ ] No price, no minimum order, no terms and no promise given.
- [ ] Owner sent the email and the answers in one message.
- [ ] Contact details logged somewhere findable.

⚠️ **OWNER: the current answer has no date on it.** "No reseller setup yet" was true
when this was written. Tell the VA when it changes, otherwise she keeps declining
leads you may want.
