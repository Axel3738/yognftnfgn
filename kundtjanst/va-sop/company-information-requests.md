# SOP: Customer asks for company information

**The PDF attached to this row is an older version.** Where it differs from this page,
the address in particular, follow this page.

**Use this when** a customer asks who we are: legal name, organisation number, VAT
status, address.

**Owner approval:** not needed for the values in the table below. Needed for anything
outside it.

---

## 1. What you may send, verbatim

The legal entity is **the same for every store in the group**. Only the customer-
facing address and website change per store — take those from **Store facts**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Legal name | Stonebite Ecom AB |
| Organisation number | 559576-2401 |
| VAT registered | Yes |
| Address | Stenkolsgatan 1B, 417 07 Göteborg, Sweden (since 26 Sep 2026; the old address in Harestad is no longer used) |
| Support address | The one for that store — **Store facts** |
| Website | The store the customer bought from — **Store facts** |

Nothing else. Not the owner's name, not ownership, not turnover, not bank details.

---

## 2. The reply

> Hej [NAMN]! Så klart — här är våra företagsuppgifter:
>
> Stonebite Ecom AB
> Org.nr: 559576-2401
> Momsregistrerat: ja
> Adress: Stenkolsgatan 1B, 417 07 Göteborg, Sverige
> Kontakt: [SUPPORTADRESS UR STORE FACTS]
>
> Hör av dig om du behöver något mer!

Write in the customer's language (DeepL). Send it the same day — this is a two-minute
reply and a slow one looks like we have something to hide.

---

## 3. What to escalate instead of answering

| They ask for | Do |
|---|---|
| A formal invoice with the org. number | "Another company wants an invoice" |
| The **VAT number** (the SE…01 format) | Ask the owner — we publish "VAT registered: yes", not the number itself |
| Registration certificate, ownership, who the CEO is | Ask the owner first, answer nothing |
| Anything for a legal or authority matter | Owner, same day, and say so in the channel |

---

## 4. Definition of done

- [ ] Only the fields in §1 were sent.
- [ ] Support address and website taken from Store facts for **that** store.
- [ ] Reply in the customer's language, same day.
- [ ] Anything outside the table escalated instead of guessed.

⚠️ **OWNER: the VAT number.** Customers do ask for it by name. Should the VA give it
out? Until you answer, they say "VAT registered: yes" and escalate.
