# Following up the auto-reply — as Head of Customer Support

**Category: Other stuff** · Internal use only · Works for every store: the per-store
values are on **Store facts**.

## Use this when

- A card under **"The AI bot has replied"** on the Kundtjänst dashboard.
- A thread in **VA-PRIO** that already has a reply from "Kundtjänst [store]" that
  nobody on the team wrote.
- The replied arrow on an email you have not touched.

If none of these apply, the bot did not answer: use the ordinary SOP for the
question (Start here has the table). What the bot is and how it sorts emails is
on the page **Auto-reply bot — what it does, and what you do**. This page starts
where that one ends: the customer already has one reply from the store, and you
write the second.

## The idea

The bot's reply is the first line: fast, factual, signed "Kundtjänst [store]".
**Your reply is the second line, and it comes from the Head of Customer Support
who has just had the case escalated to her personally.** The customer should feel:
"the first answer came within a minute, and now the person in charge has taken my
case over herself." So every follow-up opens as if the case was forwarded to you
by name, and then you handle the actual problem exactly as the ordinary SOP for
that problem says. The persona changes the opening and the ownership. It never
changes the facts, and it never changes who approves what.

## The rules

1. **Read the bot's email in Sent before you write.** Build on it. Never
   contradict a fact it gave, and never repeat one either. The dashboard card
   says in one line what the bot wrote; the email is the truth.
2. **Never mention a bot, an AI, an automatic reply or "our system".** Both
   replies are from the store.
3. **Never ask again for what the customer already sent.** Photos, the order
   number, a description. If the bot asked for photos and none have come, say
   what happens the moment they arrive. One reminder after two days, never a
   third.
4. **Keep the bot's promise.** An upset customer was told: *"Du kan räkna med svar
   inom 48 timmar."* The clock starts at the bot's email, not at the customer's,
   and the dashboard card counts it down. Aim for the same day.
5. **Owner approval stays where the other SOPs put it:** a refund, an accepted
   return, a replacement, "the parcel is lost". What you can promise on your own
   is what you have done today and when you will write next.
6. **The customer's language.** Swedish is the master text here; DeepL for the
   other markets.
7. **Sign as the person in charge**: your first name and the Swedish title
   *Kundtjänstansvarig* plus the store name from Store facts. Two replies, two
   people, one store.
8. **If the bot was wrong, put it right first, then report it** (section
   "The bot was wrong").

## The opener and the sign-off

Every follow-up starts with the opener and ends with the sign-off. Between them
comes the case, from the SOP for that problem.

**The opener**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| Hej [FIRST NAME]! Det här ärendet skickades precis vidare till mig personligen. Jag är kundtjänstansvarig här, och jag har läst både ditt mejl och din order [ORDER NUMBER]. | Hi [FIRST NAME]! This case was just escalated to me personally. I am the Head of Customer Support here, and I have read both your email and your order [ORDER NUMBER]. |

No order number on the case yet: end the second sentence after "ditt mejl".

**The sign-off** (three lines, copy as they stand)

```
Vänliga hälsningar
[YOUR FIRST NAME]
Kundtjänstansvarig, [STORE NAME from Store facts]
```

English meaning: Kind regards / [your first name] / Head of Customer Support, [store name].

## Outcome 1: Upset customer (the calming reply)

**What the bot sent.** The customer's first name, then *"Jag förstår helt din
frustration."*, one sentence naming the problem (a wrong item, a broken item, a
parcel that has not arrived, waiting too long, poor quality, "not as pictured"),
and the promise: *"Jag har eskalerat det här direkt till vårt ansvariga team som
ett brådskande ärende. Du kan räkna med svar inom 48 timmar."* Depending on the
case it also: told the customer where the parcel is right now (from the tracking),
said the order has sat unshipped for N days, asked for photos, sent the return
instructions, or asked for the order number. The email was flagged and moved to
VA-PRIO. The dashboard card lists exactly which of these it did.

**Your job.** Reply within 48 hours of the bot's email, as the person the case was
escalated to. First open the order and the thread and decide what the problem
actually is; then follow that problem's SOP (Product arrived broken, Wrong product
delivered, Package missing after tracking shows delivered, Order not arrived within
expected timeframe, Customer wants to return product, the dispute pages). Ask the
owner for approval where that SOP says so, and do it before you reply if you can
reach him the same day. If you cannot, reply anyway with what you have done and
when you will write next: silence is the one thing the customer was promised not
to get.

**Deadline.** The card says "Reply promised by" and counts down from the bot's
email. Yellow means under 24 hours, red under 12 hours or passed.

**Templates.** Pick the one that matches what the bot already did.

**1A: the photos have arrived (damaged, wrong or not as pictured), the owner has decided**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| [OPENER] Tack för bilderna, de säger allt. Att du fick [WHAT WENT WRONG, e.g. en vara som inte stämmer med det du beställde] är inte okej, och jag vill rätta till det ordentligt. [THE OWNER'S DECISION, e.g. Vi skickar en ny [PRODUCT] till dig utan kostnad. Den går iväg från vårt lager, och du får ett mejl med spårningslänk så fort den är skickad.] Du behöver inte göra något mer just nu. Hör av dig direkt om något är oklart, så tar jag det på en gång. [SIGN-OFF] | [OPENER] Thank you for the photos, they say it all. Getting [what went wrong] is not okay, and I want to put it right properly. [The owner's decision, e.g. We are sending you a new [product] at no cost. It ships from our warehouse, and you get an email with a tracking link the moment it is on its way.] You do not need to do anything else right now. Write to me straight away if anything is unclear and I will deal with it at once. [SIGN-OFF] |

**1B: the bot asked for photos and none have come yet**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| [OPENER] Jag har lagt ditt ärende överst i min kö. Så fort dina bilder kommer in tittar jag på dem samma dag och återkommer med besked. [SIGN-OFF] |

**1C: the parcel has not arrived or has been waiting too long (the bot already gave the tracking status)**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| [OPENER] Jag ser samma sak som du: [THE STATUS IN ONE SENTENCE, e.g. paketet har inte rört sig sedan den 18 september]. Det är för länge, och jag har i dag [WHAT YOU DID, e.g. startat en efterforskning hos fraktbolaget / bett lagret prioritera din order]. I will come back to you by [DATE] at the latest, whether I have heard from them or not. [SIGN-OFF] |

Do not repeat the tracking lines the bot sent. Do not quote the delivery window
(Store facts) in an email that carries the tracking link.

**1D: the customer wants their money back or wants to return the item**

If the bot already sent the return instructions (the card says so), they stand:

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| [OPENER] Returinstruktionerna du fick i vårt förra mejl gäller. Så fort paketet är hos oss kontrollerar vi det och jag återkommer till dig samma dag. Svara gärna på det här mejlet med spårningsnumret när du postat det, så följer jag paketet åt dig. [SIGN-OFF] | [OPENER] The return instructions you got in our previous email stand. As soon as the parcel is with us we check it, and I come back to you the same day. Please reply to this email with the tracking number once you have posted it, and I will follow the parcel for you. [SIGN-OFF] |

If the bot did not send them: **Customer wants to return product** decides
whether the return is inside the window and what the owner needs to approve, then
**Return address and return instructions** gives you the text. The refund itself
follows **Refund not received after approved return** and the three-step page.

**Extra lines the bot may have added, and what they mean for you**

| The card says | What you do |
|---|---|
| Told the customer where the parcel is | Do not repeat it. Say what you did about it (1C). |
| Said the order has sat unshipped for N days | The warehouse is late: ask the owner what to tell the customer and use 1C with "bett lagret prioritera". |
| Asked for photos | 1A if they came, 1B if not. |
| Sent the return instructions | 1D, first template. |
| Asked for the order number | Look the customer up by email in Shopify first; you often find the order without asking. If you truly cannot, the opener without the order number, then one question. |

**Escalation.** Threatens the bank, Klarna or a chargeback: the page **Chargeback or
dispute via bank or Klarna** the same hour, and tell the owner the same day. Your
reply to the customer still follows this page.

## Outcome 2: Simple, photos requested (calm customer, damaged, faulty or wrong item)

**What the bot sent.** *"Tråkigt att höra att leveransen inte blev som den skulle.
Det tittar vi på direkt."* (or *"…att varan inte fungerar som den ska"*, or *"…att
den inte passar som den ska"*), then the photo request: the item, the packaging
and the shipping label; or a photo or short video of the fault; or a photo of the
item in place plus measurements or model. Flagged and moved to VA-PRIO.

**Your job.** Check whether the photos have arrived. They have: this is now a
damaged, faulty or wrong item case, handled per **Product arrived broken** or
**Wrong product delivered**, with the owner's approval before you promise a
replacement, refund or return. They have not after two days: one reminder, never a
third. Deadline: the same business day the photos arrive.

**2A: the photos have arrived, the owner has decided**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| [OPENER] Tack för bilderna. Jag ser [WHAT THE PHOTOS SHOW, e.g. att sömmen släppt på ena sidan], och så ska det inte se ut. [THE OWNER'S DECISION]. Hör av dig om du undrar något, annars hör du från mig när [NEXT STEP, e.g. den nya varan är skickad]. [SIGN-OFF] | [OPENER] Thank you for the photos. I can see [what the photos show], and it should not look like that. [The owner's decision]. Write to me if you have any questions; otherwise you will hear from me when [next step]. [SIGN-OFF] |

**2B: the single reminder, two days without photos**

## Outcome 3: Simple, return instructions sent

**What the bot sent.** *"Så här gör du returen:"* followed by the four steps:
original packaging, name and order number on the parcel, the return address,
straight to the address and not to a pickup point, the customer pays the postage,
reply with the tracking number. Flagged and moved to VA-PRIO. (The owner decided
that a customer who asks to return gets the instructions at once.)

**Your job.** Do not send the instructions again. Wait for the customer's tracking
number and note it on the order in Shopify. When the parcel has arrived (the owner
tells you, or the customer's tracking shows delivered), the owner inspects it and
approves the refund; then **Refund not received after approved return** and the
three-step page. If the customer writes again with a question, answer it in the
Head of Customer Support voice.

**3A: the customer sent the tracking number**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| Hej [FIRST NAME]! Tack, jag har noterat ditt spårningsnummer på ordern. När paketet kommit fram till oss kontrollerar vi det och jag återkommer till dig samma dag. Du behöver inte göra något mer. [SIGN-OFF] | Hi [FIRST NAME]! Thank you, I have noted your tracking number on the order. When the parcel reaches us we check it and I come back to you the same day. You do not need to do anything else. [SIGN-OFF] |

## Outcome 4: Simple, marked delivered but not received

**What the bot sent.** *"Enligt fraktbolaget levererades paketet [DATE]"* plus the
checklist: the mailbox, a notice about a pickup point, the nearest pickup point,
neighbours, a sheltered spot by the door, and *"Hittar du det ändå inte: svara på
det här mejlet så undersöker vi vidare direkt."* Flagged and moved to VA-PRIO.

**Your job.** Nothing until the customer replies. If they reply that the parcel is
still missing: **Package missing after tracking shows delivered**, from step 4
(the carrier investigation), and you write as the person the case was escalated
to. Never say the parcel is lost before the carrier confirms it and the owner has
approved what to offer.

**4A: still missing, investigation started**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| [OPENER] Att paketet är markerat som levererat utan att du har fått det tar jag på allvar. Jag har i dag startat en efterforskning hos fraktbolaget som gjorde leveransen, och jag återkommer till dig så fort jag hört från dem, senast [DATE]. Om du hinner: en bild på platsen där paket brukar lämnas hos dig hjälper oss i utredningen. [SIGN-OFF] | [OPENER] A parcel marked as delivered that you have not received is something I take seriously. Today I opened an investigation with the carrier that made the delivery, and I will come back to you as soon as I hear from them, by [DATE] at the latest. If you have a moment: a photo of the spot where parcels are usually left at your place helps the investigation. [SIGN-OFF] |

## Outcome 5: Simple, address change confirmed

**What the bot sent.** *"Tack, jag har skickat din nya adress vidare till vårt
lager med prioritet. Din order [ORDER NUMBER] har inte skickats än."* plus "if
they manage to change it before the parcel leaves, it goes to the new address;
write to us at once if the shipping confirmation shows the wrong one". Flagged and
moved to VA-PRIO. The bot only says this when the order has **not** shipped; an
address change on a shipped order is left to you with no reply.

**Your job.** The bot promised the warehouse would hear about it. **You are the
one who tells the warehouse**: change the address the same day, per **Order
placed with incorrect delivery address**, before the order ships. Then confirm in
one line. If the change is no longer possible, that SOP says what to tell the
customer and what the owner has to approve. Deadline: today, the order is being
packed.

## Outcome 6: Simple, fully answered

**What the bot sent.** One of: where the parcel is (latest scan, tracking link,
parcel number), the delivery time and packing time, our reply time with "please
write the order number in the email", the company details, or "we need your order
number". Not moved to VA-PRIO. Only the "we need your order number" reply is
flagged, so the thread is in your flagged list; nothing to do there until the
customer replies with the number.

**Your job.** Nothing, unless the customer writes again. When they do, the bot
stays silent (it never writes twice in a thread) and the reply is yours:

- A plain question (a new question, a thank-you, "which pickup point?"): a short
  normal reply. No opener; you are not escalating a question about opening hours.
- The customer sent the order number the bot asked for: look the order up and
  answer the original question, per that question's SOP.
- The reply turns the case into a problem (the parcel is still not there, the item
  is wrong, they want to return): now this page applies. Opener, then the SOP for
  the problem.

Never restart the thread with facts the bot already gave.

**6A: the customer wrote again with a plain question**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| Hej igen [FIRST NAME]! [THE ANSWER IN ONE OR TWO SENTENCES]. Hör av dig om det är något mer. [SIGN-OFF] | Hi again [FIRST NAME]! [The answer in one or two sentences]. Let me know if there is anything else. [SIGN-OFF] |

## Outcome 7: Flagged only (the "For you" bucket)

The bot sent nothing: returns and refunds it could not verify, exchanges and sizes,
cancellations, disputes, invoices, resellers, attachments, anything it could not
check. The customer has had **no reply at all**. This page does not apply: write
the first reply per the ordinary SOP, fast, and sign as usual.

## The bot was wrong

Wrong facts, wrong tone, wrong language, a customer you had already answered, or a
promise it should not have made. Put it right with the customer first, in the same
voice, then report it. Do not wait for the owner to fix the text with the customer.

**Template**

| Swedish (use this) | English meaning |
|---|---|
| Hej [FIRST NAME]! Jag är kundtjänstansvarig här och har tagit över ditt ärende. Det första svaret du fick var inte rätt, och det ber jag om ursäkt för. Det som gäller är: [THE CORRECT FACTS IN ONE OR TWO SENTENCES]. [NEXT STEP AND WHEN YOU WRITE NEXT]. [SIGN-OFF] | Hi [FIRST NAME]! I am the Head of Customer Support here and have taken over your case. The first reply you received was not right, and I apologise for that. What applies is: [the correct facts in one or two sentences]. [Next step and when you write next]. [SIGN-OFF] |

Then, in the escalation channel (Store facts), one line:

```
Auto-reply wrong: order [ORDER NUMBER] — [what was wrong in one sentence]
```

Every report fixes the rule for every store.

## The dashboard card

On the Kundtjänst page, block **"The AI bot has replied"**. One card per email the
bot answered in the last 30 days.

- **The badge** says who the customer is: *ANGRY CUSTOMER · AI bot replied* (red)
  or *AI bot replied*. The order number is the big text; the subject line is under
  it.
- **What the bot wrote**: the reply in one line. **Your next step**: this page in
  one line.
- **Reply promised by** with the countdown: only on angry customers. Yellow under
  24 hours, red under 12 hours or when the promise has passed.
- **To follow up** holds the cards the bot promised something on or handed to you,
  angry customers on top, oldest first. **Fully answered by the bot** holds the
  rest; nothing to do there unless the customer writes again.
- **Mark as followed up** when the customer has your reply, not before. The card
  moves to **Followed up (archive)** with your name and the time. **Undo** brings
  it back. It also leaves the owner's "Needs you today" list.
- **Dry-run drafts** is history from before the bot went live: those customers
  got nothing from the bot, and you answered them yourself.

Marking a card does nothing in the mailbox: the flag and the VA-PRIO folder are
still yours to clear when the customer has been answered.

## Readability test, before every send

The owner's recurring complaint is text that does not sound like a person wrote it.
Before you send, read the email aloud once and check:

1. Every sentence flows into the next. No jumps.
2. No dash characters anywhere in the email. Write "1 till 2 arbetsdagar", not a
   range with a dash.
3. No two sentences begin the same way.
4. Exactly one promise, and it has a date or "samma dag". It is a promise you can
   keep without the owner.
5. Nothing in it repeats the bot's email, and nothing contradicts it.
6. The customer could tell one thing from the two replies: the second one is the
   person in charge.

---

## Definition of done

- [ ] You read the bot's email in Sent before writing.
- [ ] The reply opens with the opener (or the reminder/wrong-reply opener) and
      ends with your name and *Kundtjänstansvarig*.
- [ ] It builds on the bot's reply: no repeated facts, no contradictions, no
      second request for what the customer already sent.
- [ ] The actual problem was handled per its own SOP, with the owner's approval
      where that SOP requires it.
- [ ] An angry customer had your reply within 48 hours of the bot's email.
- [ ] The readability test passed.
- [ ] The card is marked as followed up, and the flag or VA-PRIO folder is
      cleared once the customer has been answered.
- [ ] A wrong bot reply was put right with the customer and reported with the
      order number.
