# SOP: Questions about discounts and campaigns

**Use this when** a customer asks whether there is a discount code, a campaign, or a
better price.

**Owner approval:** required before any code or discount is given. **Never invent a
code**, and never hint that one might be coming.

---

## 1. Check the list before you ask the owner

Active codes live in the campaign list (the owner keeps it; ask where it is if you
have not been shown). Look there first — asking the owner about every single email is
what makes the owner stop answering.

| What you find | What you do |
|---|---|
| An active code for this store | Send it with its terms: what it gives, what it applies to, when it expires |
| Nothing active | Answer with the template in §2. No apology, no hinting |
| A code the customer found somewhere that does not work | Check whether it expired or is for another store. Say which — do not just say "invalid" |

---

## 2. When there is no campaign

> Hej [NAMN]! Just nu har vi ingen aktiv kampanj eller rabattkod. Priserna du ser i
> butiken är de som gäller. Vi lägger ut erbjudanden på sajten och i sociala medier
> när de kommer, så det är där du ser dem först.

*Meaning: no active campaign or code right now; the prices in the shop are the
current ones; offers go up on the site and social media when they come.*

Write in the customer's language (DeepL). Keep it short. A long apology reads as if a
discount is negotiable.

---

## 3. Bulk, loyalty and "I always buy here"

Do not decide these yourself and do not say no on the owner's behalf either.

> Tack för att du frågar — jag tar upp det med min kollega som bestämmer om
> erbjudanden och återkommer.

Then send the owner: what they asked for, order history if they have one, and the
amount involved. Come back to the customer with the answer either way, even if it is
no.

---

## 4. An angry customer who demands a discount

Do not buy peace with a code. Answer the actual complaint using the SOP that fits it
— a late parcel, a damaged item, a wrong item all have their own page, and those
have real remedies. A discount handed to an angry customer teaches them to be angry.

---

## 5. Definition of done

- [ ] Campaign list checked before the owner was asked.
- [ ] Active code sent **with its terms**, or the no-campaign template sent.
- [ ] No code invented, no future campaign hinted at.
- [ ] Bulk and loyalty requests forwarded to the owner **and** answered back to the
      customer.
- [ ] Angry customers answered with the SOP for their real problem, not with a
      discount.
