# Ad comments — the morning report, and what you do

**Use this when** a report appears in the Discord channel **#ad-comments**, or you
are pinged there.

Every morning a routine reads every new comment on every ad that is running, on
Facebook and Instagram, for every store. It posts **one report per store** in that
store's Discord server, in **#ad-comments**. It only reads. It never replies to a
comment, never hides one and never deletes one — you do that, from the page.

---

## 1. The four parts of the report

| Part | What it is | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Needs a human today** | Order not received, an unhappy buyer, a scam accusation, a threat to report us, a safety issue, spam | Handle every line today (section 2). You are pinged when one of them is a customer. |
| 🔵 **Buying questions with no reply in the thread** | Price, size, "does it fit my …", payment — and nobody has replied yet | Reply from the page. When a suggested reply is included, paste the text in the backticks exactly as written (section 3). |
| 🟡 **Objections** | Reasons people give for not buying ("moisture", "cheaper on Temu", "will it blow off") | Nothing. They go to the creative team for the next ads. |
| 🟢 **Leads** | Customer words that should become an ad | Nothing. Same as above. |

Each line has the ad name and a link. The link opens the comment on Facebook or
Instagram (**IG** in the line means Instagram).

"No reply in the thread" means nobody at all has replied yet — neither a customer nor
the page. The report cannot see who wrote a reply, so if someone else already
answered correctly, you do not need to answer again.

---

## 2. 🔴 Needs a human today

| The line says | What you do |
|---|---|
| **order not received** | Reply in the thread: we are sorry, please email our support address with your order number so we can check it right away. Then follow **Order not arrived within expected timeframe** when the email comes. |
| **unhappy buyer** | Same reply: ask them to email support with their order number and a photo. Then follow the page for their problem (**Product arrived broken**, **Wrong product delivered**, **Customer wants to return product**). |
| **scam accusation** | If the line says it is a customer: as above. If it is someone who never bought (the report says so): do nothing. Never argue in a comment. |
| **threat to report us** | Reply as above, and write in **#customer-service** so the owner sees it the same day. |
| **safety** | Do not reply. Write in **#customer-service** with the link the same day. |
| **spam / link** | Hide the comment from the page (… → Hide comment). Never delete. |

**Never in a comment:** a promise of a refund, a replacement, a delivery date or a
discount, the carrier's tracking number, or anything you have not checked. The
support address is on **Store facts**.

---

## 3. Suggested replies

We are training the reply drafts right now. The owner approves every draft
before it is used, so the report only says how many drafts are waiting. Do not
reply to those comments yourself until you are told a reply is approved.
Buying questions without a draft: answer only if the answer is on the product
page; otherwise leave it for the next report.

---

## 4. What you never do

- Never delete a comment. Hiding spam is the only moderation.
- Never pause, change or report an ad because of its comments. Ads are the owner's.
- Never reply to the same person twice in the same thread in one day.
